## Summary

Bosta keeps the cash it collects at the door, credits it to the brand's Bosta wallet minus its fees, and later cashes the wallet out to the bank. This feature proves that money line by line. Every delivery's cash cycle (Bosta's own record of what it collected, what it charged, and when it credited the rest) arrives from the Bosta mirror and becomes a small statement of settlement lines; Bosta's downloadable wallet sheet is imported through a column mapping the brand saves once, with a preview before anything is written, and adds the wallet's own rows (compensation, cash outs, packing, insurance) without ever counting a delivery's cash cycle twice. Each line is matched to its order by tracking number, or accounted to an account code when it has no order, and an unmatched line always says why; fees are also checked against the brand's Bosta rate card, so a fee above the contract stands out. When an order's lines are matched its cash moves to remitted, which here means credited in Bosta's wallet, and once everything agrees, to reconciled; the wallet is rolled forward from balance to balance and each cash out is checked off against the bank, without touching the order's states. Period totals compare what Bosta says with what the app holds, show what Bosta still owes at the period's end, export as CSV for the accountant, and stay labelled provisional until feature 23 closes the period.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As the accountant, I want Bosta's money for every delivery to arrive by itself, matched to its order, so that I only look at the lines that do not match and each one tells me why and what to do next.
- As the accountant, I want to import Bosta's wallet sheet with a mapping I set once and a preview I check, so that cash outs, compensations, and anything the mirror missed are in the same place, and nothing counts twice.
- As the owner, I want to know how much Bosta owes me at the end of a month (cash not yet credited, compensation not yet paid, the wallet not yet cashed out, cash outs not yet in my bank), so that no money sits with the carrier unseen.
- As the owner, I want fees above my Bosta contract flagged, so that a rate change or a fee I never asked for does not quietly eat my margin.
- As the accountant, I want a month's totals beside the app's own figures, split as I post them, and a CSV of the lines and the summary, so that I can book the month without copying screens.
- As operations, I want a lost parcel's approved claim to be closed by the compensation Bosta pays, and an approved claim that is never paid to stand out, so that no claim is left waiting.
- As the vendor, I want the slice proven on one real month of the pilot brand's settlement, so that the design rests on Bosta's real data.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract; each is checked in [verify.md](verify.md)):

*Accounts and the per delivery cash cycles (the lead source)*
- **AC-1** (one account per Bosta connection): the settlement module keeps exactly one live carrier settlement account per Bosta connection (`owner_type` `carrier`, `owner_connection_id` the connection), created by the system the first time a cash cycle or a file needs it (`SettlementAccounts.ensureForConnection`, under the advisory lock `settlement_account:<connection id>`; the built partial unique index refuses a second), with `code` `BOSTA-` plus the first 8 characters of the connection id in capitals, `name` the connection's label in both languages, and its opening cutoff (AC-35). `/settlement` lists the accounts with their balance, unmatched count, last import, and the word provisional.
- **AC-2** (a cash cycle becomes a statement): on `CarrierCashCycleRecorded` the module enqueues `settlement-carrier-import` for that delivery in the publisher's transaction (singleton key `<delivery id>:<revision>`); the job reads the delivery back through `CarrierDeliveries.byTrackingNumbers` (never the event payload) and, only when the cash cycle has a deposit time, writes one `carrier_api` statement for that delivery and revision: `external_reference` `bosta-cc:<cashCycleId>:<trackingNumber>:r<revision>`, `covers_from` and `covers_to` and `deposited_on` the deposit day, `deposited_amount` and `statement_total` Bosta's `deposited_amt`, and the lines of *Lines from a cash cycle*. Each line's `occurred_on` is the deposit day in the install time zone (`timezone` setting), `external_line_ref` `<trackingNumber>:<line_kind>` (the fee name in `fee_code` on `other_fee` lines), `tracking_number` and `business_reference` from the delivery, and `store_id` from AC-15. A zero amount writes no line; a cash cycle with no deposit time writes nothing and waits (spec 0002 settlement child, `occurred_on`). Replaying the job writes nothing new (the statement's unique key).
- **AC-3** (revisions supersede): a later revision of the same delivery's cash cycle (a changed amount or a changed cash cycle id, spec 0018 AC-10) writes a new statement with `supersedes_statement_id` the delivery's live statement on this account, and in the same transaction a `cleared` row with the reason `superseded` on every line of the old statement (accounted lines included, domain review D1), then matches the new lines (AC-10). An event with a revision at or below the live one changes nothing. When the delivery's live cash cycle lines came from a sheet, AC-36 applies.
- **AC-4** (the sweep, spec 0007 M1): a nightly `settlement-carrier-sweep` (03:30 in the install time zone) and a first run when an account is created walk `CarrierDeliveries.cashCycles({ connectionId, depositedFrom, depositedTo, cursor, limit: 500 })` from the later of the account's opening cutoff and 60 days back (first run) or the last 14 days (nightly), and import every deposited cash cycle whose revision the account does not hold yet, so a lost event never leaves a cash cycle out. A run stops after 10 minutes and the next one resumes from its cursor.
- **AC-5** (Bosta's arithmetic, review B2): after the derivation table has chosen each line, including the refund sign branch, the signed sum of the delivery's derived lines must equal Bosta's `deposited_amt`; for a send and a return to origin, Bosta's own `deposited_amt = cod − bosta_fees` is checked as well. When either differs by 0.01 or more, the statement and its lines are still written as derived, and every line of it gets `unmatched` with `cash_cycle_arithmetic_differs`, listed for a person. Before milestone 1, `cash-cycle-lines.ts` is run over an anonymised copy of the reference brand's 5,622 cash cycle rows and the number of `bosta_unitemized` residue lines and arithmetic failures is recorded in [verify.md](verify.md) (review m1).

*Bosta's sheet (fallback and evidence)*
- **AC-6** (the column mapping, owner decision Q-22.6): a holder of `settlement.work` saves a mapping per account on `POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/file-mappings`: a name, the layout (`per_delivery`: one row per delivery with amount columns; or `per_transaction`: one row per wallet transaction with a type column and one amount column), the header row number (1 to 20), the role of each used column by its header text (tracking number, business reference, deposit date, cash cycle id, transaction id, type, amount, COD, shipping fee, opening package fee, FlexShip fee, other fee (with its `fee_code`), VAT, total fees, deposited amount, balance, description), for `per_transaction` the map from each type value to a line kind or to `evidence_only` or `ignore`, and for every type value mapped to a line kind its family, `cash_cycle_part` or `wallet_only` (review B1; the starting map in *Lines from a sheet*; a type value with no family is `TYPE_UNMAPPED`, so a new Bosta type never falls into the wrong rule), the date format (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `DD/MM/YYYY`, `MM/DD/YYYY`, or a spreadsheet date number), and the decimal separator. Header texts may be Arabic or English and are matched after trimming and folding case. Editing saves a new version that supersedes the old one; a mapping is never changed in place. Columns the mapping does not name are never read into the app.
- **AC-7** (the preview): `POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/file-preview` takes one `.xlsx` or `.csv` file of at most 5 MB and 20,000 rows and a mapping id, writes nothing, and answers: the file's SHA-256, its row count, and per row one class: `new` (lines it would write, with kinds and amounts), `confirmed` (the account already holds the live cash cycle lines of this delivery, or this wallet transaction, and the amounts agree), `differs` (it holds them and an amount differs, with both amounts), `evidence_only` (a daily total, with the sheet's figure and the per delivery sum of that day), or `rejected` with a reason (`TRACKING_MISSING`, `AMOUNT_UNREADABLE`, `DATE_UNREADABLE`, `TYPE_UNMAPPED`, `ROW_EMPTY`); plus the totals by line kind and class, the days the file covers, and the match forecast for the `new` lines. It never answers a value from a column the mapping does not name.
- **AC-8** (apply): `POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/file-imports` with the same file, the mapping id, and `expectedSha256` (409 `FILE_CHANGED` when it differs) stores the file in object storage (`settlement/<account id>/<sha256>.<ext>`), then in one transaction writes one `carrier_file` statement (`external_reference` the file name, `sha256`, `object_key`, `covers_from` and `covers_to` the first and last row date, `statement_total` the sum of the file's amounts over every `new`, `confirmed`, and `differs` row, `imported_by` the person), the `new` rows' lines (each with `occurred_on` the row's date), the day evidence of AC-33, and any balance the sheet shows as a wallet balance observation (AC-31), runs the rules (AC-10), and writes the audit entry `settlement.file_imported` (mapping id and version, row counts by class). The same file again on the same account answers 409 `FILE_ALREADY_IMPORTED`. A corrected file is imported with `supersedesStatementId` and a reason code: its statement supersedes the old one and the old lines are cleared with `superseded`, as AC-3.

*Two sources, one count (spec 0002 domain review M8)*
- **AC-9** (no delivery counted twice, review B1): the line kinds a cash cycle produces (`cod_collected`, `shipping_fee`, `opening_package_fee`, `fee_vat`, `cod_fee`, `customer_refund`, and `other_fee` with `flex_ship`, `flex_ship_refusal`, `bosta_unitemized`, or `bosta_fees_total`) of one delivery live on one statement at a time within an account. A cash cycle, or a sheet row of the `cash_cycle_part` family, for a tracking number whose cash cycle kind lines are already live (on a statement nobody superseded, with heads not `cleared`) on another statement is never written: it is compared. Equal amounts by kind (or equal net when one side has no breakdown) count as `confirmed`; a difference makes those live lines `unmatched` with `sources_disagree`, opens the task `settlement.sources_disagree` linked to the newer statement, publishes `CarrierSettlementSourcesDisagree`, and lists both amounts. This holds in both directions and between two overlapping sheets, except for a mirror revision (AC-36). Rows of the `wallet_only` family (`compensation`, `adjustment`, `deposit`, `other_fee` `insurance`, `other_fee` `packing_material`, and any type a mapping marks `wallet_only`) are always added beside the delivery's cash cycle lines, with or without a tracking number, and are deduplicated only against live lines on other statements by content (spec 0002 M8): the same transaction id, or, when the mapping has no transaction id column, the same kind, fee name, tracking number, day, and amount. When a sheet's lines settle a delivery the mirror has no deposited cash cycle for, the module publishes `CarrierCashSettledByFile` (connection, delivery id when known, tracking number, deposit day), so the connector can stop calling that delivery overdue (requested from spec 0018).

*Matching*
- **AC-10** (the rules, in order): after every import, after every relevant event (`CarrierDeliveryLinked`, `OrderStateChanged` on cash, `CarrierClaimDecided`, a refund record change, a rate card change), and nightly, the matching job (capability `settlement.carrier_reconciliation`) takes each line whose head is not `matched`, `accounted`, or `cleared`, oldest `occurred_on` first, in batches of 500, and applies the first rule that holds, writing one `settlement_line_matches` row with `matched_by_type` `rule` and its `rule_code`, or an `unmatched` row with the reason when the reason changed (an unchanged reason writes nothing). The rules and reasons are the *Matching rules* table. A line's state is always `lineMatchState` over its rows.
- **AC-11** (tolerance, owner decision Q-22.3): amounts are compared exactly after both sides are parsed to 2 decimal places, half away from zero; any difference of 0.01 or more is a difference. A person may still match a line that differs, with a reason code.
- **AC-12** (a person's actions): a holder of `settlement.work` matches a line to an order (`POST /api/settlement/lines/:id/match`, with a reason code), accounts it to an account code (`/account`, with an optional payment reference of at most 500 characters), unmatches it with a reason code (`/unmatch`), or picks the claim for a compensation (`/claim`), each with `expectedHeadId` (the line's current head, null for none; 409 `MATCH_STALE` when it moved). A line whose head is `accounted` or `cleared` answers 409 `LINE_FINAL`. Each action writes one match row with `matched_by_type` `person` and `matched_by` the person, and the audit entry `settlement.line_matched_by_person`, `settlement.line_accounted`, `settlement.line_unmatched_by_person`, or `settlement.compensation_claim_picked`.
- **AC-13** (refunds, the lane C agreement, owner decision Q-22.8): this feature matches only Bosta's refund lines: a `customer_refund` line (the cash a courier handed a customer on a customer return pickup, see *Lines from a cash cycle*) is matched to its order and to the refund record with method `carrier_cash` on that order with the same amount (`refund_id` on the match row), and confirms that refund through the credit module. The refund record is the minimal one spec 0016 proposes for the credit module in slice 1a (its owner question 2, a thin slice of feature 35), extended with the method `carrier_cash`; Q-22.8 is that same decision, not a second one. With no such record the line is `unmatched` with `refund_record_missing` and the task `settlement.refund_unmatched` opens; it matches on the refund record's creation. A refund by InstaPay, wallet, or bank transfer never touches a carrier settlement.
- **AC-14** (compensation to a claim, audit item 12, spec 0019 AC-22): a `compensation` line is matched to a claim from `CarrierClaims.awaitingCompensation({ connectionId: account.owner_connection_id })` on these fields, in this order: the connection (never across accounts); the tracking number when the line carries one (then the claim with that tracking number is the match, whatever the amount, and spec 0019 records `received_differs` when the amounts differ); otherwise `approved_amount` equal to the line's amount, `decided_at` on or before the line's `occurred_on`, and, when more than one claim is left, the one whose `carrier_complaint_ref` appears in the line's text (`partner_text`); and the claim's `compensation_state` `awaiting` (the read answers only those). Exactly one claim gives a `matched` row on the claim's order, rule `claim_by_tracking` or `claim_by_amount`, and `CarrierClaims.recordCompensation(tx, claimId, { settlementLineId, amount, depositedOn: occurred_on }, actor)` in the same unit. None gives `unmatched` `no_claim_for_compensation`; several give `claims_ambiguous`; both open `settlement.compensation_unmatched`. When a line has no tracking number, matches no claim by amount, and exactly one awaiting claim exists on the connection, the line drawer offers that claim as the single candidate whatever the amount (review M5); a person confirms it (never a rule), and spec 0019 records `received_differs`. A person may also account the line as `carrier_compensation`.
- **AC-15** (the store on every line, spec 0007 B1): a carrier line's `store_id` is the account's store binding when the connection has one, otherwise the store of the delivery its tracking number names, read through `CarrierDeliveries.byTrackingNumbers` (spec 0018 Q-19.1), never an orders table. A line that needs an order and has no store is `unmatched` with `store_unknown` and is tried again on `CarrierDeliveryLinked`. A wallet line with no tracking number on an unbound account keeps `store_id` null.

*The order's cash (spec 0002 AC-20, spec 0013 AC-17)*
- **AC-16** (remitted means credited in Bosta's wallet, owner decisions Q-22.2 and Q-22.10): when an order whose cash state is `collected_by_carrier` has every live `cod_collected` line of its forward delivery `matched`, and their statements carry a `deposited_on`, the module calls `OrderTransitions.recordSettlement(tx, orderId, { to: 'remitted', remittedAmount: <sum of those lines>, depositedOn: <latest deposit day>, sourceId: <the last line's match id> }, systemJob)` inside the matching unit (`OrderTransactions.run`). `remitted` here means Bosta has credited the cash to the brand's Bosta wallet, not that it is in the bank: every screen and report of this feature labels the state "credited by Bosta" in both catalogs (the code stays `remitted`; no change to spec 0002's cash enum), and the bank side is the cash out state of AC-32, which never touches the order's machine. An order still `unpaid` whose cash line arrived is left `unmatched` with `collection_not_recorded` and tried again when its cash state changes (spec 0018 records the collection, Q-19.4), except the partial delivery case of AC-17.
- **AC-17** (reconciled): a `remitted` order moves to `reconciled` (`recordSettlement` `{ to: 'reconciled', fullySettled: true }`) when all of these hold, computed by the pure `carrierCashTarget` in `packages/contracts/src/settlement/`: every live line naming any of the order's deliveries is `matched` or `accounted`; `remitted_amount` equals `cod_collected_amount`; `cod_collected_amount` equals `cod_due_amount` (never `cod_expected_amount`, spec 0002 orders child); the order's fee lines (every fee kind except the FlexShip refusal fee, on every delivery linked to the order: the send, a return to origin, a customer return pickup, an exchange pickup), negated, equal the sum of its current `delivery_charges` rows for those deliveries other than the `refusal_fee` leg (`DeliveryCharges.forOrders`, spec 0019 AC-11 and its revision for X1, compared by delivery), and the `flex_ship_refusal` line equals the `refusal_fee` leg (review M4); no fee line is above the rate card (AC-30); no open `sources_disagree` on its deliveries. Otherwise the order stays `remitted` and the screen lists its blockers: `line_unmatched`, `collected_below_due`, `collected_above_due`, `charges_differ` (labelled "Bosta's two records differ"), `fee_above_contract`, `sources_disagree`. A partial delivery has one path (review M9, as spec 0019 landed it): Bosta's collection is recorded at once at state 45 (cash `collected_by_carrier`) and only the delivered move is held (spec 0019's request (m) of spec 0013); the cash line matches that collection by rule 7, the order goes `remitted`, and until spec 0019's split lowers `cod_due_amount` to what Bosta collected it stays `remitted` with the blocker `collected_below_due`, shown with the label "awaiting the partial split" and linked to spec 0019's `delivery.collection_differs` task while the delivered move is held (`deliveredHeld` on the delivery, requested of spec 0018); after the split it reconciles. An order with nothing to collect (`nothing_to_collect`, a returned to origin parcel with a fee only, negative deposit) or `prepaid` keeps its cash state; its fee lines are matched and count in the totals.
- **AC-18** (stepping back): a person's unmatch of a `cod_collected` line, a revision that clears it (AC-3, AC-36), or a revoked match moves the order back the same way it came (`reconciled` to `remitted`, `remitted` to `collected_by_carrier`, reason `settlement_line_unmatched` or `cash_cycle_revised`) before anything else in the unit. A revision whose new `cod` differs on an order already past `collected_by_carrier` steps it back, then calls `recordCollection` with the new cash cycle `cod` (actor the carrier, source the delivery, reason `carrier.reported`), then matches again, all in one unit. Every step publishes `SettlementMatchRevoked` or `SettlementLineMatched`.
- **AC-19** (cash short or over, owner decision Q-22.4 as revised for review M7): an order blocked by `collected_below_due` or `collected_above_due` opens `settlement.cash_short` (linked to the order, hat operations, `settlement.work`, showing due, collected, and the difference). Below due: the task offers "raise a claim with Bosta", which opens a spec 0019 claim of kind `short_collection` for the difference (`CarrierClaims.openShortCollection`, requested); a holder of `settlement.close` may reconcile with the difference on `POST /api/settlement/orders/:orderId/reconcile-with-difference` with `carrier_shortage_written_off` only after that claim is `rejected`, or with `customer_paid_less_agreed` and a written note (spec 0010 audit entry reason, at most 500 characters), and with `rounding` only for a difference under 1.00. Above due: never written off; the route answers 409 `OWED_TO_CUSTOMER` until an owed back record exists for the difference in the credit module (a refund request or a credit, requested), after which the order reconciles with `customer_overpayment_owed_back`. Each reconcile calls `recordSettlement` as a person (requested from spec 0013), writes `settlement.order_reconciled_with_difference`, and shows on the period by reason.

- **AC-21** (periods and the totals check, reviews M4, M6, m3, m6, m8): a holder of `settlement.close` creates a period on an account (`POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/periods`, `startsOn`, `endsOn`; the built exclusion refuses an overlap with 409 `PERIOD_OVERLAPS`; shortcuts propose the current calendar month and "from the last period's end to the latest cash out"); closing and revoking are feature 23's. `GET /api/settlement/periods/:id/totals` answers, over the live lines whose `occurred_on` the period holds, one row per figure with what Bosta says, what the app says, and the difference: COD collected (lines against the matched orders' `cod_collected_amount`); cash due (the matched orders' `cod_due_amount` against the COD lines, the shortfall); fees net of VAT and VAT on fees as two rows (fee lines against the current `delivery_charges` of those deliveries, without the refusal leg); refusal fees collected at the door (the `flex_ship_refusal` lines against the `refusal_fee` legs); fees above contract (AC-30); refunds (refund lines against the matched carrier cash refunds); compensation (lines against `compensation_amount` recorded on the matched claims); Bosta's deposited amount (the `carrier_api` statements' `deposited_amount` against the sum of their lines); day totals that differ (AC-33); the wallet roll forward (AC-31); cash outs and cash outs not seen in bank (AC-32); pre go live lines (AC-35, shown apart); written off differences by reason (AC-19); one line for manual couriers this period (collected, received, owed, from AC-23's read); the lines by state (count and amount); and orders by cash state. A second grouping of the same totals by the delivery's final state day (read only, `groupBy=final_state_day`) serves the owner's month by deliveries. - **AC-23** (manual couriers, owner decision Q-22.5): a manual courier's cash stays outside carrier settlement accounts (spec 0019 AC-24 moves those orders' cash itself). `/settlement` shows an "Other couriers" tab, read only, with the collected, received, and owed totals by courier and day from spec 0019 (requested read), the period page shows the same as one line (AC-21), and those orders never appear in a carrier period's lines.

*The contract, the wallet, and the bank (owner decisions Q-22.9 and Q-22.10)*
- **AC-30** (the rate card, review M1): a holder of `settlement.close` keeps per account a rate card with effective dates (`POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/rate-cards`; versions never overlap, a change is a new version from a day, audited `settlement.rate_card_saved`): the shipping fee by Bosta city (carrier area id from spec 0018's `carrier_areas`, or a default for every other city) and delivery type (send, return to origin, customer return pickup, exchange), the COD fee as a percent of the COD collected (added to the shipping fee, 0 by default), the opening package fee, the FlexShip fee, the refusal fee, and the VAT rate (default 14%). After rule 7 the matcher compares each fee line of a delivery with the card in force on the delivery's Bosta creation day: shipping against city and type price plus the COD percent of the collected COD, each other fee against its price, VAT against the rate times the delivery's fees before VAT, all rounded to 2 decimals. A fee line above its price by 0.01 or more is `unmatched` with `fee_above_contract` (a person may match it with a reason, for example after the brand agreed a new rate); a fee at or below the price matches. With no card in force, fee lines match as before and the totals show "fees not checked against a contract". The reference brand's fee rows are used to find the real price points before the card's shape is fixed (brand question 7).
- **AC-31** (the wallet roll forward, review M2): a holder of `settlement.work` records Bosta's shown wallet balance on a day (`POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/wallet-balances`, `observedOn`, `balance`, `evidenceRef`, audited `settlement.wallet_balance_recorded`), or a mapped balance column of a sheet records it on import. For a period, the roll forward is: the opening balance (the observation on the day before `starts_on`, else none: "opening balance not recorded", never 0), plus every live line of the period (credits: COD, compensation, recharge, fee refunds; debits: fees, VAT, packing, insurance; minus cash outs), equals the expected closing balance, compared with the observation on `ends_on` when one exists. A difference is a totals row and a reason on the Unmatched tab (`wallet_balance_differs`).
- **AC-32** (cash outs to the bank, review M2): every `deposit` line (a cash out) has a bank state that never touches the order's cash machine: `cashed_out_by_bosta` until a holder of `settlement.work` records it received (`POST /api/settlement/lines/:id/bank-receipt`, `receivedOn`, `amount`, `bankReference` (at most 500 characters, redacted in logs), audited `settlement.cash_out_received`), then `received_in_bank` when the amount equals the cash out, or `received_differs` with both amounts and the task `settlement.cash_out_differs`. A receipt is corrected by a new receipt that supersedes it with a reason; nothing is edited. A cash out older than `settlement_bank_receipt_days` (default 5) with no receipt is listed on the Unmatched tab with `bank_receipt_awaited`. Feature 23 uses these receipts as close evidence.

*Gaps the first months bring (reviews M3, M10, M11)*
- **AC-33** (day totals kept, review M3): on apply, each `evidence_only` row is stored as a day evidence fact (the sheet's own figure for that day and kind: collected cash or Bosta fees); the per delivery sum it is compared with is computed on read, so a late cash cycle for that day updates the comparison. Every day whose figures differ is listed on the Unmatched tab with `day_total_differs` and both sums, and counted in the period totals.
- **AC-35** (the opening cutoff, review M10): each account has an opening cutoff, an instant set at creation from the earliest Shopify go live instant (`connections.go_live_at`) of the stores the connection serves (else the account's creation time), changeable by a holder of `settlement.close` with a reason (audited `settlement.opening_cutoff_changed`) while no period of the account is closed. A cash cycle kind line whose delivery Bosta created before the cutoff (`carrier_created_at` through `byTrackingNumbers`) is `accounted` `pre_go_live` by rule `pre_go_live_accounted`, never `no_order_for_tracking`, and its lines are shown as their own group in the totals. A line whose delivery is linked to an order in the app (an order the onboarding import brought in) is matched normally instead.
- **AC-36** (a mirror revision after a sheet, review M11): when a cash cycle revision above 1 arrives for a delivery whose live cash cycle kind lines came from a sheet, the module writes nothing at once, marks the comparison `sources_disagree` when the amounts differ (AC-9), and offers "take Bosta's revision" on the line drawer and the task. A holder of `settlement.work` taking it (audited `settlement.carrier_revision_taken`, with a reason code) writes the `carrier_api` statement for that revision and, in the same transaction, a `cleared` row with the reason `replaced_by_carrier_revision` on that delivery's cash cycle kind lines of the sheet only, then matches the new lines; the sheet's other lines stay live. An `accounted` sheet line answers 409 `LINE_FINAL`. This needs the expand only rule change of *Data model* (spec 0002).

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option C in [rationale.md](rationale.md): both sources feed one carrier settlement account; the per delivery cash cycles from spec 0018 lead (one small statement per delivery and revision, superseded on a revision), Bosta's sheet adds the wallet's own rows and confirms the cash cycles, a delivery's cash cycle kinds live on one live statement only, rules match by tracking number, exact amounts, and the brand's rate card, the order's cash moves through spec 0013's `recordSettlement` from one pure target function, and the wallet and the bank are followed beside the order machine, never in it.

**Implementation skills**: `nestjs-best-practices` (`kadajett/agent-nestjs-skills`, `.claude/skills/nestjs-best-practices/`) · `drizzle-orm-patterns` (`giuseppe-trisciuoglio/developer-kit`, `.claude/skills/drizzle-orm-patterns/`) · `postgresql-table-design` (`wshobson/agents`, `.claude/skills/postgresql-table-design/`) · `pg-boss-v12` (`sncollective/snc-platform`, `.claude/skills/pg-boss-v12/`) · `nextjs-app-router-patterns` (`wshobson/agents`, `.claude/skills/nextjs-app-router-patterns/`) · `react-hook-form-zod` (`.claude/skills/react-hook-form-zod/`, the mapping and rate card forms) · `playwright-best-practices` (`currents-dev/playwright-best-practices-skill`, `.claude/skills/playwright-best-practices/`)

**Decided by the architect** (expertise, not owner questions; runner up in brackets):
- One statement per delivery and cash cycle revision, superseded on a revision, uses the built shapes exactly and lets a revision replace only that delivery's lines [one statement per cash cycle id: a revision of one delivery would have to supersede every delivery of the cycle, and whether one id groups a day is unverified].
- Dedupe between sources at the delivery level (tracking number) for the cash cycle kinds only, because a sheet may give only a total fee where the mirror gives a breakdown; wallet only rows always add, deduped by content (review B1, the lead's ruling) [per kind keys of spec 0002: count the fees twice when one side has no breakdown].
- Events are hints; every import reads the delivery back through `CarrierDeliveries` [apply the event payload: a late or replayed event would write stale amounts].
- The account is created by the system on first need [by a manager: one more step before any cash shows, and the partial unique index already keeps it single].
- `settlement_statements.received_at` is database time, stamped at insert, so no reader picks a "latest" statement by a writer's clock (security review S11; expand request below) [business time: the statement already has `covers_from`, `covers_to`, and `deposited_on` for that].
- The order's cash move is a direct `recordSettlement` call in the matching unit, as spec 0013 offers it, and `SettlementLineMatched` is published for readers [orders listening to the event, as the spec 0002 settlement child first wrote: the move would leave the matching transaction's control].
- Wallet balances and bank receipts are facts a person (or a sheet) records in their own small tables, not settlement lines, so the roll forward never invents a line [an opening balance as an `adjustment` line accounted `opening_balance`: a person's figure would sit among Bosta's facts and count in the statement totals].
- A rounding residue is kept as Bosta's own `bosta_unitemized` line, never folded into the VAT line (review m1, in part) [absorb up to 0.02 into VAT: it would change a figure Bosta gave].

## Owner decisions

The architect wrote this spec without the owner. Each question carries the options and the recommended answer the design is built on; the owner ratifies or changes them before milestone 2. Q-22.8 is spec 0016's owner question 2 extended, not a second question.

- **Q-22.1 Which source leads.** (a) The per delivery cash cycles from the Bosta mirror lead; Bosta's sheet adds the wallet's own rows and confirms the cash cycles; the first live statement of a delivery's cash cycle kinds wins, a later source is compared, and a later Bosta revision may replace a sheet by a person's choice (AC-9, AC-36). (b) The monthly sheet is the only source of lines; the mirror's cash cycles are shown as provisional figures only. (c) Import both fully and pair them line by line. **Recommended: (a).** The mirror's figures arrive within a day of Bosta filling them and need no person; (b) leaves the month invisible until someone downloads a file; (c) doubles every line and needs a pairing step that can itself go wrong.
- **Q-22.2 What counts as remitted.** (a) A matched cash line whose cash cycle carries Bosta's deposit time, labelled "credited by Bosta" (in the wallet); the bank is followed separately on the cash outs (AC-32). (b) Only once a cash out to the bank covering that day is recorded. **Recommended: (a).** A cash out covers many days and lists no deliveries (E3, E7 in [rationale.md](rationale.md#bosta-evidence)), so (b) cannot tie a cash out to an order; whether `deposited_at` is the wallet credit or the bank transfer is brand question 2.
- **Q-22.3 Amount tolerance.** (a) Exact after parsing to 2 decimal places; a person may match a difference with a reason. (b) A setting, default 0.00, at most 1.00 per line. **Recommended: (a).** Bosta's float noise is removed by the 2 decimal parse (E5), so any remaining difference is real money.
- **Q-22.4 Cash short or over after everything matched** (revised for review M7). (a) Below due: the order stays `remitted` with a task; raise a `short_collection` claim with Bosta first, and write off only after it is rejected or with a written note; `rounding` only under 1.00. Above due: never written off; it is owed back to the customer through the credit module. (b) A manager reconciles any difference with a reason code. **Recommended: (a)**: a courier shortage is money Bosta may owe, and an over collection is the customer's money.
- **Q-22.5 Manual courier cash.** (a) Outside carrier settlement accounts, as spec 0019 AC-24 moves it; a read only Other couriers tab and one line on the period. (b) A settlement account per courier now. **Recommended: (a)**: the cash is handed in by a person with an evidence reference, so there is no third party statement to match.
- **Q-22.6 Bosta's sheet.** (a) Build the configurable column mapping with a preview now; ask the pilot brand for one real wallet sheet and one cash cycle breakdown now, ship a prefilled mapping for its header row, and require at least one real sheet in AC-29. (b) Wait for the sheet before building the import. **Recommended: (a).** No sheet columns are known (E4), and without a sheet the month has no evidence from outside the mirror.
- **Q-22.7 Who makes periods in this feature.** (a) A holder of `settlement.close` creates periods of any range, with shortcuts for the calendar month and for "to the latest cash out"; closing is feature 23. (b) The system makes one period per calendar month. **Recommended: (a)**: a carrier period is whatever range the brand settles (spec 0002), and Bosta settles by cash out cadence.
- **Q-22.8 The refund in the done when** (review B3). This is spec 0016's owner question 2 (where the manual refund record lives: the credit module, a thin slice of feature 35), extended with the method `carrier_cash`. (a) Bring that minimal refund record, with `carrier_cash`, into slice 1a, and count a Bosta refund line in the done when only if the pilot month has one. (b) Change the scope's done when to "refund as a negative line, when the carrier carries refunds". (c) Wait for the credit slice before calling feature 22 done. **Recommended: (a)**: the reference brand's return pickups carry COD 0 (E8), so the done when must not demand a line Bosta may never send, and the record is needed anyway for the refunds the brand does pay.
- **Q-22.9 Fees against the contract** (review M1). (a) A rate card per Bosta connection in this feature (shipping by city and type, the COD fee percent, VAT 14%, opening package, FlexShip, refusal fee), and fees above it become the unmatched reason `fee_above_contract`. (b) Carry the contract check to a later feature and label `charges_differ` honestly as "Bosta's two records differ". **Recommended: (a)**: fees are about a fifth of COD (E5), so a drift of 5 EGP on 2,000 parcels is 10,000 EGP a month, and nothing else in the design compares Bosta with anything but Bosta.
- **Q-22.10 Where cash meets the bank** (review M2). (a) The wallet roll forward and "received in bank" on cash outs in this feature, as a cash out state beside the order machine (no change to spec 0002's cash enum), with the bank evidence for close in feature 23. (b) Everything about the bank in feature 23. **Recommended: (a)**: the pilot month of AC-29 is the first one the owner judges, and a short cash out is the leak she fears most.
- **Q-22.11 An accountant hat** (review m2). (a) A settlement only hat for an outside accountant (`settlement.work`, `settlement.close`, `orders.view` for the order numbers it shows, nothing that confirms or edits orders or customers), requested of spec 0008. (b) The accountant uses the operations hat. **Recommended: (a)**: the operations hat confirms and edits orders, which an outside accountant must not do.

A customer return pickup with a non zero `cod` that fits none of branches A, B, or C writes its lines with branch A's sign and fails AC-5. Worked: `cod` 300.00, shipping 45.00, VAT 6.30, `deposited_amt` −351.30 is branch A (lines −300.00, −45.00, −6.30, sum −351.30, AC-5 passes); `cod` −300.00 with the same deposit is branch B (the same lines); `cod` 300.00 with `deposited_amt` 248.70 is branch C. Bosta gives no separate COD fee in the cash cycle (E2); `cod_fee` lines come only from a sheet that has one, and the rate card's COD percent is checked on the shipping line. A returned to origin parcel gives fee lines only and a negative statement total.

### Lines from a sheet (AC-6 to AC-8)

- `per_delivery`: one row gives the same lines as the table above from its mapped columns (COD, shipping, open package, FlexShip, other fee, VAT), or, when the mapping names only a total fees column, one `other_fee` line with `fee_code` `bosta_fees_total`; these are all `cash_cycle_part`. A deposited amount column is checked like AC-5 (`cash_cycle_arithmetic_differs`); a balance column records a wallet balance observation (AC-31).
- `per_transaction`: each row is one line of the kind its type value maps to. The recommended starting map from Bosta's documented wallet types (E3), each with its family: Compensation to `compensation` (`wallet_only`), Cash out to `deposit` negated, money that left the wallet (`wallet_only`), Packing Material Fees to `other_fee` `packing_material` (`wallet_only`), Insurance fee to `other_fee` `insurance` (`wallet_only`), Recharge Balance to `adjustment` positive (`wallet_only`), the wallet's "refunds" credit to `adjustment` positive with the type text in `partner_text`, never `customer_refund` (`wallet_only`, review m4; brand question 11), Collected Cash Cycle and Bosta Fees to `evidence_only` (stored as day evidence, AC-33, never lines).
- `external_line_ref`: the transaction id when mapped, else the row number; `occurred_on` the row's deposit date; `partner_text` the mapped description, cut at 500 characters.

Each reason has a next step sentence in the catalog (AC-26).

### The order's cash target (AC-16 to AC-18)

`carrierCashTarget({ cashState, codCollected, codDue, remitted, lines, charges, openDisagreement, feesAboveContract })` is pure and answers the state the order should be in (`collected_by_carrier`, `remitted`, `reconciled`, or `unchanged` for an order outside the carrier path) and the list of blockers. `order-cash-sync.ts` reads the order through `OrderQueries.summaryOf`, the order's live lines and heads, and `DeliveryCharges.forOrders`, then moves the order along the shortest legal path of spec 0013's table (stepping back first when needed), one `recordSettlement` call per step, inside the same `OrderTransactions.run` unit as the match rows. Concurrency: the unit takes the order's row lock through orders (spec 0013's locked order) and the line's chain is serialized by the built unique `supersedes_match_id`, so two matchers cannot both win. The wallet and bank states of AC-31 and AC-32 are never inputs of this function.

### Key invariants

- The settlement module is the only writer of the settlement tables and of every table this feature adds; the order's cash changes only through spec 0013's calls.
- A delivery's cash cycle kinds are on at most one live statement of an account; a duplicate by content is compared, never written; wallet only rows add (AC-9).
- Lines are never edited or deleted; a correction is a superseding statement (or a taken Bosta revision, AC-36), and the predecessors end `cleared`.
- Every unmatched line has a reason; every accounted line has an account code (the built checks).
- `remitted` means credited in Bosta's wallet and needs a matched COD line with a `deposited_on`; `reconciled` needs `cod_collected_amount` equal to `cod_due_amount`, never `cod_expected_amount`, unless a person reconciled a shortfall with a reason after the claim path; an over collection is never written off.
- The wallet and bank states never move the order's cash state.
- Every money figure shown or answered by this feature carries `provisional` until feature 23 closes its period; an unknown is never shown as 0.
- No import reads a value from the event payload; every amount is read back from the mirror or the file.

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- The month's Bosta money is matched as it arrives; the accountant works only the lines and items that say why they do not match, each with its next step.
- Three leaks the reference brand could not see are now visible: fees above the contract, a cash out short of what reached the bank, and compensation that never came.
- Every order ends in a final cash state or shows what stops it, which is what the wind down and the daily report need.
- The sheet import works with any column layout Bosta uses, so an unknown export format never blocks the slice.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- Thousands of small statements a month (one per delivery and revision); the Statements tab groups them by deposit day.
- The rate card, wallet balances, and bank receipts are typed in by a person; a brand that never records them gets "not checked" and "not recorded" rows, never a false agreement.
- Seven new tables or columns and one chain rule change, requested of spec 0002 as expand only.
- Several facts stay unverified (the meaning of `deposited_at`, how a refund on a return pickup and the FlexShip refusal fee show, the sheet's columns); the brand questions in Follow-up settle them.
- The refund rule depends on the credit slice of Q-22.8, and the claim, charge, and partial rules on spec 0019's revision.

**Neutral**:
- This feature is the first to store files, so object storage configuration lands with it.
- Period close is deliberately absent: every figure is provisional until feature 23.
