## Summary

Bosta keeps the cash it collects at the door, credits it to the brand's Bosta wallet minus its fees, and later cashes the wallet out to the bank. This feature proves that money line by line. Every delivery's cash cycle (Bosta's own record of what it collected, what it charged, and when it credited the rest) arrives from the Bosta mirror and becomes a small statement of settlement lines; Bosta's downloadable wallet sheet is imported through a column mapping the brand saves once, with a preview before anything is written, and adds the wallet's own rows (compensation, cash outs, packing) without ever counting a delivery's cash cycle twice. Each line is matched to its order by tracking number, or accounted to an account code when it has no order, and an unmatched line always says why and can be worked in bulk; fees are also checked against the brand's Bosta rate card, including fees matched before the card was typed in. When an order's lines are matched its cash moves to remitted (credited in Bosta's wallet), and to reconciled once everything agrees or spec 0019 has decided the cash difference; the wallet is rolled forward from balance to balance and each cash out is checked off against the bank, without touching the order's states. Period totals compare what Bosta says with what the app holds, show everything Bosta still owed at the period's end, export as CSV for the accountant, and stay labelled provisional until feature 23 closes the period.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As the accountant, I want Bosta's money for every delivery to arrive by itself, matched to its order, so that I only look at the lines that do not match, each one tells me why and what to do next, and I can act on a whole group at once.
- As the accountant, I want to import Bosta's wallet sheet with a mapping I set once and a preview I check, so that cash outs, compensations, and anything the mirror missed are in the same place, and nothing counts twice.
- As the owner, I want to know how much Bosta owed me at the end of a month (every delivered parcel's cash not yet credited, compensation not yet paid, the wallet not yet cashed out, cash outs not yet in my bank), so that no money sits with the carrier unseen.
- As the owner, I want fees above my Bosta contract flagged, even for a month already imported, so that a rate change or a fee I never asked for does not quietly eat my margin.
- As the accountant, I want a month's totals beside the app's own figures, split as I post them, and a CSV of the lines and the summary, so that I can book the month without copying screens.
- As operations, I want a lost parcel's approved claim to be closed by the compensation Bosta pays, and an approved claim that is never paid to stand out, so that no claim is left waiting.
- As the vendor, I want the slice proven on one real month of the pilot brand's settlement, so that the design rests on Bosta's real data.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract; each is checked in [verify.md](verify.md)). AC-36 moved to feature 23 with the second pass trim (*Later than slice 1a*); its number is not reused.

*Accounts and the per delivery cash cycles (the lead source)*
- **AC-1** (one account per Bosta connection): the settlement module keeps exactly one live carrier settlement account per Bosta connection (`owner_type` `carrier`, `owner_connection_id` the connection), created by the system the first time a cash cycle or a file needs it (`SettlementAccounts.ensureForConnection`, under the advisory lock `settlement_account:<connection id>`; the built partial unique index refuses a second), with `code` `BOSTA-` plus the last 8 characters of the connection id in capitals (the first 8 of a uuidv7 repeat for connections made within about a minute, review r2 m1), `name` the connection's label in both languages, and its opening cutoff (AC-35). `/settlement` lists the accounts with their unmatched count, last import, and, as the balance, the roll forward's expected balance with its opening observation, or "opening balance not recorded" (AC-31, review r2 m7), all marked provisional.
- **AC-2** (a cash cycle becomes a statement): on `CarrierCashCycleRecorded` the module enqueues `settlement-carrier-import` for that delivery in the publisher's transaction (singleton key `<delivery id>:<revision>`); the job reads the delivery back through `CarrierDeliveries.byTrackingNumbers` (never the event payload) and, only when the cash cycle has a deposit time, writes one `carrier_api` statement for that delivery and revision: `external_reference` `bosta-cc:<cashCycleId>:<trackingNumber>:r<revision>`, `covers_from` and `covers_to` and `deposited_on` the deposit day, `deposited_amount` and `statement_total` Bosta's `deposited_amt`, and the lines of *Lines from a cash cycle*. Each line's `occurred_on` is the deposit day in the install time zone (`timezone` setting), `external_line_ref` `<trackingNumber>:<line_kind>` (the fee name in `fee_code` on `other_fee` lines), `tracking_number` and `business_reference` from the delivery, and `store_id` from AC-15. A zero amount writes no line; a cash cycle with no deposit time writes nothing and waits (spec 0002 settlement child, `occurred_on`). Replaying the job writes nothing new (the statement's unique key).
- **AC-3** (revisions supersede): a later revision of the same delivery's cash cycle (a changed amount or a changed cash cycle id, spec 0018 AC-10) writes a new statement with `supersedes_statement_id` the delivery's live statement on this account, and in the same transaction a `cleared` row with the reason `superseded` on every line of the old statement (accounted lines included, domain review D1), then matches the new lines (AC-10). An event with a revision at or below the live one changes nothing. When the delivery's live cash cycle lines came from a sheet, the revision is compared (AC-9) and a person corrects the sheet (until feature 23 builds the "take Bosta's revision" choice, *Later than slice 1a*). Whether Bosta ever settles one tracking number in two cash cycles (cash first, a return fee later), which this rule would read as a revision, is brand question 13, answered before milestone 2.
- **AC-4** (the sweep, spec 0007 M1): a nightly `settlement-carrier-sweep` (03:30 in the install time zone) and a first run when an account is created walk `CarrierDeliveries.cashCycles({ connectionId, depositedFrom, depositedTo, cursor, limit: 500 })` from the later of the account's opening cutoff and 60 days back (first run) or the last 14 days (nightly), and import every deposited cash cycle whose revision the account does not hold yet, so a lost event never leaves a cash cycle out. A run stops after 10 minutes and the next one resumes from its cursor.
- **AC-5** (Bosta's arithmetic, review B2): after the derivation table has chosen each line, including the refund sign branch, the signed sum of the delivery's derived lines must equal Bosta's `deposited_amt`; for a send and a return to origin, Bosta's own `deposited_amt = cod − bosta_fees` is checked as well. When either differs by 0.01 or more, the statement and its lines are still written as derived, and every line of it gets `unmatched` with `cash_cycle_arithmetic_differs`, listed for a person. Before milestone 1, `cash-cycle-lines.ts` is run over an anonymised copy of the reference brand's 5,622 cash cycle rows and the number of `bosta_unitemized` residue lines and arithmetic failures is recorded in [verify.md](verify.md) (review m1).

*Bosta's sheet (fallback and evidence)*
- **AC-6** (the column mapping, owner decision Q-22.6): a holder of `settlement.work` saves a mapping per account on `POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/file-mappings`: a name, the layout (`per_delivery`: one row per delivery with amount columns; or `per_transaction`: one row per wallet transaction with a type column and one amount column), the header row number (1 to 20), the role of each used column by its header text (tracking number, business reference, deposit date, cash cycle id, transaction id, type, amount, COD, shipping fee, opening package fee, FlexShip fee, other fee (with its `fee_code`), VAT, total fees, deposited amount, balance, description), for `per_transaction` the map from each type value to a line kind or to `evidence_only` or `ignore`, and for every type value mapped to a line kind its family, `cash_cycle_part` or `wallet_only` (review B1; the starting map in *Lines from a sheet*; a type value with no family is `TYPE_UNMAPPED`, so a new Bosta type never falls into the wrong rule), the date format (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `DD/MM/YYYY`, `MM/DD/YYYY`, or a spreadsheet date number), and the decimal separator. Header texts may be Arabic or English and are matched after trimming and folding case. Editing saves a new version that supersedes the old one; a mapping is never changed in place. Columns the mapping does not name are never read into the app.
- **AC-7** (the preview): `POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/file-preview` takes one `.xlsx` or `.csv` file of at most 5 MB and 20,000 rows and a mapping id, writes nothing, and answers: the file's SHA-256, its row count, and per row one class: `new` (lines it would write, with kinds and amounts), `confirmed` (the account already holds the live cash cycle lines of this delivery, or this wallet transaction, and the amounts agree), `differs` (it holds them and an amount differs, with both amounts), `evidence_only` (a daily total, with the sheet's figure), or `rejected` with a reason (`TRACKING_MISSING`, `AMOUNT_UNREADABLE`, `DATE_UNREADABLE`, `TYPE_UNMAPPED`, `ROW_EMPTY`); plus the totals by line kind and class, the days the file covers, and the match forecast for the `new` lines. It never answers a value from a column the mapping does not name.
- **AC-8** (apply): `POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/file-imports` with the same file, the mapping id, and `expectedSha256` (409 `FILE_CHANGED` when it differs) stores the file in object storage (`settlement/<account id>/<sha256>.<ext>`), then in one transaction writes one `carrier_file` statement (`external_reference` the file name, `sha256`, `object_key`, `covers_from` and `covers_to` the first and last row date, `statement_total` the sum of the file's amounts over every `new`, `confirmed`, and `differs` row, `imported_by` the person), the `new` rows' lines (each with `occurred_on` the row's date), the day evidence of AC-33, and any balance the sheet shows as a wallet balance observation (AC-31), runs the rules (AC-10), and writes the audit entry `settlement.file_imported` (mapping id and version, row counts by class). The same file again on the same account answers 409 `FILE_ALREADY_IMPORTED`. A corrected file is imported with `supersedesStatementId` and a reason code: its statement supersedes the old one and the old lines are cleared with `superseded`, as AC-3.

*Two sources, one count (spec 0002 domain review M8)*
- **AC-9** (no delivery counted twice, reviews B1 and r2 N7): the line kinds a cash cycle produces (`cod_collected`, `shipping_fee`, `opening_package_fee`, `fee_vat`, `cod_fee`, `customer_refund`, and `other_fee` with `flex_ship`, `flex_ship_refusal`, `bosta_unitemized`, or `bosta_fees_total`) of one delivery live on one statement at a time within an account. A cash cycle, or a sheet row of the `cash_cycle_part` family, for a tracking number whose cash cycle kind lines are already live (on a statement nobody superseded, with heads not `cleared`) on another statement is never written: it is compared. Equal amounts by kind (or equal net when one side has no breakdown) count as `confirmed`; a difference makes those live lines `unmatched` with `sources_disagree`, opens the task `settlement.sources_disagree` linked to the newer statement, publishes `CarrierSettlementSourcesDisagree`, and lists both amounts. This holds in both directions and between two overlapping sheets. Rows of the `wallet_only` family (`compensation`, `adjustment`, `deposit`, `other_fee` `insurance`, `other_fee` `packing_material`, and any type a mapping marks `wallet_only`) are added beside the delivery's cash cycle lines, with or without a tracking number, and are deduplicated against live lines on other statements by content (spec 0002 M8): the same transaction id, or, when the mapping has no transaction id column, the same kind, fee name, tracking number, day, and amount. One exception (review r2 N7): a `wallet_only` fee row with a tracking number whose amount equals that delivery's live `bosta_unitemized` line is the fee the cash cycle already holds, so it is `confirmed` and not added (brand question 17 settles whether insurance sits inside Bosta's fees). When a sheet's lines settle a delivery the mirror has no deposited cash cycle for, the module publishes `CarrierCashSettledByFile` (connection, delivery id when known, tracking number, deposit day), so the connector can stop calling that delivery overdue (requested from spec 0018).

*Matching*
- **AC-10** (the rules, in order): after every import, after every relevant event (`CarrierDeliveryLinked`, `OrderStateChanged` on cash, `CarrierClaimDecided`, a cash difference resolution of spec 0019, a refund record change), and nightly, the matching job (capability `settlement.carrier_reconciliation`) takes each line whose head is not `matched`, `accounted`, or `cleared`, oldest `occurred_on` first, in batches of 500, and applies the first rule that holds, writing one `settlement_line_matches` row with `matched_by_type` `rule` and its `rule_code`, or an `unmatched` row with the reason when the reason changed (an unchanged reason writes nothing). A rate card save reruns the fee rules over matched lines too (AC-30). The rules and reasons are the *Matching rules* table. A line's state is always `lineMatchState` over its rows.
- **AC-11** (tolerance, owner decision Q-22.3): amounts are compared exactly after both sides are parsed to 2 decimal places, half away from zero; any difference of 0.01 or more is a difference. A person may still match a line that differs, with a reason code.
- **AC-12** (a person's actions): a holder of `settlement.work` matches a line to an order (`POST /api/settlement/lines/:id/match`, with a reason code), accounts it to an account code (`/account`, with an optional payment reference of at most 500 characters), unmatches it with a reason code (`/unmatch`), or picks the claim for a compensation (`/claim`), each with `expectedHeadId` (the line's current head, null for none; 409 `MATCH_STALE` when it moved). A line whose head is `accounted` or `cleared` answers 409 `LINE_FINAL`. Each action writes one match row with `matched_by_type` `person` and `matched_by` the person, and the audit entry `settlement.line_matched_by_person`, `settlement.line_accounted`, `settlement.line_unmatched_by_person`, or `settlement.compensation_claim_picked`.
- **AC-37** (bulk on an Unmatched group, review r2 N5): on any Unmatched group a holder of `settlement.work` acts on up to 500 lines in one request (`POST /api/settlement/lines/bulk`, the line ids each with its `expectedHeadId`): `retry` (run the rules again now), `account` (one account code and reason code for all), or `accept` (match each line to its own delivery's order with one reason code; only for groups whose lines already know their order, such as `fee_above_contract` or `amount_differs`). Each line still gets its own match row; a stale or final line is skipped and listed in the answer. One audit entry `settlement.lines_bulk_actioned` lists the ids, the action, and the codes. Fee lines accepted above the contract stay visible in the totals as "accepted above contract".
- **AC-13** (refunds, the lane C agreement, owner decision Q-22.8): this feature matches only Bosta's refund lines: a `customer_refund` line (the cash a courier handed a customer on a customer return pickup, see *Lines from a cash cycle*) is matched to its order and to the refund record with method `carrier_cash` on that order with the same amount (`refund_id` on the match row), and confirms that refund through the credit module. The refund record is the minimal one spec 0016 proposes for the credit module in slice 1a (its owner question 2, a thin slice of feature 35), extended with the method `carrier_cash`; Q-22.8 is that same decision, not a second one. With no such record the line is `unmatched` with `refund_record_missing` and the task `settlement.refund_unmatched` opens; it matches on the refund record's creation. A refund by InstaPay, wallet, or bank transfer never touches a carrier settlement.
- **AC-14** (compensation to a claim, audit item 12, spec 0019 AC-22): a `compensation` line is matched to a claim from `CarrierClaims.awaitingCompensation({ connectionId: account.owner_connection_id })` on these fields, in this order: the connection (never across accounts); the tracking number when the line carries one (then the claim with that tracking number is the match, whatever the amount, and spec 0019 records `received_differs` when the amounts differ); otherwise `approved_amount` equal to the line's amount, `decided_at` on or before the line's `occurred_on`, and, when more than one claim is left, the one whose `carrier_complaint_ref` appears in the line's text (`partner_text`); and the claim's `compensation_state` `awaiting` (the read answers only those). This covers spec 0019's `short_collection` claims as well as lost and damaged ones. Exactly one claim gives a `matched` row on the claim's order, rule `claim_by_tracking` or `claim_by_amount`, and `CarrierClaims.recordCompensation(tx, claimId, { settlementLineId, amount, depositedOn: occurred_on }, actor)` in the same unit. None gives `unmatched` `no_claim_for_compensation`; several give `claims_ambiguous`; both open `settlement.compensation_unmatched`. When a line has no tracking number, matches no claim by amount, and exactly one awaiting claim exists on the connection, the line drawer offers that claim as the single candidate whatever the amount (review M5); a person confirms it (never a rule), and spec 0019 records `received_differs`. A person may also account the line as `carrier_compensation`.
- **AC-15** (the store on every line, spec 0007 B1): a carrier line's `store_id` is the account's store binding when the connection has one, otherwise the store of the delivery its tracking number names, read through `CarrierDeliveries.byTrackingNumbers` (spec 0018 Q-19.1), never an orders table. A line that needs an order and has no store is `unmatched` with `store_unknown` and is tried again on `CarrierDeliveryLinked`. A wallet line with no tracking number on an unbound account keeps `store_id` null.

*The order's cash (spec 0002 AC-20, spec 0013 AC-17)*
- **AC-16** (remitted means credited in Bosta's wallet, owner decisions Q-22.2 and Q-22.10): when an order whose cash state is `collected_by_carrier` has every live `cod_collected` line of its forward delivery `matched`, and their statements carry a `deposited_on`, the module calls `OrderTransitions.recordSettlement(tx, orderId, { to: 'remitted', remittedAmount: <sum of those lines>, depositedOn: <latest deposit day>, sourceId: <the last line's match id> }, systemJob)` inside the matching unit (`OrderTransactions.run`). `remitted` here means Bosta has credited the cash to the brand's Bosta wallet, not that it is in the bank, and `reconciled` means checked against Bosta, not banked: on carrier orders every screen and report of this feature labels them "credited by Bosta" and "checked against Bosta" in both catalogs (review r2 m8; the codes stay; no change to spec 0002's cash enum), and the Orders tab shows the account's "cashed out, not yet seen in bank" figure. The bank side is the cash out state of AC-32, which never touches the order's machine. An order still `unpaid` whose cash line arrived is left `unmatched` with `collection_not_recorded` and tried again when its cash state changes (spec 0018 records the collection at delivered, Q-19.4, also while spec 0019 holds the delivered move of a partial).
- **AC-17** (reconciled, reviews M9, r2 N1, r2 m12): a `remitted` order moves to `reconciled` (`recordSettlement` `{ to: 'reconciled', fullySettled: true }`) when all of these hold, computed by the pure `carrierCashTarget` in `packages/contracts/src/settlement/`: every live line naming any of the order's deliveries is `matched` or `accounted`; `remitted_amount` equals `cod_collected_amount`; the cash agrees with `cod_due_amount` (never `cod_expected_amount`, spec 0002 orders child) in one of these ways, read from spec 0019's decision through `CashDifferences.resolutionOf(orderIds)` (spec 0019 AC-32: per order the difference amount, its cause, and one resolution): `cod_collected_amount` equals `cod_due_amount`; or the resolution is `claim_paid` and `cod_collected_amount` plus the compensation spec 0019 recorded on that claim (`CarrierClaims.forOrders`, compensation state `received` or `received_differs`) equals `cod_due_amount`; or the resolution is `written_off` (route `courier_shortfall_written_off` or `brand_side_write_off`, with its amount and time), which is the evidence and shows on the period by route; or the resolution is `over_collection_refund_customer` (the refund owed is the credit module's) or `over_collection_belongs_to_brand` (the excess counts in the totals as "kept by the brand at the door"); the order's fee lines (every fee kind except the FlexShip refusal fee, on every delivery linked to the order: the send, a return to origin, a customer return pickup, an exchange pickup), negated, equal the sum of its current `delivery_charges` rows for those deliveries other than the `refusal_fee` leg (`DeliveryCharges.forOrders`, compared by delivery), and the `flex_ship_refusal` line equals the `refusal_fee` leg (review M4); no fee line is above the rate card (AC-30); no open `sources_disagree` on its deliveries. Otherwise the order stays `remitted` and the Orders tab lists its blockers: `line_unmatched`, `collected_below_due`, `collected_above_due`, `charges_differ` (labelled "Bosta's two records differ"), `fee_above_contract`, `sources_disagree`. Settlement never decides or writes off a cash difference (spec 0019 owns every write off): a `collected_below_due` or `collected_above_due` blocker carries the label of the order's resolution and links to spec 0019's open task for it: `held_partial` "awaiting the partial split" (spec 0019's `delivery.collection_differs`, no claim offered), `brand_side_pending` "Bosta was never told the new amount" (the collected cash equals Bosta's `cod_amount`, so the difference is the brand's, cause `carrier_record_stale` or `cod_rule_mismatch` on spec 0018's `CarrierCollectionDiffers`, never a claim), `customer_owes` "the customer owes the difference", `claim_pending` "claim with Bosta pending" (with the claim and its amount), `over_collection_pending` "over collection to decide", and `none` "waiting for operations to decide". A partial delivery has one path: Bosta's collection is recorded at 45, the cash line matches it, the order is `remitted` with `collected_below_due` (`held_partial`) while the delivered move is held, spec 0019's split then sets `cod_due_amount` from the pieces the customer kept, and any rest is spec 0019's `short_collection` claim, read as above. An order with nothing to collect (`nothing_to_collect`, a returned to origin parcel with a fee only, negative deposit) or `prepaid` keeps its cash state; its fee lines are matched and count in the totals.
- **AC-18** (stepping back, review r2 m11): a person's unmatch of a `cod_collected` line, a revision that clears it (AC-3), or a revoked match moves the order back the same way it came (`reconciled` to `remitted`, `remitted` to `collected_by_carrier`, reason `settlement_line_unmatched` or `cash_cycle_revised`) before anything else in the unit. On an order already past `collected_by_carrier`, settlement is the writer of a corrected collection: a revision whose new `cod` differs steps the order back, then calls `recordCollection` with the new cash cycle `cod` (actor the carrier, source the delivery, reason `carrier.reported`), then matches again, all in one unit. Spec 0018's own correction on such an order answers `{ applied: false }` with the attention `collection_changed_after_remittance` (requested of spec 0013), which spec 0013 clears when settlement's `recordCollection` applies in that unit. Every step publishes `SettlementMatchRevoked` or `SettlementLineMatched`.
- **AC-38** (cash on an order that owed nothing, review r2 N8): a `cod_collected` line on an order whose cash is `prepaid`, or `nothing_to_collect` with fulfillment delivered, is `unmatched` with `collected_with_nothing_due`, whose next step reads "The customer may have paid twice: check her payment and owe the difference back"; it links to spec 0019's over collection for the order (requested: spec 0019 records a collection on an order with nothing due as an over collection, resolution `over_collection_pending`), and it is matched by rule `collected_with_nothing_due_resolved` once the resolution is `over_collection_refund_customer` (a refund owed exists in the credit module) or `over_collection_belongs_to_brand`. Its amount has its own totals row.
- **AC-19** (cash differences are spec 0019's, owner decision Q-22.4 as revised for review r2 N1): settlement has no write off route, opens no claim (it never calls `CarrierClaims.openShortCollection`, which spec 0019 itself calls and which refuses `SHORTFALL_IS_BRAND_SIDE` and `PARTIAL_UNDECIDED`), and has no cash short task. It reads `CashDifferences.resolutionOf(orderIds)` (spec 0019 AC-32) and applies AC-17. The period totals show written off amounts by route, compensation received on short collection claims, brand side differences, and over collections by resolution.

*What is missing, the period, and the provisional label*
- **AC-20** (expected but not seen, spec 0018 Follow-up M2, reviews M5 and r2 N3): the account's Unmatched tab also lists, as items with a reason: every delivery of its connection that spec 0018 holds in `cash_cycle_overdue` or `cash_cycle_not_seen` (read through `CarrierDeliveries.awaitingCashCycle`, requested), with tracking number, store and order number, collected amount, final state day, and days waiting, minus those a live sheet line already settles (AC-9), reason `cash_cycle_awaited`; and every claim from `CarrierClaims.overdueCompensation({ connectionId, decidedBefore })`, with `decidedBefore` today minus spec 0019's setting `claim_compensation_overdue_days` (default 30; this feature has no threshold of its own), with claim number, order, approved amount, decided day, and days waiting, reason `compensation_awaited`.

*The contract, the wallet, and the bank (owner decisions Q-22.9 and Q-22.10)*
- **AC-30** (the rate card, reviews M1, r2 N4, r2 m4): a holder of `settlement.close` keeps per account rate card versions (`POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/rate-cards`, audited `settlement.rate_card_saved`), each in force from its `effective_from` until the next current version's: one shipping price per delivery type (send, return to origin, customer return pickup, exchange; a type with no price is not checked), optional overrides of the shipping price by zone (a Bosta carrier area id from spec 0018's `carrier_areas`; the delivery's zone override wins over its city override, which wins over the type's price), the COD fee as a percent of the COD collected (added to the shipping price, 0 by default), the opening package fee, the FlexShip fee, and the VAT rate (default 14%). The refusal fee is not on the card: a refusal fee collected below the brand's terms is spec 0019's check (`REFUSAL_FEE_ABOVE_TERMS`), since the line is money for the brand. A version typed wrong is corrected for the same days by a new version that supersedes it with the same or an earlier `effective_from` and a reason code (audited `settlement.rate_card_corrected`); the superseded version is never in force again. After rule 9 the matcher compares each fee line of a delivery with the version in force on the delivery's Bosta creation day: shipping against the price plus the COD percent of the collected COD, each other fee against its price, VAT against the rate times the delivery's fees before VAT, all rounded to 2 decimals. A fee line above its price by 0.01 or more is `unmatched` with `fee_above_contract`. Every save or correction reruns this check over every fee line of the account's open periods whose delivery Bosta created on or after the version's `effective_from`, matched ones included: a rule matched fee line above the price gets a new `unmatched` `fee_above_contract` row superseding its match (`matched` is not terminal), a line now within the price is matched again, and a person's match with a reason is never reopened but counts as "accepted above contract". A receipt is corrected by a new receipt that supersedes it with a reason; nothing is edited. A cash out older than `settlement_bank_receipt_days` (default 5) with no receipt is listed on the Unmatched tab with `bank_receipt_awaited`. Feature 23 uses these receipts as close evidence.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option C in [rationale.md](rationale.md): both sources feed one carrier settlement account; the per delivery cash cycles from spec 0018 lead (one small statement per delivery and revision, superseded on a revision), Bosta's sheet adds the wallet's own rows and confirms the cash cycles, a delivery's cash cycle kinds live on one live statement only, rules match by tracking number, exact amounts, and the brand's rate card, the order's cash moves through spec 0013's `recordSettlement` from one pure target function that reads spec 0019's cash difference decisions, and the wallet and the bank are followed beside the order machine, never in it.

**Implementation skills**: `nestjs-best-practices` (`kadajett/agent-nestjs-skills`, `.claude/skills/nestjs-best-practices/`) · `drizzle-orm-patterns` (`giuseppe-trisciuoglio/developer-kit`, `.claude/skills/drizzle-orm-patterns/`) · `postgresql-table-design` (`wshobson/agents`, `.claude/skills/postgresql-table-design/`) · `pg-boss-v12` (`sncollective/snc-platform`, `.claude/skills/pg-boss-v12/`) · `nextjs-app-router-patterns` (`wshobson/agents`, `.claude/skills/nextjs-app-router-patterns/`) · `react-hook-form-zod` (`.claude/skills/react-hook-form-zod/`, the mapping and rate card forms) · `playwright-best-practices` (`currents-dev/playwright-best-practices-skill`, `.claude/skills/playwright-best-practices/`)

**Decided by the architect** (expertise, not owner questions; runner up in brackets):
- One statement per delivery and cash cycle revision, superseded on a revision, uses the built shapes exactly and lets a revision replace only that delivery's lines [one statement per cash cycle id: a revision of one delivery would have to supersede every delivery of the cycle, and whether one id groups a day is unverified].
- Dedupe between sources at the delivery level (tracking number) for the cash cycle kinds only, because a sheet may give only a total fee where the mirror gives a breakdown; wallet only rows add, deduped by content (review B1, the lead's ruling), except a fee row equal to the delivery's unitemized fee (review r2 N7) [per kind keys of spec 0002: count the fees twice when one side has no breakdown].
- Events are hints; every import reads the delivery back through `CarrierDeliveries` [apply the event payload: a late or replayed event would write stale amounts].
- The account is created by the system on first need [by a manager: one more step before any cash shows, and the partial unique index already keeps it single].
- `settlement_statements.received_at` is database time, stamped at insert, so no reader picks a "latest" statement by a writer's clock (security review S11; expand request below) [business time: the statement already has `covers_from`, `covers_to`, and `deposited_on` for that].
- The order's cash move is a direct `recordSettlement` call in the matching unit, as spec 0013 offers it, and `SettlementLineMatched` is published for readers [orders listening to the event, as the spec 0002 settlement child first wrote: the move would leave the matching transaction's control].
- Wallet balances and bank receipts are facts a person (or a sheet) records in their own small tables, not settlement lines, so the roll forward never invents a line [an opening balance as an `adjustment` line accounted `opening_balance`: a person's figure would sit among Bosta's facts and count in the statement totals].
- A rounding residue is kept as Bosta's own `bosta_unitemized` line, never folded into the VAT line (review m1, in part) [absorb up to 0.02 into VAT: it would change a figure Bosta gave].
- A rate card version is in force until the next current version starts, so the table needs no end date and stays append only; a correction is a superseding version [an `effective_to` column set once: a typo could not be corrected for the same days, review r2 N4].

### Data model

### Lines from a cash cycle (AC-2, AC-5)

From the delivery's cash cycle fields of spec 0018 (`carrier_deliveries`, parsed to 2 decimals by its mapper). Bosta's fees arrive as positive numbers, so each fee line is the fee negated; a fee Bosta gives back (a negative fee) is a positive line.

| Line | `line_kind` | `fee_code` | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| cash collected | `cod_collected` | | `cash_cycle_cod`, on a send that ended delivered or partly delivered (phase `delivered`) |
| FlexShip refusal fee collected at the door (spec 0019 domain review M9) | `other_fee` | `flex_ship_refusal` | + `cash_cycle_cod`, on a send or a return to origin that began as a send and did not end delivered: the cash the courier took from a customer who refused is a carrier charge, never order cash, so it never moves the order's cash state; compared with the `refusal_fee` charge leg (Assumed: that Bosta reports it in the cash cycle's `cod`; brand question 6) |
| shipping | `shipping_fee` | | − `cash_cycle_shipping_fees` |
| open package | `opening_package_fee` | | − `cash_cycle_opening_package_fees` |
| FlexShip | `other_fee` | `flex_ship` | − `cash_cycle_flex_ship_fees` |
| fees Bosta did not itemise | `other_fee` | `bosta_unitemized` | − (`cash_cycle_bosta_fees` − `cash_cycle_vat` − shipping − open package − FlexShip), only when it is 0.01 or more either way |
| VAT | `fee_vat` | | − `cash_cycle_vat` |
| a refund handed out, branch A | `customer_refund` | | on a customer return pickup with `cod` > 0 and `deposited_amt` = − `cod` − `bosta_fees`: − `cod` |
| a refund handed out, branch B | `customer_refund` | | on a customer return pickup with `cod` < 0 and `deposited_amt` = `cod` − `bosta_fees`: `cod` (already negative) |
| exchange cash | `cod_collected` | | on an exchange delivery linked to an exchange replacement (spec 0018 AC-33): `cod`, the replacement order's forward cash, matched by rule 1 like a send (the price difference, spec 0019 AC-34); on one not linked to a replacement: `cod`, `unmatched` `non_forward_cash` until a person links it |
| cash on any other type (review r2 m2) | `cod_collected` | | on a cash collection (Bosta type 15) or any other type spec 0018 maps to `cash_collection` or `other`, with `cod` not zero: `cod`, `unmatched` `non_forward_cash` |

On a customer return pickup a `cod` above 0 is always a refund Bosta paid out (spec 0019's second pass): it is branch A, and when Bosta's `deposited_amt` is `cod` − `bosta_fees` instead of − `cod` − `bosta_fees`, the signed sum fails AC-5 and a person looks at it. Worked: `cod` 300.00, shipping 45.00, VAT 6.30, `deposited_amt` −351.30 is branch A (lines −300.00, −45.00, −6.30, sum −351.30, AC-5 passes); `cod` −300.00 with the same deposit is branch B (the same lines); `cod` 300.00 with `deposited_amt` 248.70 writes branch A's lines and fails AC-5 (`cash_cycle_arithmetic_differs`). Bosta gives no separate COD fee in the cash cycle (E2); `cod_fee` lines come only from a sheet that has one, and the rate card's COD percent is checked on the shipping line. A returned to origin parcel gives fee lines only and a negative statement total.

### Lines from a sheet (AC-6 to AC-8)

- `per_delivery`: one row gives the same lines as the table above from its mapped columns (COD, shipping, open package, FlexShip, other fee, VAT), or, when the mapping names only a total fees column, one `other_fee` line with `fee_code` `bosta_fees_total`; these are all `cash_cycle_part`. A deposited amount column is checked like AC-5 (`cash_cycle_arithmetic_differs`); a balance column records a wallet balance observation (AC-31).
- `per_transaction`: each row is one line of the kind its type value maps to. The recommended starting map from Bosta's documented wallet types (E3), each with its family: Compensation to `compensation` (`wallet_only`), Cash out to `deposit` negated, money that left the wallet (`wallet_only`), Packing Material Fees to `other_fee` `packing_material` (`wallet_only`), Insurance fee to `other_fee` `insurance` (`wallet_only`, deduplicated against an equal `bosta_unitemized` line of its delivery, AC-9), Recharge Balance to `adjustment` positive (`wallet_only`), the wallet's "refunds" credit to `adjustment` positive with the type text in `partner_text`, never `customer_refund` (`wallet_only`, review m4; brand question 11), Collected Cash Cycle and Bosta Fees to `evidence_only` (stored as day evidence, AC-33, never lines).
- `external_line_ref`: the transaction id when mapped, else the row number; `occurred_on` the row's deposit date; `partner_text` the mapped description, cut at 500 characters.

### Matching rules (AC-10, AC-11)

Applied in order to a line that is not settled; the first that decides writes its row.

| # | Applies to | Rule | Result and code |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a line of a statement whose arithmetic failed | AC-5 | `unmatched` `cash_cycle_arithmetic_differs` |
| 2 | a cash cycle kind line of a delivery with an open source difference | AC-9 | `unmatched` `sources_disagree` |
| 3 | a cash cycle kind line whose delivery Bosta created before the account's opening cutoff and is not linked to an order | AC-35 | `accounted` `pre_go_live`, rule `pre_go_live_accounted` |
| 4 | any line with a tracking number | delivery through `CarrierDeliveries.byTrackingNumbers({ connectionId })` | none: `unmatched` `no_order_for_tracking`; not linked: `delivery_not_linked`; no store: `store_unknown` |
| 5 | `cod_collected` on an order whose cash is `prepaid`, or `nothing_to_collect` with fulfillment delivered | AC-38 | with spec 0019's resolution `over_collection_refund_customer` or `over_collection_belongs_to_brand`: `matched`, rule `collected_with_nothing_due_resolved`; else `unmatched` `collected_with_nothing_due` |
| 6 | `cod_collected` | order cash `unpaid` or `nothing_to_collect` | `unmatched` `collection_not_recorded` |
| 7 | `cod_collected` on a send | amount equals the order's `cod_collected_amount` (a partial's collection, recorded at 45 while spec 0019 holds the delivered move, matches here too) | `matched`, rule `cod_equals_collected`; else `unmatched` `amount_differs` |
| 8 | `cod_collected` on another type | the derivation table | `unmatched` `non_forward_cash` |
| 9 | `shipping_fee`, `opening_package_fee`, `other_fee` (the refusal fee included), `fee_vat`, `cod_fee` with a tracking number, on a delivery of any type | the delivery's order | `matched`, rule `fee_by_tracking`; never compared with `cod_collected_amount` and never a cash move |
| 10 | the same fee kinds, after rule 9 and on every rate card save | the rate card of AC-30, when a version is in force | above the price: `unmatched` `fee_above_contract`, superseding a rule match |
| 11 | `customer_refund` | AC-13 | `matched` with `refund_id`, rule `refund_carrier_cash`; else `unmatched` `refund_record_missing` |
| 12 | `compensation` | AC-14 | `matched`, rule `claim_by_tracking` or `claim_by_amount`; else `no_claim_for_compensation` or `claims_ambiguous` |
| 13 | a line with no tracking number and a business reference | `OrderQueries.idByNumber(storeId, number)` after the connector's parse (`#1001`, `1001`, `<prefix>:#1001`), in the account's store or uniquely across the tenant's stores | fee kinds go to rules 9 and 10; `cod_collected` to rules 5 to 7; several orders: `unmatched` `order_reference_ambiguous` |
| 14 | `deposit` | always | `accounted` `carrier_deposit`, rule `deposit_accounted`; its bank state is AC-32 |
| 15 | `other_fee` `packing_material` or `insurance` with no tracking number | always | `accounted` `supplies` or `carrier_insurance`, rule `wallet_fee_accounted` |
| 16 | anything else | none | `unmatched` `no_order_for_line`, for a person to match or account (`carrier_compensation`, `carrier_deposit`, `supplies`, `carrier_insurance`, `bank_charge`, `opening_balance`, `pre_go_live`, `carrier_fee_unallocated`, `carrier_balance_recharge`, `carrier_fee_refund`) |

Rule 10 runs after rule 9 in the same pass: a fee line is first matched by tracking, then checked, so the history shows both. Match states are the built four: `matched`, `accounted` (terminal), `unmatched` (always with a reason), `cleared` (only for a superseded statement; terminal). The built reason codes stay (`not_yet_matched`, `no_order_for_tracking`, `amount_differs`, `store_unknown`, `unmapped_product`, `duplicate_line`); this feature adds `delivery_not_linked`, `collection_not_recorded`, `collected_with_nothing_due`, `cash_cycle_arithmetic_differs`, `sources_disagree`, `fee_above_contract`, `refund_record_missing`, `no_claim_for_compensation`, `claims_ambiguous`, `non_forward_cash`, `order_reference_ambiguous`, `no_order_for_line`; the items of the Unmatched tab add `cash_cycle_awaited`, `compensation_awaited`, `wallet_balance_differs`, `bank_receipt_awaited`. `duplicate_line` stays unused: a duplicate is never written (AC-9). Each reason has a next step sentence in the catalog (AC-26); `non_forward_cash` says to link an exchange delivery to its replacement order (spec 0019 AC-34), or, for any other type, names feature 34.

### The order's cash target (AC-16 to AC-19, AC-38)

`carrierCashTarget({ cashState, codCollected, codDue, remitted, lines, charges, resolution, claimCompensation, openDisagreement, feesAboveContract })` is pure and answers the state the order should be in (`collected_by_carrier`, `remitted`, `reconciled`, or `unchanged` for an order outside the carrier path) and the list of blockers with their labels and links, all taken from the resolution. `resolution` is the order's entry of `CashDifferences.resolutionOf` (spec 0019 AC-32); `claimCompensation` is the compensation spec 0019 recorded on the order's `short_collection` claim (`CarrierClaims.forOrders`). `order-cash-sync.ts` reads the order through `OrderQueries.summaryOf`, the order's live lines and heads, `DeliveryCharges.forOrders`, and the resolution, then moves the order along the shortest legal path of spec 0013's table (stepping back first when needed), one `recordSettlement` call per step, inside the same `OrderTransactions.run` unit as the match rows. Concurrency: the unit takes the order's row lock through orders (spec 0013's locked order) and the line's chain is serialized by the built unique `supersedes_match_id`, so two matchers cannot both win. The wallet and bank states of AC-31 and AC-32 are never inputs of this function.

### Key invariants

- The settlement module is the only writer of the settlement tables and of every table this feature adds; the order's cash changes only through spec 0013's calls.
- A delivery's cash cycle kinds are on at most one live statement of an account; a duplicate by content is compared, never written; wallet only rows add, except a fee row equal to the delivery's unitemized fee (AC-9).
- Lines are never edited or deleted; a correction is a superseding statement, and its predecessors end `cleared`.
- Every unmatched line has a reason; every accounted line has an account code (the built checks).
- `remitted` means credited in Bosta's wallet and needs a matched COD line with a `deposited_on`; `reconciled` needs `cod_collected_amount` equal to `cod_due_amount`, never `cod_expected_amount`, or one of spec 0019's resolutions of AC-17; settlement never writes off or claims a cash difference itself.
- The wallet and bank states never move the order's cash state.
- Every money figure shown or answered by this feature carries `provisional` until feature 23 closes its period; an unknown is never shown as 0.
- No import reads a value from the event payload; every amount is read back from the mirror or the file.

### Security model

- Hats: operations and manager hold `settlement.work` and `settlement.close` (`packages/contracts/src/access.ts`); the settlement only hat of Q-22.11 is requested of spec 0008; no other hat sees `/settlement`.
- Personal data (Law 151): Bosta's sheet may hold receiver names, phones, and addresses; the file stays in the brand's object storage in Egypt (ADR 0002), only mapped columns are read, the preview answers only mapped values, and download is audited. `partner_text` and `payment_reference` are already on the redaction list; this feature adds `fileHeader`, `sampleRows`, and `bankReference` to `packages/config/src/redaction.ts`. The CSV export guards text cells against formulas (AC-34).
- Audit (spec 0010 registry, both labels): `settlement.file_imported`, `settlement.file_downloaded`, `settlement.file_mapping_saved`, `settlement.file_mapping_retired`, `settlement.line_matched_by_person`, `settlement.line_accounted`, `settlement.line_unmatched_by_person`, `settlement.lines_bulk_actioned`, `settlement.compensation_claim_picked`, `settlement.period_created`, `settlement.period_exported`, `settlement.rate_card_saved`, `settlement.rate_card_corrected`, `settlement.wallet_balance_recorded`, `settlement.cash_out_received`, `settlement.cash_out_difference_accepted`, `settlement.opening_cutoff_changed`, `settlement.open_list_downloaded`. Record types `settlement_line`, `settlement_statement`, `settlement_period`, `settlement_account`.
- Rule actions write no audit entry; their match rows (`matched_by_type` `rule`, `rule_code`) are the record.

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- The month's Bosta money is matched as it arrives; the accountant works only the lines and items that say why they do not match, each with its next step, in bulk where it makes sense.
- Three leaks the reference brand could not see are now visible: fees above the contract (even for a month imported before the card), a cash out short of what reached the bank, and compensation that never came; "owed by Bosta" counts every parcel's cash Bosta held at the period's end.
- One decision per cash difference: spec 0019 decides, settlement reconciles from it.
- Every order ends in a final cash state or shows what stops it, which is what the wind down and the daily report need.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- Thousands of small statements a month (one per delivery and revision); the Statements tab groups them by deposit day.
- The rate card, wallet balances, and bank receipts are typed in by a person; a brand that never records them gets "not checked" and "not recorded" rows, never a false agreement. A card saved late rewrites many lines' states (each a new match row), which the bulk action then works.
- Six new tables and three changes to built tables, requested of spec 0002 as expand only.
- Several facts stay unverified (the meaning of `deposited_at`, how a refund on a return pickup and the FlexShip refusal fee show, whether insurance sits inside Bosta's fees, the sheet's columns); the brand questions settle them.
- The refund rule depends on the credit slice of Q-22.8, and the cash difference, claim, charge, and owed figures on spec 0019's revision.

**Neutral**:
- This feature is the first to store files, so object storage configuration lands with it.
- Period close is deliberately absent: every figure is provisional until feature 23.
