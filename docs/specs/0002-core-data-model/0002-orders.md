# 0002 child: stores, channels, and orders with two state machines

Part of the [2026-11-05 amendment of spec 0002](index.md#amendment-2026-11-05). Satisfies **AC-18** to **AC-23** and **AC-35**. Sources: final plan sections 6.1, 6.14, 7.1, 7.2 (`docs/positioning/2026-11-05-egypt-order-operations-layer.md`); spec 0007 section 1 (connections, stores, outbound commands) and section 7 (order number per store); carried B1 and B2; the domain review `docs/reviews/domain-data-model.md` (B1, B2, M1, M3 to M5, M9, m1 to m4, m6, m7, m10, m11) and the gate review's fixes 1 to 5.

## Summary

An order belongs to one store and one channel, carries where it came from (campaign, ad set, ad, discount code, media buyer, influencer), and has a type: a sale, an exchange replacement, or a gift. It moves through two separate state machines, one for the parcel (fulfillment) and one for the money (cash), and every move is an append only event. A partial delivery splits a line into a delivered row and a returned row with exact money, and the amount the courier should collect drops with it. One pure function derives the order's outcome (kept, cancelled before shipping, failed delivery, refunded, partially refunded, open) from those states, so every report reads the same answer.

**Built here versus later.** This feature migrates the tables marked ★ with every check, index, and trigger, and builds the pure pieces in `packages/contracts/src/orders/`: `order-states.ts` (both transition tables, `nextState` which raises `InvalidTransition`, and `replayStates` which rebuilds both states from an order's events), `order-outcome.ts` (`deriveOrderOutcome`), `line-split.ts` (the split amounts), and `order-amounts.ts` (the allocation of prepaid and credit to lines and shipping, and `codDue`). `OrdersService`, which applies them inside transactions and writes `order_events`, is feature 16; the carrier side is feature 19 and the delivery failures feature 21. Test scenarios tagged with a feature number are proven by that feature's tests.

### Money on the order: allocation, expected, and due

- **Allocation (B1).** `prepaid_amount` and `credit_applied_amount` are spread over the order's non cancelled lines and its shipping, by gross share, the way discounts are: each line (by `position`) takes `round(amount × line gross / total_amount, 2)` half away from zero, and shipping takes the rest. The line shares are `order_lines.prepaid_amount` and `order_lines.credit_applied_amount`; the shipping shares are `orders.shipping_prepaid_amount` and `orders.shipping_credit_applied_amount`. *Cent boundary (clarified at the build, 2026-11-06):* plain rounding can break a check, for example three 100.00 lines with free shipping and 100.00 credit leave 0.01 on a shipping charge of 0. So prepaid is spread first, then credit; each share is capped at its row's room (gross, or shipping charged, minus the shares already given there), and any rest beyond shipping's room goes back to the lines in reverse position order, each up to its room (`allocatePayments` in `order-amounts.ts`). They are recomputed on every edit until the carrier delivery exists, then frozen. A return credit later uses them to know how much of a line was paid in cash, by card, or with credit ([credit ledger](0002-customers-credit-tasks.md#customer-credit-ledger)).
- **`cod_expected_amount`** is what the courier was told to collect: `total_amount − prepaid_amount − credit_applied_amount`, recomputed on every edit until `carrier_delivery_created_at` is set, then frozen.
- **`cod_due_amount` (B2)** is what the customer owes for what she kept: the sum over lines in `open`, `delivered`, or `returned` of `gross_amount − prepaid_amount − credit_applied_amount`, plus the whole `shipping_charged_amount − shipping_prepaid_amount − shipping_credit_applied_amount` while at least one such line remains, never below 0. Before delivery it equals `cod_expected_amount`; a partial delivery lowers it by the returned rows only, because shipping is charged in full on any partial delivery (owner answer, 2026-11-05); a full return to origin or a lost parcel takes it to 0. A refusal moves no line in the built model, so the due amount stays full until the carrier reports the parcel back (Follow-up, feature 19). The credit applied to lines that come back is returned to the customer's balance by the credit module on the `LinesReturnedToOrigin` event, as a `consumption_release` entry (feature 21 and 28); a release never counts in `returned_credit_amount`, so a credit paid order that was partially delivered still derives `kept`.
- **Reconciliation compares `cod_collected_amount` with `cod_due_amount`**, never with `cod_expected_amount` (gate review fix 5; feature 22 builds the comparison, feature 24 the "cash expected versus remitted" figure). A difference is a real shortfall and opens a task.

> Amended 2026-11-07 by spec 0013 (domain review D3, the cancel half): a whole order cancellation leaves its lines `open` and every total as it was, and `codDue` also reads the fulfillment state and returns 0 for a `cancelled` order, so cash `unpaid` to `nothing_to_collect` fires in the cancel's own transaction. Removing one piece still cancels that line. The refused half stays with feature 19.

## Fulfillment state machine

Values: `new`, `confirmed`, `packed`, `shipped`, `delivered`, `partially_delivered`, `failed_attempt`, `returned_to_origin`, `exchanged`, `refused`, plus `cancelled` and `lost`. `cancelled` is added to the final plan's list because an order cancelled at confirmation (cancelled with reason, duplicate, fake) must leave the queue in a named state. `lost` is added because a parcel the carrier lost never comes back: as `returned_to_origin` the warehouse would wait for it and the returned to origin rate would rise, and the reference brand reports "lost" as its own delivery outcome (domain review M3).

| From | To | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| `new` | `confirmed` | person | the confirmation outcome "confirmed" (feature 18) |
| `new`, `confirmed`, `packed` | `cancelled` | person | reason code required; refused once `carrier_delivery_created_at` is set |
| `confirmed` | `packed` | person | refused while `write_back_state <> 'none'` or while the connectors service reports an open command on the order (spec 0007 section 1, blocking rule) |
| `packed` | `confirmed` | person | an edit after packing, before the carrier delivery exists |
| `new`, `confirmed`, `packed` | `shipped` | carrier | the carrier reports pickup. A carrier report is a fact and is never refused. From `new` it opens a task "shipped without confirmation"; with `write_back_state <> 'none'` it opens a task "shipped before the edit synced" (mirror mode, where the carrier's own app creates deliveries) |
| `cancelled` | `shipped` | carrier | a fact that contradicts the cancellation; always opens a task |
| `shipped`, `failed_attempt` | `failed_attempt` | carrier | each failed attempt is its own event (the attempt count is the number of these events) |
| `shipped`, `failed_attempt` | `delivered` | carrier | every `open` line becomes `delivered` |
| `shipped`, `failed_attempt` | `partially_delivered` | carrier or person | requires the line split below; at least one line `delivered` and one `returned_to_origin` |
| `shipped`, `failed_attempt` | `refused` | carrier | refused at the door, with `refused_reason_code` (`size`, `changed_mind`, `price`, `not_ordered`, `damaged`, `other`) |
| `shipped`, `failed_attempt`, `refused` | `returned_to_origin` | carrier | the parcel is back with the brand; every `open` line becomes `returned_to_origin` |
| `shipped`, `failed_attempt`, `refused` | `lost` | carrier or person | the carrier reports the parcel lost (Bosta state 48 or its lost labels, mapped in feature 19), or a person records it with a reason; every `open` line becomes `lost` |
| `delivered`, `partially_delivered` | `exchanged` | system_job | an exchange on this order completed (feature 34) |

> Amended 2026-11-07 by spec 0013: the `cancelled` row also accepts `system_job` with a reason code, for a cancellation that arrives from the channel (feature 13 maps the channel's reason to a code starting `channel.`); like a person's, it is refused once `carrier_delivery_created_at` is set.

Final states: `delivered` and `partially_delivered` (except to `exchanged`), `returned_to_origin`, `lost`, `exchanged`, `cancelled` (except the carrier fact). Any other pair raises `InvalidTransition` with both states.

- **A refund only return after delivery has no fulfillment transition** (gate review fix 4): the returned lines become `returned`, the outcome becomes `refunded` or `partially_refunded` through the credit projections, and `fulfillment_state` stays `delivered` (or `partially_delivered`). `exchanged` is the only post delivery fulfillment state; feature 34 must not invent a `returned` state.
- **A door refusal with a size request** ("refuse the M, take the L", domain review M4) is `refused` with `refused_reason_code = 'size'`; the replacement is an `exchange_replacement` order with `original_order_id` and `exchange_id` like any exchange (feature 21 opens the exchange from the refusal). The original's outcome stays `failed_delivery`; the metric dictionary (feature 24) classes a size refusal linked to a replacement as an exchange, not a returned to origin failure.
- **A lost parcel's stock** already left the ledger when the shipment posted (`order_shipment` at `shipped`), so `lost` posts no stock movement; the accounting export (feature 45) classes that shipment's cost as a loss by the order's state, and any carrier compensation arrives as a settlement line of kind `compensation` ([settlement child](0002-settlement.md)).
- **Imports** (feature 15, and an importer from a brand's own console) set the first state as found with one event `from_state = null`, `actor_type = 'import'`; they never replay a path. Historical refunds come in as credit entries posted by the importer (domain review M9, [credit ledger](0002-customers-credit-tasks.md#customer-credit-ledger)), so an imported refunded order derives `refunded` like a live one.

## Cash state machine

Values: `unpaid`, `collected_by_carrier`, `remitted`, `reconciled`, plus `prepaid` and `nothing_to_collect`. The final plan names the first four; the other two are added because a gateway paid order and a gifted, fully credited, or never delivered order have nothing for a carrier to collect, and the license wind down needs a final cash state for each order (spec 0007, *Switching off on lapse*). The carrier's COD fee and shipping fee are never netted into these amounts: they are settlement lines of their own ([settlement child](0002-settlement.md)).

**First state**, set when the order is created: `nothing_to_collect` if `cod_expected_amount = 0` and `prepaid_amount = 0`; `prepaid` if `cod_expected_amount = 0` and `prepaid_amount > 0`; otherwise `unpaid`.

| From | To | Who | Rule and amounts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `unpaid` | `collected_by_carrier` | carrier, or person with a reason | the carrier reports COD collected, or a person enters the amount from the courier's note when the carrier does not report it (final plan open question 2's fallback); `cod_collected_amount` = that amount |
| `collected_by_carrier` | `collected_by_carrier` | carrier, or person with a reason | a further collection or a corrected amount; one event per change, `amount` = the new total |
| `collected_by_carrier` | `remitted` | system_job | a matched settlement line deposits this order's cash and its statement carries a deposit date (`deposited_on`); `remitted_amount` from the matched lines |
| `remitted` | `reconciled` | system_job | every settlement line of the order is matched or accounted (fees and refunds included) |
| `unpaid` | `nothing_to_collect` | system_job | fulfillment reaches `cancelled`, `returned_to_origin`, `refused`, or `lost` with `cod_due_amount = 0` and nothing collected |
| `nothing_to_collect` | `collected_by_carrier` | carrier | a late carrier report of cash on an order thought uncollected; opens a task |
| `prepaid` | `reconciled` | system_job or person | the gateway payout is matched (gateway settlement is later; until then `prepaid` is final, decided default 9) |
| `reconciled` | `remitted` | system_job | a match or a period close was revoked (reason required) |
| `remitted` | `collected_by_carrier` | system_job | a deposit match was revoked (reason required) |
| `collected_by_carrier` | `unpaid` | carrier, or person with a reason | the carrier reversed a collection |

Final cash states: `reconciled`, `nothing_to_collect`, and `prepaid` until gateway settlement exists.

## Partial delivery splits lines

When a carrier or a person records that `d` of a line's `q` units were delivered and `r = q − d` came back (`0 < d < q`), the orders service, in one transaction: inserts a new line with `quantity = r`, the same `variant_id`, `unit_price`, `tax_rate`, `title`, `channel_line_id`, and `unit_cost`, `split_from_line_id` = the original, `line_state = 'returned_to_origin'`, and money: `gross_amount`, `net_amount`, `discount_amount`, `prepaid_amount`, and `credit_applied_amount` each = `round(original × r / q, 2)` half away from zero (computed in exact `numeric`, the original spec's rounding rule), and `tax_amount` = the new gross minus the new net; then updates the original line to `quantity = d`, `line_state = 'delivered'`, and each money column to `original − new`. *Cent boundary (clarified at the build, 2026-11-06):* the new row's `prepaid_amount` and `credit_applied_amount` move by the least amount (prepaid held first, then credit clamped) that keeps `prepaid_amount + credit_applied_amount <= gross_amount` on both rows, for example a line of 3 at gross 1.00 with 0.50 prepaid and 0.50 credit returns 1 unit as gross 0.33, prepaid 0.17, credit 0.16 (`splitLine` in `line-split.ts`). The two rows always sum to the original, so `lines_gross_amount` and `total_amount` stay unchanged by a split, and the check `gross_amount = net_amount + tax_amount` holds on both rows; `cod_due_amount` drops by the returned row (above). A line with `quantity = 1` cannot split; it is delivered or returned whole. The before and after go to the audit trail (feature 10). The returned row then follows the returned to origin path (restock by scan, feature 21); a returned to origin restock and a customer return restock post at the line's frozen `unit_cost`, the shipment's own unit cost, never 0 ([catalog child](0002-catalog-locations.md#stock-ledger-changes)); the cash side records the partial amount on `cod_collected_amount`.

## The derived outcome

One pure function, `deriveOrderOutcome`, in `packages/contracts/src/orders/order-outcome.ts`, is the only place the outcome is computed. The orders service stores its result on `orders.outcome` in the same transaction as any change to its inputs; nothing sets the column directly.

Inputs: `fulfillment_state`, `delivered_amount`, and `refunded_amount = max(0, returned_credit_amount − exchange_consumed_amount)`.
- **`delivered_amount` (domain review M1)** is the sum of `gross_amount` over lines delivered at the door: `delivered` plus `returned` (a line returned after delivery was still delivered and paid for), never `returned_to_origin`, `lost`, or `cancelled`. So returning one of two delivered pieces leaves the denominator at both.
- `returned_credit_amount` is the return credits posted on this order's lines and `exchange_consumed_amount` is the credit consumed (`order_consumption`, net of any `consumption_release`) by replacement orders whose `original_order_id` is this order (both projections from the credit ledger). So a refund, a payout, or credit kept as store credit counts as refunded, while credit spent on this order's own exchange does not.

| `fulfillment_state` | Condition | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `cancelled` | | `cancelled_before_shipping` |
| `new`, `confirmed`, `packed`, `shipped`, `failed_attempt` | | `open` |
| `refused`, `returned_to_origin`, `lost` | | `failed_delivery` |
| `delivered`, `partially_delivered`, `exchanged` | `refunded_amount = 0` | `kept` |
| `delivered`, `partially_delivered`, `exchanged` | `0 < refunded_amount < delivered_amount` | `partially_refunded` |
| `delivered`, `partially_delivered`, `exchanged` | `refunded_amount >= delivered_amount` | `refunded` |

So a partial delivery with nothing refunded is `kept` (the returned rows show in line states and in the returned to origin rate), and an exchange whose replacement cost at least the returned credit is `kept`; both are decided defaults 1 and 2. A lost parcel is `failed_delivery` with `fulfillment_state = 'lost'` visible to the metric dictionary. The function is total over the enum (an exhaustive `switch`, AGENTS.md).

## Order types and gifted orders

- **`sale`**: the default.
- **`exchange_replacement`**: created by the exchange flow (feature 34), or from a door refusal with a size request (feature 21), with `original_order_id` and `exchange_id`. Its lines are priced normally. `credit_applied_amount` is the credit it consumed ([credit ledger](0002-customers-credit-tasks.md#customer-credit-ledger)); `cod_expected_amount` is the balance due the courier collects. When the brand's policy posts credit only after inspection, the replacement is created after the credit posts, otherwise its COD is the full price. Metric dictionary rules (feature 24) decide how its revenue counts; the model only marks it.
- **`gifted`**: `total_amount = 0` (check above: line gross and shipping both 0), so the first cash state is `nothing_to_collect` and no COD can be asked for shipping (gate review fix 3). A line may keep its retail `unit_price` with an equal discount, so marketing can report the retail value given away (domain review m6). `payment_method = 'none'`, a `gift_reason_code` is required (for example influencer, giveaway, staff). Excluded from revenue and from return rates by its type (feature 24 filters on it). Its stock leaves through a normal `order_shipment` posting at ledger cost, and its carrier fees arrive as normal settlement lines; the export rules matrix (feature 45) and reports class both as marketing cost because the order's type is `gifted`. How a Shopify order becomes `gifted` (a tag or a discount code) is feature 13's decision. Who may create one: the manager hat (decided default 8, feature 8).

## Edits until the carrier delivery exists, and outbound sync (B2)

An order is editable (address, added line, a variant change as cancel plus add) until **a carrier delivery exists for it**: `carrier_delivery_created_at` is set by the first carrier fact of any state about the order (pickup requested included), in driver mode and in mirror mode alike, where the carrier's own Shopify app creates the delivery (domain review M5). Each edit writes an audit entry (feature 10) and, for a Shopify order, an `outbound_commands` row in the same transaction. **`outbound_commands.sync_state` is the single truth** for whether a write back landed. `orders.write_back_state` is the orders module's projection of its open commands (`failed` if any is failed, else `waiting` if any is waiting, else `none`), updated in the command writer's transaction through domain events (A6 in the [tenancy child](0002-tenancy-platform.md#projections-and-domain-events-a6-decided-here-built-by-the-first-feature-that-needs-it)), so the confirmation queue and the pick list can filter on it with an index and show "edit not synced, hold" (carried n1). The `packed` transition does not trust the projection: it asks `ConnectorsService.openCommandsFor('order', id)` inside its transaction. A failed command opens a task through `OutboundCommandFailed` (feature 17). A carrier delivery that appears while a write back is open opens a task (transition table), and feature 19 opens a task when a delivery's own COD differs from the order's `cod_expected_amount` (what the courier was told, frozen when the delivery exists), never from `cod_due_amount`, which drops on a partial delivery and would raise a false task.

## Critical test scenarios

- Two stores both import order `#1001`: both stored; a second `#1001` in one store fails on the unique key; a Shopify connection without a store fails its check; a second Shopify connection on one store fails; a Bosta connection with and without a store both insert; a `gateway` connection inserts, verifies **AC-18**.
- `nextState` accepts every allowed fulfillment and cash pair for its actor and raises `InvalidTransition` for every other pair (an exhaustive test over the enum product, including `delivered` to `new`, a person moving `cancelled` to `shipped`, `lost` to `delivered`, and `reconciled` to `unpaid`); `replayStates` over a recorded event list gives the last states; `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on `order_events` fail; `refused` without a reason fails its check, verifies **AC-19**, **AC-20**, **AC-33**.
- `nextState` refuses `confirmed` to `packed` when the input says a write back is open, and reports a task for a carrier `shipped` while a write back is open; `outbound_commands` rejects `resolved` without a resolution, verifies **AC-19**, **AC-35**.
- (feature 16) Every transition writes exactly one `order_events` row in the same transaction as the state change, and a replay of the stored events equals the stored states, verifies **AC-19**, **AC-20**.
- Partial delivery: a line of 3 at gross 1,000.00 with discount 100.00 splits into 2 delivered and 1 returned (returned row gross 333.33, discount 33.33; original 666.67, 66.67), `lines_gross_amount` and `total_amount` unchanged; on a 2,400.00 order of three pieces with 50.00 shipping, all COD, one piece back: `cod_expected_amount` stays 2,450.00, `cod_due_amount` becomes 1,650.00, and a collection of 1,650.00 reconciles with no shortfall; a line of 1 cannot split, verifies **AC-21**.
- Allocation: a 1,000.00 order (two lines of 600.00 and 350.00, shipping 50.00) with 300.00 credit applied gives line shares 180.00 and 105.00 and shipping 15.00; a cancelled line leaves `lines_gross_amount` and the allocation, verifies **AC-21**, **AC-24**.
- Outcome: one case per table row, plus an order of two delivered 500 pieces with one returned and refunded (`partially_refunded`, delivered amount still 1,000), an 800 to 500 exchange (refunded 300 of 800, `partially_refunded`), a 500 to 800 exchange (`kept`), and a refund only return that leaves `fulfillment_state = 'delivered'` with outcome `refunded`, verifies **AC-22**.
- Gifted: a gifted order with a line gross above 0 or a shipping charge fails the check; a gifted line with `unit_price` 500.00 and discount 500.00 passes; it starts `nothing_to_collect`; an exchange replacement without `original_order_id` fails, verifies **AC-23**.
