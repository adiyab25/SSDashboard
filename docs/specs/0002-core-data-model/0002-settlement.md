# 0002 child: the settlement primitive

Part of the [2026-11-05 amendment of spec 0002](index.md#amendment-2026-11-05). Satisfies **AC-25**. Sources: final plan sections 6.2, 6.13, 7.4, 7.5 (`docs/positioning/2026-11-05-egypt-order-operations-layer.md`); spec 0007 section 1 (carrier accounts, the store carried onto every settlement line) and section 3 (third party money confirmed only after period close); reference brand notes section 2 (confirmed ranges, revoked with a reason, never deleted); the domain review `docs/reviews/domain-data-model.md` (B3, B4, M3, M6 to M8, m12).

## Summary

One mechanism settles money with every third party that holds some of it: the carrier (Bosta remits cash minus fees and refunds), a consignment partner (owes for what sold, is credited for returns and defective pieces, charged for missing pieces), and later a payment gateway. Each settlement account has periods, external statements, and lines. A line is matched to an order, or accounted to an account code when it has no order (a payment, a deposit, a compensation); anything unmatched carries a reason. A line belongs to the period whose dates hold it, so lines imported before the period existed still count. A period is closed with evidence and can be revoked with a reason, and nothing is ever deleted.

**Built here versus later.** This feature migrates the six tables with every check, exclusion, and trigger, and builds two pure functions in `packages/contracts/src/settlement/`: `lineMatchState` (a line's state from its match rows) and `settlementPeriodStatus` (a period's status from its events). Importing, matching, and the reconciliation screen are feature 22 (carrier) and 43 (partner); partner ingestion, day coverage, and counts are features 42 and 43; period close with evidence is feature 23. Test scenarios tagged with a feature number are proven by that feature's tests.

## Rules

- **Lines are the third party's facts.** They are stored as the statement gives them and never edited. A corrected statement is a new statement with `supersedes_statement_id`; its lines may reuse the old line numbers (the dedupe key is per statement), and the superseded statement's lines are cleared by `cleared` match rows with the reason `superseded`. Within one account, the importer refuses a line that repeats the same facts on another live statement whose coverage overlaps (for a carrier: the same tracking number, line kind, and cash cycle; for a partner: the same partner order reference, code, and day) with `DuplicateLine` (features 22 and 42).
- **A line's period is derived, never stored** (domain review B3). A line belongs to the period of its account whose range holds its `occurred_on`; the exclusion constraint makes that period unique, and the index (`account_id`, `occurred_on`) finds it. A line imported before its period exists simply joins it when the accountant creates it. Creating or widening a period can never reach into a closed period (the exclusion constraint), and a line whose `occurred_on` falls in a closed period is refused at import without an admin's written reason (feature 23).
- **`occurred_on`** is, for carrier lines, the deposit date of the cash cycle the line belongs to (so a carrier period holds what the carrier actually remitted in that range and agrees with the bank); carrier lines are imported only from a cash cycle that has a deposit date, or from a statement file row's own date. For partner lines it is the sale or return day the partner reports.
- **Matching is history.** A line's current state is its latest `settlement_line_matches` row, the head of its supersede chain (the row no other row supersedes; not the latest time, since concurrent transactions can commit out of order) (a line with none is `unmatched`, reason `not_yet_matched`). Matching again, accounting, unmatching, or clearing adds a row that supersedes the previous one; nothing is updated. Rules match exact evidence (for example tracking number and amount) and record `rule_code`; a person matches the rest. Reason codes for unmatched lines start with `not_yet_matched`, `no_order_for_tracking`, `amount_differs`, `store_unknown`, `unmapped_product`, `duplicate_line`; features 22 and 43 may add codes.
- **Lines with no order are accounted, not stuck** (domain review B4). A `partner_payment`, a `deposit`, a `compensation` with no delivery, a bank charge, or an `adjustment` gets a terminal `accounted` row with an `account_code`, and the refund or payment document it settles when there is one, by a rule or a person. `cleared` is only for lines of a superseded statement.
- **The store on a carrier line** (B1): from the carrier account's store binding when it has one; otherwise from the delivery the tracking number names, read through the orders module's exported service, never its table (its `store_id`, which feature 19 sets from the business reference or the Shopify store name, spec 0007 section 1). A carrier line whose store cannot be found is `unmatched` with `store_unknown`.
- **A count is evidence, not money** (domain review M7). A statement of kind `count` records what was counted at the partner location: the count posts `count` movements through the ledger and produces a comparison with the partner's statement, never payable lines, when the partner's own statement exists for the period. Only when the partner reports nothing for a period do the count's derived sales become that period's payable `consignment_sale` lines and drive the `consignment_sale` stock postings, so a partner is never charged, and stock never leaves, twice. Differences between a count and a statement become `missing_piece_charge` lines or tasks on the period. Features 42 and 43 build it.
- **Effects on orders** go through events: a match on a `cod_collected` line, or a deposit on a statement with a `deposited_on` date, emits `SettlementLineMatched` with the order and amount; the orders module moves its cash state ([orders child](0002-orders.md#cash-state-machine)), and compares collected cash with the order's `cod_due_amount`, never with what the delivery was created with. A revoked match emits `SettlementMatchRevoked` and the order steps back.
- **Period close.** A period closes when every line whose `occurred_on` falls in it has a current (head) match row of `matched`, `accounted`, or `cleared`, and its evidence agrees: the statement total (or the partner's own unit total) equal to matched plus accounted (feature 23 sets the evidence rules). So a partner period closes on the partner's payment, as final plan 7.4 says. Closing writes a `closed` event and sets `status = 'closed'` in the same transaction; a revoke writes a `revoked` event with a reason and sets `open`. The period row is never deleted, and `status` is always the latest event's result (a test rebuilds it from the events).
- **Database guards added at the build** (2026-11-06, [security-data-model-m4](../../reviews/security-data-model-m4.md) MJ2, m4, m5; [review-data-model-m4](../../reviews/review-data-model-m4.md) MJ2): `settlement_periods.status` follows its events (inserting a `closed` or `revoked` event sets it; any other change of `status` is refused), a period's account never changes and no period is ever deleted; a `closed` event carries evidence; a settlement account's owner type and owner never change (a new owner is a new account); a statement supersedes only a statement of its own account; a line's match rows form one chain (a new row on a line that has rows must supersede its current head, of the same line, and never an `accounted` row), so `lineMatchState` reads the head of the chain, not the latest time; a match row's order line belongs to its order; `fee_code` is set on `other_fee` lines. Refusing new lines or match rows inside a closed period without a written reason stays with feature 23.
- **Partner periods** follow the partner's agreed cadence (feature 43); carrier periods are whatever range the brand settles (a cash cycle week or a month). Day coverage (which days each partner has reported, the "no sales" days) is read from statement `covers_from` and `covers_to` (feature 42 builds the grid).
- **Gateway** accounts exist in the enum and the owner check now, and `connections.provider` has `gateway`, so adding them later is rows and an adapter, not a migration.
- Which settlement owner type a location's sales settle through is a question for `LocationPolicyService` ([catalog and locations child](0002-catalog-locations.md#locations-and-the-owner-type-policy)), never a comparison in settlement code.

## Critical test scenarios

- `settlementPeriodStatus` rebuilds `open` or `closed` from a period's events, and `lineMatchState` gives a line's state from its match rows (none means `unmatched`, `not_yet_matched`; `accounted` is terminal), verifies **AC-25**.
- An account with owner type `carrier` and a party fails its check; two overlapping periods for one account fail the exclusion; two accounts may share dates, verifies **AC-25**.
- A line imported twice into one statement with the same `external_line_ref` fails the unique key; a corrected statement reusing line number 1 inserts; `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on lines, matches, statements, and period events fail for the app role and the owner, verifies **AC-25**, **AC-33**.
- A `matched` row without an order or variant fails; an `accounted` row without an account code fails; an `unmatched` row without a reason fails, verifies **AC-25**.
- (feature 23) Lines dated 3 to 28 November imported daily, the November period created on 3 December: the period finds all of them by `occurred_on`. A partner period with sales, a return, and a `partner_payment` accounted as `payment_received` closes when matched plus accounted equals the statement total, verifies **AC-25**.
- (feature 22) Match, then unmatch with a reason, then match again: three rows, the latest wins, nothing updated, verifies **AC-25**.
- (feature 23) Close a period, revoke it with a reason, close it again: three events, `status` matches a rebuild from events; revoking without a reason fails, verifies **AC-25**.
- (feature 22) An unbound Bosta account with lines for two stores: each line carries its delivery's store, verifies **AC-25** (B1).
