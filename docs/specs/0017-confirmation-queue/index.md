## Summary

The confirmation queue is where a support agent talks to every new order's customer before it ships, which is the brand's main defence against parcels coming back unpaid. It is a screen over the orders that are still `new`, oldest first and grouped by customer, where the agent sends a prefilled Arabic WhatsApp message (a plain `wa.me` link, no WhatsApp API) and waits for the reply, or calls, then records one outcome; the outcome confirms, cancels, or schedules the next try through the orders module's existing moves, and three unanswered calls or expired message waits over two working days close the order. Because in mirror mode the Bosta delivery is made from Shopify, the queue writes its result to the Shopify order as two brand named tags (confirmed, held), and the brand sends orders to Bosta only from the Shopify view "confirmed and not held". In the same card the agent merges duplicate orders by choosing lines and address, fixes the address into the carrier's governorate and city, and changes sizes or adds pieces, with every edit written back to Shopify and an unsynced one shown as "Edit not synced, hold". It also answers the open money question D2: a shipping deposit pays the shipping first and is kept as the refused delivery fee by default, and the prepaid share of a piece that never reaches the customer is owed back to her.

## Requirements

**User stories**:

- As a support agent, I want the oldest unconfirmed order at the top, a promised callback at its promised time, and the same customer's other open orders beside it, so that nobody waits two days and I spot duplicates without searching.
- As a support agent, I want one tap to open WhatsApp on the customer's number with the order already written in Arabic, mark the message sent, and find her card again from her phone when she replies, so that confirming on WhatsApp works the way the desk really works.
- As a support agent, I want to record one outcome and have the app do the rest (confirm, cancel with the reason, wait for a reply or a transfer, or schedule the callback in working hours), so that I never keep a spreadsheet of who to call back.
- As a support agent, I want to fix the address into the carrier's governorate and city with the likely one suggested, and to change a size or add a piece, so that the courier gets the right parcel and the right amount.
- As a support agent, I want to merge a customer's duplicate orders in one tap, keeping the size and address she wants, and have the extra ones cancelled on Shopify with a reason, so that only one right parcel ships.
- As operations, I want confirmed and held orders marked on the Shopify order itself, so that I send to Bosta only what support confirmed and nothing that is held.
- As a warehouse person, I want a "to pack" list that puts any held order at the top, marked "Edit not synced, hold", so that I never pack the old size.
- As a manager, I want each order's tries, outcomes, agent, line, and times kept for ever, so that the confirm rate and the time to confirm can be measured per agent, store, and channel.
- As a support agent, I want an order whose Bosta delivery could not be fixed to come back to me with what to tell the customer, so that the courier does not surprise her at the door.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract; each is checkable on its own):

_The queue_

- **AC-1** (who is in it): `/confirmation` (permission `orders.confirm`, capability `orders.confirmation_queue`) lists every order whose fulfillment state is `new`, of every type and store, with no row needed beforehand (the queue is a read over `orders` left joined to `order_confirmations`). An order leaves it the moment its fulfillment state leaves `new`, whatever moved it. Orders imported by go live as `confirmed` (spec 0013 AC-25) and gap orders that skip the queue (spec 0009 AC-27) never appear.
- **AC-2** (lanes and order): four lanes, each with its count: **Due now** (no `next_attempt_at`, or `next_attempt_at` at or before now), **Later** (`next_attempt_at` after now, ordered by that time), **Reconfirm** (orders with an open reconfirmation, AC-21), and **Held** (confirmed orders whose hold is on, AC-19, and orders closed in the app whose Shopify cancel is still to happen, AC-11). Due now puts promised callbacks that are due first, by promised time, with "Promised 18:00" on the card (review m1), then every other group oldest first by the placed time of its oldest order, then order id; returned reply waits are marked "No reply: call" and returned transfer waits "Deposit not received" (review M1, M4). Pages are keyset pages of 50 groups (100 at most) with no skipped or repeated group while orders arrive. A group is every order in the lane with the same `customer_id`, or, for an order without a customer, the same `ship_to_phone`; a group's other open orders of the same canonical customer (spec 0014 `idsOf`) within `confirmation.duplicate_window_hours` are shown on its card even when they sit in another lane.
- **AC-3** (the card): each order card shows the number with its store (spec 0013 AC-3), the placed time and its age in the install time zone, type and gift flag, channel, the customer's name, phone (`tel:` link, `LtrText`) and phone kind, second phone, risk flag and returner counts (spec 0014 `briefs`, `OrderQueries.countsForCustomers`), the lines (SKU code and name, size, quantity, price, and available stock at the online location through spec 0011's online stock read), total, prepaid with its method, purpose, and check state, COD expected, the address with its area codes, the courier note, the tries so far (outcome, channel, line, agent, time), the WhatsApp thread's line and agent, the write back state of each open command, and a banner when the order already has a carrier delivery (AC-20). A phone that does not parse shows the raw text with "Fix the phone" and disables WhatsApp and Call until fixed.
- **AC-4** (soft claim, per group): opening a card claims every order of its group for 10 minutes for that agent (`claimed_by`, `claimed_until` on each order's row, review m2); other agents see "Mona is on this customer" and the group sorts after unclaimed ones in Due now, but can still open it and take it over (the claim moves, no error). Recording an outcome on every order of the group, or releasing the card, clears the claim. A claim writes no audit entry and no event.
- **AC-29** (find by phone, review M1): `POST /api/confirmation/find` with a phone in the body (never in the URL; typed or pasted from WhatsApp, any form `EgyptianPhone` parses) answers the open orders of that phone's canonical customer (`CustomersService.findByPhone`) or of orders with that ship to phone, each with its lane, and the orders of that phone closed by the queue in the last 14 days (`closed_no_answer`, `cancelled`, `fake`) with a "Reopen" action that creates spec 0013's `reopened_from` order (one reopen per original, spec 0013 AC-23; review m8). A phone that matches nothing answers "no open order for this number" without echoing it.

_Talking to the customer_

- **AC-5** (WhatsApp deep link): the WhatsApp button opens `https://wa.me/<digits>?text=<urlencoded message>`, where `<digits>` is the phone in international form without `+`, zeros, spaces, or dashes (`EgyptianPhone` E.164 with the `+` removed), in a new window with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. It is offered for the main and the second phone; a phone of kind `landline` disables it with "Landline: call only", and a `foreign` mobile allows it (review m3). The message is the brand template `confirm` from the setting `confirmation.whatsapp_templates`, in Arabic by default (English when the agent switches the message language on the card), with its placeholders filled in the browser from the card data already loaded: `{customer_name}`, `{store_name}`, `{order_number}`, `{items}` (one line per open line: name, size, quantity), `{total}`, `{cod_amount}` (COD expected), `{payment_line}` (the catalog words for "due on delivery: amount", or "paid in full" when COD expected is 0, review m4), `{address}`, and `{deposit_amount}` (the shipping charged, for the `deposit_request` template); money through `useFormat()` in the message language. The `closing` template is offered after a closing outcome (review m8) and `deposit_request` beside "Ask for a shipping deposit" (AC-22, review m10). The link and the message are built only in the browser and never sent to the api, logged, or stored.
- **AC-6** (call, and the outcome on return): the Call button is a `tel:` link to the main phone (a second button for the second phone when set). After the agent taps WhatsApp or Call and the page becomes visible again, the card shows "What happened?" with focus on the outcome group, so the outcome is logged on return. The agent picks her WhatsApp line once per session from `confirmation.whatsapp_lines` (kept in the browser, sent with every WhatsApp outcome as `sendingLine`).

_Outcomes_

- **AC-7** (one outcome, one transaction): `POST /api/confirmation/orders/:id/outcomes` takes `attemptKey` (a uuid made by the browser for each press), `expectedUpdatedAt` (the order's), `outcome`, `channel` (`whatsapp`, `call`, or `none`), `sendingLine` (required with `whatsapp`), and the fields that outcome needs. In one `OrderTransactions.run` it locks the order, writes exactly one `confirmation_attempts` row, updates `order_confirmations`, calls the orders move the outcome names, opens, reschedules, or closes the callback task (AC-17), enqueues the tag command when the order's tag state changes (AC-27), and publishes `ConfirmationOutcomeRecorded`. A repeat of the same `attemptKey` with the same body answers 200 with the stored result and writes nothing; the same key with another body answers 409 `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. A stale `expectedUpdatedAt` answers 409 `ORDER_STALE`.
- **AC-8** (what each outcome does), for an order in `new` (Due now or Later):

| Outcome | Needs | Does to the order | Does to the queue |
|---|---|---|---|
| `confirmed` | the area of `confirmation.require_area` when the order is editable in the app (else 422 `AREA_REQUIRED`); with a short line, the agent's answer to "This piece is not in stock. Confirm anyway, or offer another size?", recorded as reason `short_acknowledged` (else 422 `SHORT_LINE_UNANSWERED`, review m6) | `OrderTransitions.confirm` (spec 0013 AC-7: `new` to `confirmed`, tops up short lines) | done, outcome `confirmed`; the confirmed tag is written (AC-27); the order moves to Held while its hold is on |
| `message_sent` | channel `whatsapp`, `sendingLine` | nothing | `next_attempt_at` = the record time plus `confirmation.reply_wait_minutes` working minutes, kind `reply_wait`; the thread's line and agent are recorded; not a no answer (review M1) |
| `no_answer` | channel `call`, or channel `whatsapp` after a reply wait has passed (else 422 `REPLY_WAIT_OPEN`) | nothing until the last no answer; on the one that reaches `confirmation.no_answer_attempts`, `OrderTransitions.cancel` with reason `no_answer` | `no_answer_count + 1`; `next_attempt_at` from the callback rule (AC-9); on the last, done with outcome `closed_no_answer` and the `closing` template offered |
| `callback_requested` | `callbackAt` between now plus 5 minutes and 14 days ahead | nothing | `next_attempt_at = callbackAt`, kind `promised`, owner the agent (AC-17) |
| `awaiting_transfer` | `deadlineAt` from now to 72 hours ahead, default now plus `confirmation.deposit_wait_hours` in working time | nothing | `next_attempt_at = deadlineAt`, kind `deposit_wait`; returns to Due now marked "Deposit not received" (review M4) |
| `cancelled` | `reasonCode` from `customer_cancelled`, `out_of_stock`, `wrong_details`, `deposit_not_paid`, `other` | `OrderTransitions.cancel` with that reason | done, outcome `cancelled` |
| `duplicate` | `duplicateOfOrderId`, an open order of the same group | `OrderTransitions.cancel` with reason `duplicate` and the target (spec 0013 AC-7) | done, outcome `duplicate` (the merge of AC-13 is the one tap path) |
| `fake` | `reasonCode` `confirmed_fake` (invented details, abuse) or `denies_ordering` (the owner of the number says she never ordered) (review m9) | `OrderTransitions.cancel` with reason `fake`; with `confirmed_fake` and a customer, `CustomersService.setFlag(tx, customerId, 'fake', { reasonCode: 'confirmed_fake' }, actor)` in the same transaction unless she already carries `fake`; with `denies_ordering`, no flag | done, outcome `fake` |

  Every cancel keeps spec 0013's rules: a Shopify store order is cancelled in the app only while `writeBackAvailable(orderId, 'shopify.order_cancel')` is true; otherwise the outcome is still recorded, the order is not cancelled, and the confirmation row is done with `pending_channel_cancel = true`, shown in Held as "Cancel in Shopify" until the channel cancel arrives (AC-11). A cancel of an order with a carrier delivery raises `terminate_carrier_delivery` (spec 0013 AC-8) and the card says to terminate it in the Bosta dashboard. Before a cancel of an order with `prepaid_amount > 0`, the button names the money: "This cancels and refunds 2,000.00 to the card" for a card payment refunded by the Shopify cancel (Q-18.6), or "This cancels; 50.00 InstaPay is owed back" for any other prepayment (AC-31).
- **AC-9** (the callback rule, Cairo time and working hours): all times are in the install `timezone` setting (default `Africa/Cairo`, daylight saving included) and inside the brand's working hours, the platform setting `operations.working_hours` of spec 0016 (weekly hours plus dated exceptions for Ramadan, Eid, and public holidays, so the pilot's 2027 holidays are covered, review m5), using spec 0016's pure working time functions in `packages/contracts/src/tasks/task-due.ts`. After counted no answer number `k` of `N` (`confirmation.no_answer_attempts`, default 3): for `k = 1`, `next_attempt_at` = the record time plus `confirmation.no_answer_retry_minutes` working minutes (default 180); for `1 < k < N`, the next working day in the other part of the day from the first no answer (18:00 when the first was before 16:00, else the opening time plus 60 minutes; the review's Q-18.2 view); for `k = N`, the order closes (AC-8). A reply wait and a transfer wait count in working time too. Any computed time outside working hours moves to the next opening. With the defaults: a call at 11:00, again at 14:00, again the next working day at 18:00, and the third no answer closes, always over at least two working days. The rule is one pure function `nextCallbackAt` in `packages/contracts/src/orders/confirmation-rules.ts`, used by the api and by the card's preview ("next try tomorrow at 18:00").
- **AC-10** (counting): `no_answer_count` counts the `no_answer` rows of the order's current confirmation that counted (`counts_as_no_answer`); `message_sent`, `callback_requested`, `awaiting_transfer`, and an edit do not change it. An agent may record an outcome on an order in Later (calling early is allowed) and it counts by the rule of AC-8.
- **AC-11** (leaving the queue another way): when an order leaves `new` by any other path, its confirmation row is set done with outcome `superseded` in the same transaction (a handler on spec 0013's `OrderStateChanged` inside the orders module) and its open callback task is closed with `superseded`. When a pending channel cancel (AC-8) arrives as `cancelFromChannel`, the row's `pending_channel_cancel` clears in that transaction and the order leaves Held.

_Duplicates_

- **AC-12** (spotting them): a card whose group has two or more open orders (in `new`, `confirmed`, or `packed`) placed within `confirmation.duplicate_window_hours` (default 72) shows "Possible duplicate" with each other order's number, store, placed time, lines, address, payment, and total, side by side (spec 0013 review m4).
- **AC-13** (merge in one tap, review M2, m7): the merge dialog shows the orders' lines side by side with a choice per line (keep, drop, take from the other order) and a choice of address ("use the address of order #1002"). Preselected: the kept order is the prepaid one when exactly one order of the group is prepaid, else the oldest (it carries the campaign of the first visit); when two orders hold the same style in different sizes, the newer size; when the addresses differ, the newer address, said in words. `POST /api/confirmation/merges` takes `keepOrderId`, `duplicateOrderIds[]` (1 to 5), `lines` (the kept order's final open lines: its own line ids to keep, and lines to take from a duplicate as `{ fromOrderId, lineId }` with the same variant, quantity, unit price, and discount), `addressFromOrderId?`, each order's `expectedUpdatedAt`, and an `attemptKey`. In one `OrderTransactions.run` it locks every named order in ascending id, applies one `OrderEditing.edit` on the kept order (removes, adds, and the `ship_to` copy; reason `customer_request`), so it makes one edit and one write back, cancels each duplicate with reason `duplicate` and `duplicateOfOrderId = keepOrderId`, records a `duplicate` attempt on each duplicate, writes one `order.duplicates_merged` audit entry on the kept order, and leaves the kept order in the queue. It refuses with 422 `MERGE_PREPAID` when an order to cancel has `prepaid_amount > 0`, 422 `MERGE_OTHER_STORE` when a line is taken from an order of another store (cancelling a duplicate of another store is allowed; the piece is added by a normal edit), 422 `MERGE_NOT_SAME_GROUP` when the orders are not of one group, and 409 `INVALID_TRANSITION` when any is past `packed`. Each duplicate of a Shopify store becomes a `shopify.order_cancel` command through spec 0015 (`OrderCancelled` with `origin = 'person'`), executed with Shopify's cancel reason mapped from `duplicate` and `notifyCustomer: false`, behind `step.shopify_duplicate_cancel`; while that step or the executor is unavailable, the merge still records everything in the app and each duplicate shows "Cancel in Shopify" in Held.

_Address_

- **AC-14** (the carrier area list): the list is spec 0018's `carrier_areas` table in the connectors module (loaded by its refresh job from Bosta's v2 city, zone, and district reads, with an operator import command as the fallback), read through its exported service; this spec owns no area table. The picker reads one level at a time through `GET /api/confirmation/areas` (`orders.confirm`), which calls that service. Which Bosta level fills each of the order's three codes (`governorate`, `city`, `zone`) is spec 0018's mapping. Each governorate row carries the Shopify province code it maps to (`shopify_province_code`, review M3), asked of spec 0018.
- **AC-15** (fix the address with suggestions, review M3): the card's address panel has the address text, the courier note, and three linked pickers (governorate, then city, then zone). `confirmation.require_area` takes `none`, `governorate_city` (default), or `governorate_city_zone`; the zone is optional by default, and a chosen zone is also appended to the courier note text so the courier sees it whatever Bosta maps. Above the pickers sit at most three suggestions from `GET /api/confirmation/orders/:id/area-suggestions`, in this order: (0) the province and city the customer chose at Shopify checkout (`ship_to_checkout_province_code`, `ship_to_checkout_city`), matched to the list; (1) the area of this customer's most recent delivered order (any id of the canonical customer), else her most recent confirmed one; (2) the areas whose Arabic or English name appears in the order's address text, longest name first; (3) within the city of the first match, the zones most chosen on orders confirmed in the last 180 days. Choosing a suggestion fills the pickers; saving sends one `ship_to` edit through `OrderEditing.edit` with `area: { list, governorateCode, cityCode, zoneCode }` and reason `address_fix` (spec 0013 AC-9, AC-10). Until the brand answers which Shopify fields the Bosta app reads (Q-18.8), the zone is an app field for the returned to origin rate by area.

_Edits and their write back_

- **AC-16** (size changes and added pieces): from the card the agent replaces a line (another size or color of the same style first, then any variant through the catalog search of spec 0012) or adds a line, at the variant's catalog price by default, through `OrderEditing.edit` (reason `size_change` or `customer_request`). After any edit that changes COD expected, the card shows "Tell the customer the new amount: X" and the WhatsApp message carries the new amount. Spec 0013's refusals stand and are shown in words: `EDIT_IN_SHOPIFY` (the write back step is off, with a link to the order in Shopify), `ORDER_LOCKED` (a carrier delivery exists, AC-20), `EDIT_CHANGES_PAYMENT`, and `EDIT_OVERPAID` (a prepaid piece cannot be removed or made cheaper in slice 1a; D2 below).
- **AC-17** (callbacks as tasks): through spec 0016's `TasksService` (calls in [Needs from spec 0016 (tasks)](#needs-from-spec-0016-tasks)), every outcome that sets `next_attempt_at` opens, or reschedules, the one `confirmation.callback` task of the order (dedupe key `confirmation.callback:<orderId>`), due at `next_attempt_at`. Routing (the review's Q-18.10 view): after a WhatsApp try (`message_sent`, or a `no_answer` on WhatsApp) the task is assigned to the agent who holds the thread (the last agent who sent on the order's recorded line), because the reply arrives on her phone; a `callback_requested` or `awaiting_transfer` is assigned to the agent who recorded it; an automatic callback after a call goes to the `support` hat pool. Any `tasks.assign` holder may move it at a shift change. Any outcome that ends the confirmation closes the task with that outcome code, and a person cannot close it by hand in My work (`closedBy: 'system'`; its row opens the card through a type path override). The task is a projection of `order_confirmations` written in the same transaction; the queue reads `order_confirmations`, never the task. Until spec 0016 is built, milestones 1 to 3 run with no task (the Later lane shows every scheduled try).
- **AC-18** (executors): the `shopify.order_edit`, `shopify.order_cancel`, and `shopify.order_tags` executors are registered with spec 0015's `OutboundExecutors.register` under `connectors.shopify.order_write_back` and the steps `step.shopify_order_edit_write_back`, `step.shopify_duplicate_cancel`, and `step.shopify_order_tags_write_back`. The edit reads `OrderQueries.writeBackViewOf` at execution time, applies lines with an order edit session committed with `notifyCustomer: false` and the address with an order update: the address text, the city name in the Shopify address's city, and the governorate's `shopify_province_code` as the province code, never a Bosta name; a governorate without a code writes no province and the command fails with the code `PROVINCE_UNMAPPED` (review M3). On sync it calls `OrderIntake.recordChannelLineIds`. The cancel cancels with restock, `notifyCustomer: false`, the reason mapped from the code, and, for a card prepaid order, the refund (Q-18.6). Every Shopify mutation and argument is checked against the pinned API version at build. All three follow spec 0015's failure contract (AC-24): `OutboundCommandFailed` is published, and spec 0016 opens `sync.write_back_failed` for it.
- **AC-19** (the hold, carried B2 and n1): an order's hold is on while it has an open `shopify.order_edit` or `shopify.order_cancel` command (`write_back_state` other than `none`, spec 0013 AC-12), the flag "carrier record stale" (spec 0018), or an open reconfirmation (AC-21). A held order shows "Edit not synced, hold" (or "Carrier record stale, hold") with the command's state and provider code on its card and in Held, after it is confirmed, until the hold clears, and carries the held tag on Shopify (AC-27). From Held the agent (or operations) retries the command, marks it fixed in Shopify (spec 0015's resolve, `fixed_in_provider`), or undoes the edit: `POST /api/confirmation/orders/:id/undo-edit` (`commandId`, `expectedUpdatedAt`) resolves the failed command with `edit_undone` through `ConnectorsService.resolve` in one transaction, and the read back spec 0015 task 13 enqueues on resolve brings Shopify's unchanged order back over the app's edit through `applyChannelEdit`. The warehouse's list `/orders/to-pack` (permission `fulfillment.work`) lists `confirmed` orders oldest first with every held order in a separate section at the top marked "Edit not synced, hold: do not pack", printable on one A4 page per 30 orders; `markPacked` keeps refusing a held order (spec 0013 AC-7). A `shopify.order_tags` command never puts an order on hold.
- **AC-27** (the queue's result on the Shopify order, review C1): the outbound kind `shopify.order_tags` (`{ orderId }`, ids only; shaped by spec 0015, executed here, behind `step.shopify_order_tags_write_back`) writes the queue's state to the Shopify order as the two tags of the setting `confirmation.shopify_tags` (default `confirmed` and `hold-edit-not-synced`). It is a state push: at execution time the executor reads the order and sets the confirmed tag exactly when the order is past `new`, not cancelled, and its hold is off, and the held tag exactly when its hold is on (AC-19), adding and removing only those two tags. One command is enqueued (idempotency key `shopify.order_tags:<orderId>:<order updated_at>`) in the transaction of every change that can flip either tag: a `confirmed` outcome, the hold starting or ending (the `OutboundCommand*` events of an edit or cancel command, spec 0018's flag set or cleared), and an unpack or a reconfirm. A failed tag command is a failed outbound command like any other (spec 0016 opens `sync.write_back_failed`), so a confirmed order the Shopify filter misses is never silent, and the in app to pack list stays the second guard. **Operating rule** (for the pilot and the runbook): orders are sent to Bosta only from the Shopify orders view filtered by the confirmed tag and not the held tag (or from `/orders/to-pack`), never from the plain open orders list; the pilot acceptance run checks that a held order is missing from that Shopify view.

_The Bosta delivery (carried B2, audit item 9)_

- **AC-20** (an order that already has a delivery): when `carrier_delivery_created_at` is set, the card shows "Bosta already has this parcel" with the current tracking number and Bosta state from spec 0018 (after a recreated delivery, the new tracking number, since spec 0018 relinks the order). **Before confirmation** (a route Bosta assigned before the order was confirmed, as with the automatic sync of Q-18.1 option b), the order stays editable, spec 0018 raises `route_before_confirmation` for support, and the banner says "Bosta already has this parcel: any change you make here must also be made in Bosta; the app checks the two once you confirm" (spec 0018 review M5). The money freeze then happens at confirmation or at the first pickup, whichever comes first (asked of spec 0013 by both specs); from the freeze on, line and shipping edits answer spec 0013's `ORDER_LOCKED` and the card says a size or amount change must be made in the Bosta app. Confirm, `message_sent`, the cancel outcomes (which raise `terminate_carrier_delivery`), `no_answer`, and `callback_requested` stay available throughout.
- **AC-21** (the fixer acts first, then the reconfirmation, review M6 and spec 0018 review M6): spec 0018 checks a confirmed order against its delivery and publishes `CarrierRecordStale { deliveryId, orderId, trackingNumber, stateCode, fixableBy, mismatches[], editedBy? }` when they differ, again whenever the list or `fixableBy` changes, and `CarrierRecordStaleCleared { deliveryId, orderId, trackingNumber, mismatches, reason, fixOutcome }` when the flag clears (`reason` `fixed_at_carrier` or `order_matches`; `fixOutcome` `updated`, `recreated`, `cannot_update`, `cancel_recommended`, or null). Each mismatch is `{ field, fixableBy }` with `field` one of `CarrierMismatchField`: `receiver_address`, `receiver_phone` (main and second), `carrier_area`, `cod`, `items_count`; a `cod` mismatch adds `carrierValue`, `orderValue`, `origin` (`at_creation` or `after_edit`), and `paymentMethodDiffers`, and an `items_count` mismatch adds `carrierValue` and `orderValue`; no other field carries a value (Law 151). `fixableBy` is `merchant`, `bosta_support`, `bosta_support_best_effort`, `nobody`, or `closed`. **On `CarrierRecordStale`** the order is held (AC-19) and this feature opens no task of its own; the fixer acts first. When every mismatch is `merchant` fixable, the agent named in `editedBy` sees the banner at once on her card ("Change it in the Bosta app now: address, phone") and owns spec 0018's `carrier.record_stale` task for 30 minutes, after which it moves to operations; otherwise operations fixes it, and the banner is worded by `fixableBy`: `bosta_support` "operations is asking Bosta support to change it", `bosta_support_best_effort` "operations is asking Bosta support; it may not be possible", `nobody` or `closed` "Bosta cannot change this delivery: operations decides to cancel or deliver as is". A `cod` mismatch shows both amounts and, from `origin`, whether "Bosta's amount differed from the start" or "differs after the edit"; `paymentMethodDiffers` shows "Bosta has another payment method". Every `CarrierRecordStale` is real and followed the same way whatever its `origin`: spec 0018 AC-31 never publishes a systematic COD difference at creation as stale (it is a setup warning there), so this feature needs no rule for it, and `origin` only words the card. **On `CarrierRecordStaleCleared`** the Reconfirm lane follows `fixOutcome`: `updated` opens no reconfirmation; `recreated` opens one with the script "Bosta will deliver with a new tracking number, on a later day"; `cannot_update` opens one whose script names what the courier still holds; `cancel_recommended` opens one with cancel preselected; null (reason `order_matches`) opens none and closes an open reconfirmation as `cleared_by_carrier`. A reconfirmation opens a `confirmation.reconfirm` task for the `support` hat due now, and the order stays held while it is open. The card lists the fields that differ in words ("address", "phone", "area", "amount to collect", "number of pieces"); the script mentions money ("the courier will ask for X, not Y") only when `cod` is in the list and pieces ("the parcel has 2 pieces, not 3") only when `items_count` is, and for the other fields says which detail the courier has wrong, never its value. When the edit was made with the customer on the line (an attempt by the same agent within 30 minutes before the edit), the card says "She agreed to this change at 11:40", so the call informs and never asks for a second consent. Reconfirm outcomes: `reconfirmed` (with `acceptedCodAmount` when `cod` is in the list, recorded on the attempt for feature 22), `cancelled` with a reason (as AC-8, while the order can still be cancelled), `message_sent`, `no_answer`, and `callback_requested` (the rule of AC-9; reaching `N` counted no answers does not cancel a confirmed order: the attempt is recorded as `escalated` and the task moves to the `manager` hat queue).

_Money (D2)_

- **AC-22** (shipping deposit, review M4, M5): on a card whose customer carries a risk flag, or whose returner signal suggests one, the card suggests "Ask for a shipping deposit" (never blocks, consistent with spec 0002's decided default 5) beside the `deposit_request` template, which states the amount and the refusal rule of `shipping_deposit_on_refusal` before she pays (review m10). Waiting for the money is the outcome `awaiting_transfer` (AC-8). **Orders of an internal store** (the app created them): `POST /api/orders/:id/deposit` (`orders.confirm`; `expectedUpdatedAt`, `amount` above 0 and at most the order total, `method` one of `instapay`, `wallet`, `bank_transfer`, `reference` of 4 to 40 letters and digits) records it through a new orders move `OrderIntake.recordDeposit` while the order has no carrier delivery, nothing is prepaid yet, and payment is `cod`: it sets `prepaid_amount`, `prepaid_method`, `prepaid_reference`, and `prepaid_purpose = 'shipping_deposit'`, allocates the deposit to shipping first and only its excess to lines by gross share, recomputes COD expected and COD due, and writes `order.deposit_recorded`. **Orders of a Shopify store**: the deposit is taken in Shopify only, and the app reads back whatever Shopify shows under spec 0013's rules (a shipping or discount change arrives as an edit; a partial payment is not read yet); the deposit route answers 409 `DEPOSIT_IN_SHOPIFY` ("record it in Shopify; it will sync"). How the app marks a Shopify deposit as `shipping_deposit` waits for the brand's method (Q-18.5) and becomes a spec 0015 mapping then. A person with `settlement.work` checks deposits against the wallet or bank statement from the list "Deposits to check" (day, method, amount, reference; `GET /api/orders/deposits-to-check`), which sets `prepaid_verified_at` and `prepaid_verified_by` (`POST /api/orders/:id/deposit/verify`); until then the card shows "Deposit claimed, not yet checked", and a confirm on an unchecked deposit is allowed and marked on the attempt (reason `deposit_unchecked`).
- **AC-23** (the D2 rules this spec fixes for features 21, 28, and 35): (a) a shipping deposit pays the shipping first; on a delivery, full or partial, the courier collects the lines kept and no shipping (shipping is charged in full on a partial delivery, owner answer of spec 0002); (b) on a refusal or a return to origin, the part of the deposit on shipping is kept by the brand as the refused delivery fee (setting `shipping_deposit_on_refusal`, default `kept_as_delivery_fee`), and any excess on lines is owed back like any prepaid share; (c) the prepaid share of a piece that never reaches the customer (a line returned to origin or lost, or a cancelled prepaid order) is owed back to her by the tender she paid with, or kept as store credit if she chooses, posted as `prepayment_release` (spec 0002 D2 change 1), never counted in `returned_credit_amount`, so the order's outcome stays `kept` or `failed_delivery` whatever the payment method. This spec decides the rules and records the deposit; posting `prepayment_release` and the refund record are features 21, 28, and 35 (see [D2 answer](#d2-answer-prepaid-pieces-and-the-shipping-deposit)).
- **AC-31** (money owed back is never invisible, review M4, Q-18.4): when this feature cancels an order with `prepaid_amount > 0` that the Shopify cancel does not refund (any deposit or transfer, or a card payment whose refund is off), it opens spec 0016's `refund.unrecorded_payout` on the order in the same transaction (dedupe key `refund_unrecorded:<orderId>`, note "<amount> <method>"), so operations sees it and the daily report counts it until feature 35's refund record replaces it.

_Metrics, security, and screens_

- **AC-24** (what the queue produces for later features, review m11): every outcome is a `confirmation_attempts` row (order, attempt number, phase, outcome, reason, contact channel, sending line, agent, hat, recorded time, operating day) and a `ConfirmationOutcomeRecorded` event; `ConfirmationQueries.metrics({ dayFrom, dayTo, groupBy })` (groupBy `day`, `agent`, `store`, `sales_channel`, `contact_channel`) answers per group: orders entered, confirmed (counted from `orders.confirmed_at`, so an order confirmed on the order page counts; the attempt log says who and how), cancelled by reason, closed by no answer, fake by reason, duplicate, confirm rate (confirmed over confirmed plus cancelled, closed, and fake; gifted orders and duplicates left out), tries per confirmed order (`message_sent` counts as a try), median and 90th percentile time to first try and time to confirm, each in clock minutes and in working minutes of `operations.working_hours`. It also answers, per `confirmed_by`, the delivered and returned to origin rate of the orders that agent confirmed, once feature 19's states exist, the figure feature 24 uses to say whether confirmation works. The queue page shows today's strip: due now, later, confirmed today, confirm rate today, median time to confirm today.
- **AC-25** (security): every new route is `@Audience('staff')` with exactly one `@Permission` and a class level `@RequiresCapability('orders.confirmation_queue')` (the to pack and deposit routes `@RequiresModule('orders')`), each has a route sweep fixture, and a person without the permission gets 403 `FORBIDDEN`. No log line, domain event, task field other than the owed back note, or audit column written by this feature carries a phone, name, address, courier note, deposit reference, or message text; `confirmation_attempts` has no free text column.
- **AC-26** (screens): the queue, the card, find by phone, the merge dialog, the address panel, the deposit dialog and list, Held, and the to pack list render inside the back office shell with exactly one `<h1>`, in Arabic and English with right to left layout and logical CSS only, built to `packages/ui/DESIGN.md`; numbers, money, and times through `useFormat()`; order numbers, SKU codes, and phones in `LtrText`. They work on a 360 pixel wide phone (one column, the outcome bar fixed at the bottom edge, targets at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels) and on a desk by keyboard alone: arrow keys move between cards in a lane, Enter opens one, the outcome buttons are one toolbar, no single character shortcut exists (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.1.4), focus is always visible and returns to the next card after an outcome, and axe reports no violation in either language.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 1 in [rationale.md](rationale.md): the queue is a read over `new` orders inside the orders module, with an orders owned `order_confirmations` row per order (the current try state) and an append only `confirmation_attempts` log (every outcome); every outcome calls the orders module's existing moves in one order transaction; tasks are opened through spec 0016 only for scheduled tries and reconfirmations, as a projection; and the queue's result reaches the place where the Bosta delivery is made as two Shopify tags.

Runner up: the queue as a list of `confirmation.call` tasks with attempts counted from closed tasks (the counting rule of spec 0002's tasks section), rejected because it makes first sale depend on spec 0016 being built first, cannot show an order that has no task yet, and loses the outcome history whenever a task is cancelled or rescheduled.

### State transitions

The order's states are spec 0013's; this feature adds none. The confirmation row:

- `open` (first try, or a claim) → `open` (each `message_sent`, `awaiting_transfer`, `callback_requested`, and counted `no_answer` below `N`) → `done` with `confirmed`, `cancelled`, `duplicate`, `fake`, `closed_no_answer`, or `superseded`.
- A done row reopens only for a reconfirmation: `reconfirm_status` `null` → `open` (spec 0018's clear event with the fix outcome `recreated`, `cannot_update`, or `cancel_recommended`) → `done` (`reconfirmed`, `cancelled`, `cleared_by_carrier`; `escalated` keeps it open with the task moved to the manager hat).
- `pending_channel_cancel` `false` → `true` (a cancel outcome on a Shopify order while its cancel cannot be written back) → `false` (the channel cancel arrived, AC-11).

The Shopify tags follow the order (AC-27): no tag while `new`; confirmed tag when past `new` with the hold off; held tag, without the confirmed tag, while the hold is on; neither after a cancel.

### The Bosta side of a confirmation edit (audit item 9)

The independent audit of 2026-11-11, section 4, item 9, names the gap: in mirror mode a size change made after Bosta created the delivery leaves the wrong COD amount at the door. The work is split:

- **This spec (feature 18) owns the confirmation side**: what the agent sees when the order already has a delivery (AC-20: the banner with the current tracking number, edits disabled, the Bosta dashboard instruction, which outcomes remain); what is held (AC-19: "Edit not synced, hold" and "Carrier record stale, hold" on the card, Held, the to pack list, and the held tag on Shopify, AC-27); the task it raises (`confirmation.reconfirm` for the `support` hat, only after operations' outcome asks for a call); and what the agent tells the customer and which amount she accepted (AC-21).
- **Spec 0018 (feature 19, lane D, in progress) owns the Bosta side**: detecting the order's delivery; checking a confirmed order against it over the five `CarrierMismatchField` values (the flag named exactly "carrier record stale"); `fixableBy`; the `carrier.record_stale` task and its routing (the editing agent for 30 minutes when every mismatch is merchant fixable, else operations); the fix outcome; relinking a recreated delivery; `route_before_confirmation`; the `COD_RULE_MISMATCH` setup warning; clearing the flag; and the carrier area list (`carrier_areas`). This spec does not design any of it.
- **What this spec takes from spec 0018** (its final contract of 2026-11-12, after spec 0018's domain review): the events `CarrierRecordStale` and `CarrierRecordStaleCleared` with the shapes in AC-21; the fixer acts first (the editing agent when every mismatch is merchant fixable, else operations), and the reconfirmation follows `fixOutcome` on the cleared event; `recreated` relinks the order, so the banner shows the current tracking number; a route before confirmation keeps the order editable with `route_before_confirmation` (AC-20); the delivery read for the banner; `carrier_areas` with its read service, the refresh job, and the operator import as fallback, and a `shopify_province_code` on governorate rows (asked).
- **Asked of spec 0013 by both**: `markPacked` refuses an order flagged "carrier record stale" as it refuses an open write back (Follow-up).

### D2 answer: prepaid pieces and the shipping deposit

The domain review's build re-read (D2) asked two things; the scope row requires this spec to answer them, and feature 21 carries the same line. The [domain review of this spec](../../reviews/domain-confirmation-queue.md) judged the rules right and asked for the steps around them (M4, M5), folded in above.

1. **The prepaid share of a piece that never reaches the customer** is owed back to her. At confirmation, slice 1a does not refund in the app: removing or cheapening a prepaid piece stays refused (`EDIT_OVERPAID`, `EDIT_CHANGES_PAYMENT`), a prepaid duplicate cannot be merged away (`MERGE_PREPAID`), and the card says to change it in Shopify, where the payment lives (Q-18.4). A cancelled prepaid order is refunded by the Shopify cancel for a card payment (Q-18.6, the amount shown before the press), and otherwise opens `refund.unrecorded_payout` (AC-31), so the money owed is never invisible. After shipping, the prepaid share of each line returned to origin or lost is posted as `prepayment_release` (positive, from the prepaid funding, `order_id` required, kept out of `returned_credit_amount`) and paid back with a `refund_payout` of the same tender or kept as credit at her choice, so the outcome stays `kept` or `failed_delivery` for every payment method (AC-23 c). Posting it is feature 21 (returned to origin) with the credit module of feature 28, the payout feature 35.
2. **A shipping deposit is for the shipping, and on a refusal it is the refused delivery fee.** The customer is told the rule in the `deposit_request` message before she pays; the agent waits for the transfer with `awaiting_transfer`; on an internal store order the agent records it with its reference (AC-22), and on a Shopify order it is taken in Shopify only until the brand's method is known (Q-18.5); a person checks it against the statement. It is marked `prepaid_purpose = 'shipping_deposit'` and allocated to shipping first, so a partial delivery collects exactly the lines kept and opens no false shortfall (the 23.81 example of D2). On a refusal or return to origin the part on shipping is kept by default (`kept_as_delivery_fee`, Q-18.3) and any excess on lines is owed back as in point 1. **Left to feature 21**: applying the refusal rule when the parcel comes back, posting the release, and the shipping legs; **left to features 28 and 35**: the credit kind's posting rules, the refund record, and the payout.

### Key invariants

- Only `confirmation-writer.ts` writes `order_confirmations` and `confirmation_attempts`; only spec 0013's order writer writes `orders`; a single writer test greps both.
- Every outcome writes exactly one attempt row, in the same transaction as the orders move it causes; a replay with the same `attemptKey` writes nothing.
- `no_answer_count` equals the count of the order's counted `no_answer` rows of the `confirm` phase; a `message_sent` never counts; a test recounts after every scenario.
- An order is in exactly one lane; an order whose fulfillment state is not `new` is never in Due now or Later.
- The Shopify confirmed tag is never written while the order is held; a held order carries the held tag once its tag command has synced.
- A callback task, when spec 0016 is present, is open exactly when the confirmation row has `next_attempt_at` set and `status = 'open'`, with the same due time.
- No attempt row, event, task (except the owed back note's amount and method), or log line carries a phone, name, address, courier note, deposit reference, or message text.
- The WhatsApp message and link never reach the api.

### Security model

- Audience `staff` only. `orders.confirm` (support, operations, manager; personal, so no external hat) works the queue, finds by phone, merges, records deposits, and undoes edits; `settlement.work` (operations, manager) checks deposits; `fulfillment.work` reads the to pack list. The queue routes sit behind the Operations capability `orders.confirmation_queue`: a Warehouse only install keeps the plain confirm and cancel of the order page (spec 0013) and the to pack list.
- Law 151: phones, names, addresses, courier notes, the checkout city, and the deposit reference are personal data and join `PERSONAL_DATA_KEYS` where not already there; the queue answers carry them only to `orders.confirm` holders, in the body, never in a URL (find by phone is a POST). The WhatsApp message goes from the agent's browser to WhatsApp, the channel the brand already uses for this conversation; the default template carries only what the agent would type, and the template is the brand's to shorten. Shopify tags and WhatsApp line labels are patterned so no personal data can be stored in them.
- The soft claim is advisory: it never blocks a write, so a stale claim cannot lock an order.
- Rate: no public route; the outcome route is idempotent on `attemptKey`.

## Consequences

**Positive**:

- The pilot's "one week of real confirmations with no spreadsheet" has a home from milestone 1, and it does not wait on tasks, Bosta, or the Shopify write back to start.
- The queue's work no longer stops at the app's edge: whoever sends to Bosta sees confirmed and held on the Shopify order itself, which is where the delivery is made in mirror mode.
- A WhatsApp desk can work its real rhythm (send, wait, find the reply by phone) without using up the customer's tries.
- Every try is kept for ever with its agent, line, and time, so confirm rate and time to confirm come from the model, not from each page (the reference brand's contradiction lesson).
- One transaction per outcome: the try, the order move, the task, the tag command, and the event commit or roll back together, and a replay of a press changes nothing.
- The money rules of D2 are fixed before features 21, 22, and 28 are designed; a deposit has a wait, a check, and a reference, and money owed back is a task from day one.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:

- The operating rule (send to Bosta only from "confirmed and not held") is a habit the brand must keep; the app cannot stop someone sending from the plain list, it can only make the right view exist and the held order visible.
- A tag command that fails leaves a confirmed order missing from the Shopify view (the safe side) and opens a sync task; a held tag that fails to sync leaves a held order looking unheld in Shopify until the task is worked, with the to pack list as the second guard.
- Two places describe a scheduled try once spec 0016 lands (the confirmation row and the task); the row wins and the task is rewritten in the same transaction, so a bug in the projection shows a wrong due time in My work but never in the queue.
- With the Shopify write back steps off (until milestone 3, or if the brand never turns them on), every Shopify order's edit and cancel must be done in Shopify, and "Cancel in Shopify" items pile up in Held until synced back.
- In mirror mode the zone reaches Bosta only through the courier note text, and the city and province only through Shopify fields whose use by the Bosta app is unknown (Q-18.8).
- Shopify orders cannot carry an app recorded deposit in slice 1a; until the brand's method is known (Q-18.5), a deposit on a Shopify order is whatever Shopify shows, and its deposit marker waits.
- A prepaid order cannot be made cheaper in the app in slice 1a; the agent edits it in Shopify, whose snapshot the app then refuses to apply (spec 0013 `channel_edit_not_applied` with `overpaid`), so such orders need feature 35 or a manual task.
- The soft claim can be ignored by a second agent; the group claim and the thread owner make a double message unlikely, not impossible.

**Neutral**:

- Two orders owned tables, six orders columns, five new enums, three audit codes, ten settings rows (plus one read from spec 0016), two task types, one domain event, one new outbound kind and executing step; no new environment variable or queue; no area table (spec 0018's).
- The counting rule in spec 0002's tasks section is replaced for confirmation by the attempt log (spec 0016 amendment 5 records it).

## Open owner questions

Each is built as recommended unless the owner says otherwise; the status stays Assumed until they are answered.

1. **Q-18.1 How does your Bosta Shopify app send orders to Bosta?** Both Bosta Shopify apps offer only an automatic sync on order creation or a manual "Deliver with Bosta" (individually or in bulk), with no fulfillment or tag trigger, and the newer app can cancel the delivery automatically when the Shopify order is cancelled (lane D's research for spec 0018; the reference brand's data shows 136 parcels stuck at "pickup requested" holding 104.6K EGP of COD, so it syncs automatically before confirmation). _Recommended (a): turn the automatic sync off and send by hand, after confirmation, from the Shopify view "confirmed and not held" (AC-27)._ Other option (b): keep the automatic sync and accept that every confirmation edit lands after the delivery and becomes "carrier record stale" (the Reconfirm lane becomes daily work and milestones 2 and 3 are re-planned). **Must be answered before milestone 2.** Also asked: which of the two apps, and is its automatic cancel on? With option (b), or whenever Bosta assigns a route before confirmation, the order stays editable, spec 0018 raises `route_before_confirmation` for support, and the money freeze happens at confirmation or the first pickup, whichever comes first (AC-20; spec 0018 review M5). Spec 0018 refers to this question and asks none of its own.
2. **Q-18.2 The no answer rule.** _Recommended: three counted no answers (calls, or WhatsApp after an expired reply wait): a second try 3 working hours after the first, a third the next working day in the other part of the day (18:00 if the first was before 16:00, else soon after opening), then close as `no_answer`; a sent message waits 2 working hours for a reply._ Also asked: does the desk message first and wait, or call first, and how long does it wait?
3. **Q-18.3 A shipping deposit on a refusal.** _Recommended: the brand keeps it as the refused delivery fee (`kept_as_delivery_fee`), and on a partial delivery it counts against shipping only, on condition that the customer is told the rule in the deposit request before she pays._ Other options: released back to the customer; kept as store credit. Also asked: has a lawyer or the consumer protection authority ever questioned keeping it (Law 181 of 2018, not checked here)?
4. **Q-18.4 Removing or cheapening a prepaid piece at confirmation.** _Recommended: refused in the app in slice 1a; the agent changes it in Shopify, which holds the payment, and any money owed back that Shopify does not refund becomes a `refund.unrecorded_payout` task._ Other option: build a refund record now (feature 35 pulled forward).
5. **Q-18.5 How do you make the courier collect less when a customer pays shipping first?** (new brand question.) Shipping set to 0 in Shopify, a discount, a partial payment, or the COD changed in the Bosta dashboard; and who checks the money arrived, against what? _Recommended until answered: Shopify orders take the deposit in Shopify only and the app reads it back; the app records deposits only for orders it created._ The answer becomes the spec 0015 mapping before milestone 4.
6. **Q-18.6 Cancelling a card prepaid Shopify order from the queue.** _Recommended: the Shopify cancel also refunds the card, with the refund amount shown on the button before the press ("This cancels and refunds 2,000.00 to the card")._ Other option: cancel without refund and refund by hand in Shopify (then a `refund.unrecorded_payout` task opens).
7. **Q-18.7 The WhatsApp messages.** _Recommended: the default `confirm`, `closing`, and `deposit_request` templates above, gender neutral, with the payment line for prepaid orders and the address included, approved by the brand's support lead before go live._ Other option: leave the address out and confirm it by voice.
8. **Q-18.8 The carrier area list and which Shopify fields the Bosta app reads.** The list comes from Bosta's API through spec 0018. _Recommended: write the governorate's Shopify province code and the city name to the Shopify address, and append the zone to the courier note._ Asked: which Shopify address fields does the Bosta app read for its city and zone, and what do you do in Bosta today when the zone is wrong? Until answered, the zone is an app field for the returned to origin rate by area.
9. **Q-18.9 Duplicates.** _Recommended: a 72 hour window; keep the oldest order but take the newer lines and address by default when they differ, and keep the prepaid order when exactly one is prepaid._ Also asked: are your duplicates mostly double submits of the COD form, or a second order to change size or address, and which do you keep today?
10. **Q-18.10 Working hours and callbacks.** _Recommended: `operations.working_hours` 10:00 to 22:00 every day with the 2027 holidays entered as exceptions; a callback after a WhatsApp try goes to the agent whose line holds the thread, a promised callback or transfer wait to the agent who recorded it, an automatic callback after a call to the support pool._ Also asked: how many WhatsApp numbers does the desk use, on brand owned or personal phones, and does a customer always talk to the same number? A season setting for longer hours is asked of the brand.
11. **Q-18.11 Area required to confirm.** _Recommended: governorate and city required whenever the order can be edited in the app, zone optional, until the brand confirms the Bosta app reads a zone from Shopify._
12. **Q-18.12 The owner of a misused number.** _Recommended: when the owner of the number says "I never ordered this", cancel as `fake` with `denies_ordering` and set no flag; flag only `confirmed_fake`._ Other option: flag the number either way.
13. **Q-18.13 A customer who asks for delivery later** ("after the 1st", "on Thursday"). _Recommended for slice 1a: record `callback_requested` for that day and confirm then._ Other option: a "confirmed with a delivery date" outcome, not yet designed.
