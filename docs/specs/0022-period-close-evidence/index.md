## Summary

Bosta money is trusted only after someone, or an exact match of evidence, confirms it. Each settlement period gets a close check: a short list of plain conditions (every line settled, every cash out seen in the bank, Bosta's sheet agreeing with the app day by day, Bosta's deposits agreeing, the wallet rolling forward) with both figures side by side, plus what people decided by hand. A person with the close permission closes a period when nothing blocks it, writing a reason for each difference they accept. The system closes a period by itself only when the manager has turned that on, every condition is exact, no one judged lines by hand, and no one has revoked that period. Every close writes a snapshot of every line and can be undone in one step. Once a period is closed nothing new lands in it: late Bosta data is held for a manager, who reopens the period with a written reason, dismisses the data, or (once the accountant has posted the month) carries the difference into the open period. A close is revoked with a reason, never deleted. The brand's own records (orders, confirmations, shipments, carrier states) confirm per store and day by themselves once the day has ended and a sweep after it agrees (spec 0007 section 3), so the 06:00 daily report can call yesterday's orders confirmed. One read, `ConfirmedDays`, answers both kinds for the dashboard and the report. The owner ruled on every question on 2026-11-13: the period length is a per brand setting (monthly by default, weekly, or per Bosta cash out), and every other recommendation is ratified.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As the owner, I want to know which months of Bosta money and which days of orders are confirmed and which are still provisional, so that I never decide on numbers nobody checked.
- As operations or the accountant, I want one screen that tells me what still stands between a period and its close, with Bosta's figure and ours side by side and every judgment made by hand listed, so that closing a month is a checklist and not a hunt.
- As the accountant, I want to mark a month as posted and have a late Bosta correction carried into the open month instead of reopening it, so that my books and the app agree.
- As a manager, I want a closed month to stay closed when Bosta sends late or corrected data, and to decide myself, with a written reason, what happens, so that a confirmed number never changes behind my back.
- As a manager, I want an automatic close to be undone in one step and to stay undone, so that a wrong automatic decision costs a minute.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract; each is checked in [verify.md](verify.md)):

*The close check*
- **AC-1** (the conditions): `GET /api/settlement/periods/:id/close-check` (permission `settlement.work`) answers, for a carrier account's open period, one row per condition below, each `{ key, state: 'met' | 'differs' | 'blocked' | 'not_applicable', appFigure, carrierFigure, difference, count, days, nextStep }` with amounts as decimal strings, plus `checkHash` (SHA 256 of the canonical JSON of every row, the figures of AC-2, and the period's live line digest, AC-6) and `ruleVersion` (`carrier-v2`). For a closed period it answers the stored evidence of its latest close, never a recomputation (review m5). Conditions:
  - (C1) `lines_settled`: every live line whose `occurred_on` the period holds has a head match of `matched`, `accounted`, or `cleared`; `blocked` otherwise, with the count and amount of the rest by reason. A `non_forward_cash` line (Bosta Exchange cash) is matched to the replacement order once spec 0020 is amended for owner decision Q-21.13 (review M3); until then its next step reads "Match it to the replacement order, with a reason code".
  - (C2) `cash_outs_in_bank`: every `deposit` line of the period is `received_in_bank`, `received_difference_accepted`, or `returned_to_wallet` (a cash out Bosta's bank transfer sent back, linked by a person to the reversing wallet line of the same amount, requested of spec 0020 AC-32, review M10); `blocked` otherwise.
  - (C3) `sources_agree`: no open `settlement.sources_disagree` task on a statement with a live line in the period; `blocked` otherwise.
  - (C4) `carrier_sheet_days` (review B1): for every day of the period, the sheet day totals (*Data model*: stored on apply by spec 0020, requested) of the latest received live sheet covering that day, per group, equal the app's live, not cleared lines of that day and group: `cash_cycle` (the cash cycle kinds of spec 0020 AC-9, net) and `wallet_only` (`compensation`, `adjustment`, `deposit`, `other_fee` `insurance`, `other_fee` `packing_material`, and any type a mapping marks `wallet_only`, net). A sheet may span periods and overlapping sheets count each day once. `differs` lists the days that differ and the days no sheet covers that hold a line; `differs` with "No Bosta sheet for this period" when no sheet covers any day.
  - (C5) `day_totals_split`: where a sheet carries Bosta's daily Collected Cash Cycle and Bosta Fees rows (a `per_transaction` layout), the stored day evidence of kind `collected_cash` equals the period's `cod_collected` lines of that day, and `bosta_fees` equals the fee kinds of that day, with the kinds fixed by the real sheet check before milestone 3 (Follow-up); `not_applicable` with "This sheet has no day totals" when no sheet of the period carries them (review M4).
  - (C6) `carrier_deposits_agree`: every `carrier_api` statement with a live line in the period has `deposited_amount` equal to the sum of its live lines (spec 0020 AC-21); `differs` otherwise.
  - (C7) `wallet_rolls_forward`: an opening and a closing balance are known and the roll forward equals the closing one. The opening balance is the latest observation on or before the day before `starts_on` with no live line between it and that day; the closing balance is the latest observation on or before `ends_on` with no live line between it and `ends_on`, or a sheet's balance column on its last row of the period (review m1). Cash outs are negative lines and are not subtracted again (spec 0020 AC-31). `differs` otherwise ("opening balance not recorded" is `differs`, never 0).
- **AC-2** (figures recorded, not conditions): the check also answers, as figures for the record and never as conditions, each read as of `ends_on`: spec 0020's "owed by Bosta at period end" with its overdue part; cash outs not seen in the bank by `ends_on`; the closing wallet balance not cashed out (review m2); approved compensation not received; pre go live lines; the manual couriers line; and Bosta's tax invoice for the month when one is recorded (AC-28): its number, fees net of VAT, and VAT, beside the period's fee and VAT lines, with the difference (review M12). It also answers a person's judgments in the period (review M1): lines matched by a person with a difference (count, total difference), lines accounted by a person (count and amount by account code), and fees accepted above contract (spec 0020 AC-37), each with who did it. Cash still in transit at the end of a month never blocks a close.

*Closing*
- **AC-3** (a person closes): a holder of `settlement.close` closes a period with `POST /api/settlement/periods/:id/close` (`expectedCheckHash`, `acceptances: [{ conditionKey, reasonCode, carrierReference? }]`, `note`). In one transaction under `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the period row, the check is computed again; a hash different from `expectedCheckHash` answers 409 `CHECK_CHANGED` with the new check; any `blocked` condition answers 409 `CLOSE_BLOCKED`; a `differs` condition with no acceptance answers 422 `ACCEPTANCE_MISSING`, and an acceptance for a condition that is `met` or `not_applicable` answers 422 `ACCEPTANCE_NOT_NEEDED`. A reason code comes from `PERIOD_ACCEPTANCE_REASONS` (`sheet_not_downloaded`, `carrier_sheet_not_issued`, `sheet_has_no_day_totals`, `carrier_figure_wrong_claimed` (needs `carrierReference`, the Bosta ticket or claim number, at most 100 characters, review m3), `rounding_within_one_pound`, `balance_not_shown_by_carrier`, `day_dates_differ`, `other`); `note` (10 to 500 characters) is required when any acceptance is given. When the closer is the person who made any judgment of AC-2, the Close tab says so plainly ("You matched 14 of these lines yourself") without blocking. On success it inserts a `closed` event (`actor_type` `person`) whose `evidence` is the `PeriodCloseEvidence` of AC-6 with the acceptances, writes the snapshot (AC-6), writes the audit entry `settlement.period_closed` with the note as its reason text, and closes the period's open `settlement.period_ready_to_review` task. An already closed period answers 409 `PERIOD_ALREADY_CLOSED`.
- **AC-4** (the system closes on exact evidence only): the job `settlement-period-auto-close` (after each run of spec 0020's match job, and daily at 05:00 `Africa/Cairo`) closes a period by itself only when every one of these holds: the setting `settlement_auto_close` is on (default off; AC-5 offers it after the first close, review m6); every condition C1 to C7 is `met` or `not_applicable` (C4 is never `not_applicable`), and no acceptance is ever made by the system; the period has no person matched line with a difference and no person accounted line (review M1); today is at least `settlement_auto_close_wait_days` (default 5) working days after `ends_on` (AC-27, review M7); the account already has at least one period closed by a person; and the period has never been revoked, or its latest close is a person's close made after its latest revoke (review B2: after an undo, a manager's revoke, or an admit, only a person may close that period again). It writes the same event (`actor_type` `rule`, `actor_id` null), evidence, and snapshot as AC-3, the audit entry `settlement.period_closed` marked `automatic` and `undoable` with the undo handler `settlement.period_close_undo` and `undo_args` `{ periodId, eventId }`, and opens `settlement.period_closed_automatically` (AC-17).
- **AC-5** (prompts): when a period's `ends_on` plus the wait has passed and it is still open, the job opens `settlement.period_ready_to_review` (hat operations, permission `settlement.close`, linked to the period, dedupe `period_review:<period id>`), whose page is the period's Close tab; the system closes the task when the period closes. On the first working day after the expected end of an account's next period (by the cadence of AC-29), when live lines of that range belong to no period, it opens `settlement.period_to_create` (hat operations, permission `settlement.close`, linked to the account, dedupe `period_create:<account>:<expected start>`) with the cadence's range ready, closed when a period holds those lines (review M8). After an account's first close by a person, it opens `settlement.auto_close_offer` (hat manager, permission `settings.manage`, dedupe per tenant) asking "This period was closed by hand. Turn on automatic close for the next ones?", closed when the manager answers (review m6).
- **AC-6** (evidence and the snapshot): `PeriodCloseEvidence` (Zod, `packages/contracts/src/settlement/period-close.ts`) holds `ruleVersion`, `closedBy` (`person` or `rule`), every condition row of AC-1 as computed in the closing transaction, the acceptances, the AC-2 figures and judgments, the AC-21 totals of spec 0020 as of the close, every statement with a live line in the period (`id`, `kind`, `external_reference`, `sha256`, `statement_total`, `deposited_amount`), the sheet day totals used, `lineCount`, `linesDigest`, and `checkHash`. The snapshot is one `settlement_period_close_lines` row per live line of the period at the close (line, head match id, head result, amount, kind, tracking number), and `linesDigest` is SHA 256 over the sorted `line_id:head_match_id:result:amount` strings, so `GET /api/settlement/periods/:id/history` shows, for any past close, exactly what was confirmed and whether today's lines still agree.

*The gate: nothing lands in a closed period*
- **AC-7** (the database refuses): new triggers (requested of spec 0002, expand only) refuse, with the error `PeriodClosed`, an insert into `settlement_lines` whose `occurred_on` falls in a closed period of its account, into `settlement_line_matches` for a line in a closed period (the superseded clear included, which settles spec 0002's open question on it), into `carrier_cash_out_receipts` for a deposit line in a closed period, into `carrier_wallet_balances` with `observed_on` inside a closed period, and into `settlement_day_evidence` and the sheet day totals for a day inside a closed period (review m5). Each trigger reads the period with `FOR SHARE`, so it waits for a close in progress (which holds `FOR UPDATE`) and then sees its result: nothing lands in a period between its check and its close.
- **AC-8** (a late cash cycle is held, not lost): when spec 0020's carrier import (on an event or in the sweep) would write lines for a delivery whose `occurred_on` falls in a closed period, it writes nothing for that delivery, records one `settlement_held_arrivals` row (`source` `cash_cycle`, `source_ref` the statement reference spec 0020 would use, for example `bosta-cc:<id>:<tracking>:r2`, the period, the tracking number, the deposit day, and the amounts by line kind of both the live statement and the arrival), deduplicated on (`account_id`, `source_ref`), and opens `settlement.arrival_in_closed_period` (hat manager, permission `settlement.reopen`, linked to the period, dedupe `period_arrivals:<period id>`, one task per period listing every held arrival). The order's cash state does not move.
- **AC-9** (a sheet that reaches a closed period, review M5): spec 0020's file preview classes each row that falls in a closed period: a `confirmed` row (the app already holds it with equal amounts) and a balance equal to the stored one are `closed_period_unchanged`, skipped, and counted ("312 rows in October already confirmed, skipped"); a `new` or `differs` row and a balance that differs are `closed_period_changes`. Apply writes every row outside closed periods as spec 0020 AC-8 does, writes nothing for the skipped rows, and records each changing row as one held arrival (`source` `file_row`, `source_ref` `<sha256>:<row number>`, with the row's amounts), opening the same task as AC-8. Any holder of `settlement.work` can apply such a file; nothing in a closed period changes until a manager answers.
- **AC-10** (answering a held arrival): a holder of `settlement.reopen` answers each held arrival with `POST /api/settlement/held-arrivals/:id/answer` (`answer`, `reasonCode`, `note` 10 to 500 characters, `expectedPeriodEventId`). The dialog shows the live statement's amounts by kind beside the arrival's, the order and its cash state, and what each answer would change in the period's totals (review m4). Answers: `admit` revokes the period (AC-11, reason `late_arrival_admitted`) unless it is already open, then imports the arrival through spec 0020's import service (reading the delivery back, or the stored file row) in the same transaction; `dismiss` (reason codes `duplicate_of_existing`, `carrier_sent_in_error`, `other`) imports nothing; `carry_forward`, offered only when the period is marked posted (AC-26, review M6), writes one `adjustment` line on the account dated today's operating day (so in the open period that holds today), its amount the arrival's net minus the delivery's carried basis (the live net plus every amount already carried forward for that delivery, so a second revision is never counted twice, second pass S1), recording the arrival's net as the new basis, accounted to the account code `late_correction` with the tracking number, the closed period, and the arrival as its reference, leaves the closed period and its snapshot as they are and the order's cash state unmoved (the order page lists the late correction beside its cash state), and needs a today that no closed period holds (else 409 `NO_OPEN_PERIOD_TODAY`). Each answer is one row in the append only `settlement_held_arrival_answers` and one audit entry `settlement.held_arrival_answered`. The task closes when the period has no unanswered arrival. A stale `expectedPeriodEventId` answers 409 `EVENT_STALE`; an answered arrival 409 `ALREADY_ANSWERED`; `carry_forward` on a period not posted 422 `NOT_POSTED`.
- **AC-11** (revoke with a reason, never delete): `POST /api/settlement/periods/:id/revoke` (`reasonCode` from `PERIOD_REVOKE_REASONS`: `late_arrival_admitted`, `error_found_after_close`, `carrier_corrected_statement`, `automatic_close_undone`, `other`; `note` 10 to 500 characters, required except for `automatic_close_undone`; `expectedEventId` the latest close event) inserts a `revoked` event with the reason code, writes the audit entry `settlement.period_revoked` with the note as its reason text, and so sets the period `open`; the period, its events, its evidence, and its snapshot stay. It needs `settlement.reopen` (manager), except `automatic_close_undone` of a period whose latest close is a `rule` close, which `settlement.close` may also do (AC-12). A period that is not closed answers 409 `NOT_CLOSED`; a stale event id 409 `EVENT_STALE`. A revoke of a period marked posted opens `settlement.posted_period_reopened` (hat operations, permission `settlement.close`, for the accountant) naming the reason and what changed (review M6). Closing again after a revoke writes a new close with a new snapshot, and only a person may make it (AC-4).
- **AC-12** (one step undo of an automatic close, one path, review m7): the period's Close tab shows, on a period closed by the rule, "Closed automatically on <day>: Undo". Both that button and `/audit` call spec 0010's `POST /api/audit/entries/:id/undo` on the close's audit entry; its handler `settlement.period_close_undo` calls AC-11's service with `automatic_close_undone` and refuses with `undoCode` `period_changed_since` when the period's latest event is no longer that close. Either way the result is one `revoked` event, the audit entry `settlement.period_closed_undone` linked by `undoes_entry_id`, and the period's tasks reopen as if never closed. The Close tab's button needs `settlement.close`; spec 0010's route checks `audit.undo` or, for this one action, `settlement.close` (requested of spec 0010's registry entry).
- **AC-13** (a person's actions on a closed period): spec 0020's match, account, unmatch, claim, bank receipt, accept difference, returned to wallet, wallet balance, and rate card routes answer 409 `PERIOD_CLOSED` (with the period and "A manager can reopen it") for a line or day in a closed period, before the database trigger would; the rate card recheck job skips lines in closed periods and reports their count as "not rechecked: period closed"; spec 0020's opening cutoff stays as built (changeable only while no period of the account is closed). Creating a period cannot overlap a closed one (the built exclusion).

*Provisional and confirmed everywhere*
- **AC-14** (the settlement reads): `CarrierSettlementReads` (spec 0020) answers `provisional: false` for a figure only when every line it sums lies in closed periods, and `provisional: true` otherwise; `periodTotals` of a closed period answers `provisional: false` and the close event id. Features 24 and 25 never show a confirmed figure for an order with an open blocker.

*Own records days (spec 0007 section 3; asked by specs 0023 and 0024)*
- **AC-20** (what a confirmed own records day is): the brand's own records fed by API (orders, confirmations, shipments, carrier states) are confirmed per store and operating day (`Africa/Cairo`). A day's state is its latest row in the append only `own_record_day_events` (`confirmed` or `revoked`); no row means provisional. Confirming says "the app holds every record of that day the connections can give"; later changes to those records (a cancel, a delivery, a cash move) are their own later facts and never revoke the day.
- **AC-21** (the day confirms itself): the job `own-records-day-confirm` (every 15 minutes from 00:15 to 07:00 `Africa/Cairo`, then hourly) confirms store S's day D when D has ended (at spec 0010's operating day cutoff, not at midnight, second pass S6) and, for every connection serving S that is not archived (its Shopify connections, and every Bosta connection bound to S or unbound), the connection's evidence read through `ConnectorsService.dayEvidence(connectionId, D)` (requested of specs 0015 and 0018) shows: the connection active (a paused, disconnected, expired, or failing connection blocks, second pass S3); completed sweeps whose covered window (`coveredFrom`, `coveredThrough`: the source times the sweep's cursor walked) spans all of D, from no later than D's start to at least 15 minutes after D's end (beyond the Shopify sweep's 10 minute overlap; a sweep that merely ran after D is not enough, second pass S2); and no inbound event received on or before the last such sweep still `pending` or `failed`. Only a store that never had a connection confirms when D ends. It writes a `confirmed` event (`actor_type` `rule`) with evidence `{ ruleVersion: 'own-v1', connections: [{ id, provider, state, coveredFrom, coveredThrough, pending, failed }], counts: { ordersCreated, confirmed, shipped } }`, the audit entry `day_close.own_records_confirmed` marked `automatic` and `undoable` (handler `day_close.own_records_confirm_undo`), and publishes `OwnRecordsDayConfirmed`. With both connectors sweeping every 5 minutes, a normal day confirms by about 00:30, well before the 06:00 report. The rule may confirm a day again only after a revoke the rule itself made (AC-23), because a later clean sweep is new evidence; after an undo or a person's revoke, only a person may confirm that day again, as for periods (second pass S5).
- **AC-22** (a day that does not confirm): when store S's day D is still provisional `own_records_confirm_late_hours` (default 6) hours after it ended, the job opens `day_close.own_records_late` (hat operations, permission `records.confirm_day`, linked to the store, dedupe `own_day:<store>:<day>`) naming the blocking connection and why (not swept, not covered, paused or disconnected, N failed events). A holder of the new permission `records.confirm_day` (operations and manager hats, requested of spec 0008; not the settlement only accountant hat, second pass S7; until it lands, the manager) confirms it by hand with `POST /api/day-close/own-records/:storeId/:day/confirm` (`reasonCode` from `OWN_DAY_ACCEPTANCE_REASONS`: `failed_events_reviewed`, `connection_paused_known`, `other`; `note` 10 to 500 characters; `expectedEvidenceHash`), writing a `confirmed` event (`actor_type` `person`) with the evidence as it stood; the task closes on any confirm.
- **AC-23** (records found late revoke the day): when a sweep or a replayed event creates a record (an order, a delivery) whose own date falls in a confirmed day of its store (a webhook the app never got), the creating transaction also writes a `revoked` event for that day (`actor_type` `rule`, reason `sweep_found_missing`), audited, so the day reads provisional until the next run confirms it again. The same holds for a late change (second pass S4): an inbound event whose source time (Shopify's `updated_at`, Bosta's state time) falls in a confirmed day and that reaches the app only after the day confirmed (a cancel, an edit, or a carrier state the webhooks missed) revokes that day with reason `late_change_found`; an event whose source time is after the day is an ordinary later fact and revokes nothing. A Shopify backfill or an onboarding import (feature 15) that would create records dated in a confirmed day is refused with 409 `DAY_CONFIRMED` unless a holder of `settlement.reopen` sends `admitIntoConfirmedDays: { reasonCode, note }`, which revokes those days first (reason `late_import_admitted`).
- **AC-24** (revoke and undo): a holder of `settlement.reopen` revokes a confirmed day with `POST /api/day-close/own-records/:storeId/:day/revoke` (`reasonCode` from `PERIOD_REVOKE_REASONS` plus `records_corrected`, `note`, `expectedEventId`); an automatic confirm is undone in one step through spec 0010's undo (from the day close page by a holder of `records.confirm_day`, or from `/audit`), and only a person confirms it again; nothing is ever deleted.
- **AC-25** (one read for reporting): `ConfirmedDays` (exported by the new `day-close` module) answers `confirmedThrough({ dataClass: 'own_records', storeId })` (the last day D such that every day from the store's go live day to D is confirmed now) and `confirmedThrough({ dataClass: 'third_party_money', accountId })` (`PeriodConfirmations.confirmedThrough`), and `rangeStatus({ dataClass, scope, from, to })` answering `allConfirmed`, the provisional days, and the days whose latest event is a revoke (`revokedDays`). Reporting (specs 0023, 0024) never writes or decides a confirmation; it only reads this service. `GET /api/day-close/own-records?storeId=&from=&to=` (permission `orders.view`) answers each day's state, how it confirmed, and its reason; the page `/day-close` (operations and manager) shows each store's last 35 days with Confirm and Revoke as the hat allows, in both languages, keyboard only, axe clean.

*The accountant's side*
- **AC-26** (posted, review M6): a holder of `settlement.close` marks a closed period as posted with `POST /api/settlement/periods/:id/posted` (`exportId?` the audit entry of the export used, `postedOn`, `expectedEventId`), one append only `settlement_period_postings` row per close event, audited `settlement.period_posted`. The mark belongs to that close: after a revoke and a new close, the period is not posted until marked again. A posted period's held arrivals offer `carry_forward` (AC-10), and its revoke opens the accountant's task (AC-11).
- **AC-27** (working days, review M7): the wait of AC-4 and AC-5 counts working days of the brand calendar: the setting `business_weekend_days` (default Friday and Saturday) and `public_holidays` (a list of dates the manager keeps, empty by default, the runbook naming Eid and the fixed holidays to enter each year). With the defaults, the fifth working day after Monday 30 November 2026 is Monday 7 December 2026.
- **AC-29** (period length, owner decision Q-23.10): the setting `settlement_period_cadence` (per brand, `monthly` default, `weekly`, `per_cash_out`) decides the range the create task (AC-5) and spec 0020's period shortcut propose: `monthly` a calendar month; `weekly` seven days starting on the first working day after the weekend of AC-27 (Sunday with the defaults); `per_cash_out` from the day after the last period's end to the deposit day of the next cash out (`deposit` line) after it. A change of the setting applies from the next period only: periods already created keep their ranges, and the next proposed period starts the day after the account's latest period ends and follows the new cadence (a first partial range is allowed, for example the days left in the month before a weekly cadence starts). A person may still create a period of any range (spec 0020). The change is audited (`settings` audit of spec 0009).
- **AC-28** (Bosta's tax invoice, review M12): a holder of `settlement.work` records Bosta's tax invoice for an account (`POST /api/settlement/accounts/:id/carrier-invoices`: `invoiceNumber`, `issuedOn`, `coversFrom`, `coversTo`, `feesNet`, `vat`, `evidenceRef`), one append only `carrier_invoices` row, corrected by a superseding row with a reason; AC-2 shows the invoices whose cover range lies in the period as a figure beside the fee and VAT lines, never a condition.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option B in [rationale.md](rationale.md): close with evidence on the built settlement periods, inside the settlement module: a pure close check of named conditions that compares the sheet with the app day by day, a person's close with written acceptances or a rule's close only on exact evidence and never after a revoke, a line by line snapshot, database triggers that keep every write out of a closed period, held arrivals for late carrier data and changing sheet rows, a posted mark that lets a correction be carried forward, and the provisional flag read from period status; plus a small `day-close` module that confirms own records days from the connectors' sweeps and answers `ConfirmedDays` for reporting.

**Implementation skills**: `nestjs-best-practices` (`kadajett/agent-nestjs-skills`, `.claude/skills/nestjs-best-practices/`) · `drizzle-orm-patterns` (`giuseppe-trisciuoglio/developer-kit`, `.claude/skills/drizzle-orm-patterns/`) · `postgresql-table-design` (`wshobson/agents`, `.claude/skills/postgresql-table-design/`) · `pg-boss-v12` (`sncollective/snc-platform`, `.claude/skills/pg-boss-v12/`) · `nextjs-app-router-patterns` (`wshobson/agents`, `.claude/skills/nextjs-app-router-patterns/`)

**Decided by the architect** (expertise, not owner questions; runner up in brackets):
- The close check is a pure function over a read model, run once for the screen and again inside the closing transaction, and the person sends back the hash of what they saw, so nobody closes on numbers that changed under them [a stored check run: one more table that goes stale].
- The sheet is compared with the app per day and per group, not as one file total, because Bosta's wallet sheet keeps its daily cash cycle money as evidence rows, sheets span periods, and staff download overlapping ranges (review B1) [one file total against the period's lines: differs every real month].
- The gate is enforced by database triggers that take `FOR SHARE` on the period row, with the close taking `FOR UPDATE`, plus early 409s in the services for a readable message [service checks only: a job or a future writer could slip a line in between the check and the close].
- A sheet row that repeats what a closed period already holds is skipped; only a changing row is held (review M5) [refuse the whole file: a "last 60 days" download would reopen a confirmed month for nothing].
- After any revoke only a person may close the period again, because a revoke records a judgment the conditions cannot see (review B2) [let the rule close again: an undo would last one job run].
- The snapshot is one row per line, not only a digest, because the History tab and any later dispute need to show what was confirmed [a digest only: proves change, cannot show what changed].
- Undo of an automatic close always goes through spec 0010's undo service, so the audit shows it undone however it was pressed (review m7) [a feature only undo: two paths, two audits].
- Period close lives inside `apps/api/src/settlement/close/`, since period events are settlement's own tables; own records days live in a new `day-close` module, because they are not money and read the connectors and orders [own records in settlement: settlement would read connector health and order counts it has no business with; in reporting: reporting must only read a confirmation].

### Lifecycles

- Period: `open` → `closed` (a person's close, or the rule's when AC-4 allows) → `open` (a revoke, an undo, or an admit) → `closed` again only by a person, any number of times; a close may carry one posted mark; every step is an event or a row; nothing is deleted.
- Held arrival: `open` (no answer) → `admitted`, `dismissed`, or `carried_forward` (one answer, final).
- Own records day: `provisional` (no row) → `confirmed` (rule or person) → `provisional` (revoked by a late record, a person, or an undo) → `confirmed` again by the next clean run or a person.

### The jobs

- `settlement-period-auto-close` on the `settlement` queue (through `JobRegistry`, install timezone): sent after each run of `settlement-carrier-match` and at 05:00 `Africa/Cairo`. For each open period of each carrier account, oldest first, at most 50 a run, in its own transaction: lock the period `FOR UPDATE`, compute the check, close only when AC-4 holds, otherwise open or keep the review task (AC-5); then open the create and offer prompts (AC-5). A failure on one period is logged (no codes or amounts personal to a customer) and the run goes on.
- `own-records-day-confirm` on a `day-close` queue: AC-21's schedule, each store's unconfirmed days of the last 7 days, oldest first.

### Key invariants

- A closed period's lines, matches, cash out receipts, wallet balances, and day evidence never change while it is closed; the database refuses it.
- Every close has evidence and a snapshot; every revoke has a reason code; nothing about a period or a day is ever deleted.
- The system never accepts a difference, never closes over a judgment made by hand, and never closes a period a person revoked; only a person does those, with a written note.
- Late carrier data is never dropped: it is held, and a manager admits, dismisses, or (after posting) carries it forward.
- A figure is confirmed only when every record under it is confirmed: lines in closed periods, own records in confirmed days; an order's confirmed lines never hide its open cash blockers.

### Security model

- Staff only. Reading the check and history: `settlement.work`. Closing, marking posted, and confirming an own records day by hand: `settlement.close` (operations, manager, the accountant hat of spec 0020 Q-22.11). Revoking, answering held arrivals, and admitting late imports: `settlement.reopen` (manager, requested); undoing a rule's close or confirm: `settlement.close` too. Order confirmations and the own records read: `orders.view`.
- The close's evidence holds statement references, checksums, and amounts; no customer name, phone, or address. Notes are masked by spec 0010 and live only in audit entries.
- The audit undo needs `audit.undo` (manager), except the two automatic actions named in the API table.

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- "Confirmed" has one meaning across the app: the period is closed (or the day confirmed) and its snapshot is still true; every later report and export reads it from one service.
- Late or corrected Bosta data can no longer change a confirmed month silently; it waits for a manager, and a posted month takes a correction the way an accountant books one.
- Closing a month becomes a checklist with both figures side by side and every hand judgment named, which an outside accountant can run.
- Reporting gets confirmed orders by about 00:30 each day without deciding anything itself.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- Six new insert triggers on hot settlement tables add a period lookup per row; the lookup uses the built exclusion's gist index and should stay small, but the import's batch timing must be measured in milestone 2.
- A manager is needed for every late arrival into a closed month; a brand with frequent Bosta revisions will see these tasks often.
- The automatic close needs a person's first close, the manager's opt in, and a month with no hand judgments; many months will be closed by hand.
- The brand must keep its public holidays in a setting, or the wait is short over Eid.
- With the default monthly periods the daily report's money figures stay provisional for weeks; a brand that wants faster confirmation picks weekly or per cash out (Q-23.10).
- Several small requests to specs 0002, 0008, 0009, 0010, 0015, 0016, 0018, and 0020 must land for the full design.

**Neutral**:
- Spec 0020's provisional label now has a way to become confirmed; its AC-22 text ("no period can be closed") stays true only until this feature lands.
- Spec 0008 gains one permission, spec 0016 three linked types if missing, and the module map one module, `day-close`.
