## Summary

This feature decides what happens when the door does not go as planned. Every failed attempt Bosta reports becomes a row with one reason from a short list an operations team uses (customer unreachable, postponed, refused, address problem, cash problem, carrier side, unclassified), worked out from Bosta's code or its free text (often Arabic) by rules the team can teach, and it opens one call task, due before the courier's next run, that stays open until the customer actually answers and records how Bosta was told. Every piece coming back is expected on the warehouse's list: a return to origin from the moment Bosta turns the delivery into a return, a customer return pickup or an exchange pickup from the moment Bosta shows it, and a bag nobody expected through "a parcel that is not on the list". Pieces are received one by one (by a tap until feature 20 brings the scan) back into stock through the orders module, never by editing stock. Every Bosta charge lands on the order and its campaign, including the FlexShip refusal fee as an offset, a partial delivery is split by a person when Bosta reports less cash than expected, a lost or damaged parcel opens a claim that spec 0020 later matches to Bosta's compensation, an order handed to any other courier can be followed by hand, and daily checks make sure every packed or shipped order reaches an ending. Bosta's fee is paid on every parcel, so a failed delivery's reason decides who bears it: the brand (wrong item or size, damaged or not as shown, price or COD wrong), Bosta through a claim (late, or never reached the customer), or the customer, who is then flagged and pays nothing more than any FlexShip fee Bosta took at the door. A swap after delivery can ride one Bosta Exchange parcel: the replacement order's new piece goes out and the old piece comes back on the same delivery, with the price difference collected at the door.

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As a support agent, I want one call task before the courier's next run after a failed attempt, showing the reason in my words, both phones, and what can still be changed with Bosta, and to try again later when nobody answers, so that the customer is ready for the next try or tells me she refuses before the courier wastes a trip.
- As the operations lead, I want unknown courier text to land as "unclassified" instead of breaking anything, and to teach a phrase once so the next one is sorted, so that the reasons stay useful while Bosta's code list stays unpublished.
- As a warehouse person, I want a list of every parcel coming back (returns to origin, return pickups, exchanges) with what is inside, a way to record a bag that is not on the list, and to tap (later scan) each piece back onto the shelf, so that every returned piece is sellable again the same day and nothing comes back twice.
- As the owner and the media buyer, I want every Bosta charge of a failed delivery on the order and its campaign, and the returned to origin rate by governorate, city, product, campaign, refusal reason, and carrier, with returns counted from the moment Bosta turns them around, so that I see what refusals really cost and where they come from before I scale a campaign.
- As the accountant, I want a partial delivery split into delivered and returned lines with the cash the customer owed for what she kept, the prepaid money of a piece that never reached her shown as owed back, a FlexShip refusal fee matched to its charge, and a lost parcel's claim ready to match Bosta's compensation, so that the settlement agrees line by line.
- As a brand that sometimes uses its own driver, another courier, or an office pickup, I want to record the handover, the result at the door, and the cash the driver brought back for the whole run, so that such an order does not stay packed and unpaid for ever.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract; each is checked in [verify.md](verify.md)):

*Failed attempts and the next try*
- **AC-1** (one attempt row per failed attempt): on `CarrierAttemptFailed` (spec 0018) for a delivery linked to an order, the deliveries module inserts one `delivery_attempts` row in the publisher's transaction (order, carrier delivery, attempt number, attempt time, Bosta exception code when given), `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` on (`carrier_delivery_id`, `attempt_number`), so a replayed read adds nothing. Spec 0018 counts attempts from `numberOfAttempts` even when `state.exception[]` has fewer entries; such an attempt has no text and is classified `unclassified` (review m3). The courier's reason text is never copied into this module: the classifier reads it once through `CarrierDeliveries.attemptsOf(deliveryId)` in memory, and the order page reads it the same way. A delivery not yet linked is attempted again on `CarrierDeliveryLinked`. A flip to return to origin with no attempt row on that delivery inserts one (`attempt_number` the larger of 1 and Bosta's count, `unclassified`) and opens the call task of AC-4 on it with the text "find out why it is coming back", so the call has an attempt to record its contacts on (second pass n4).
- **AC-2** (the reason vocabulary, tolerant of unknown text): every attempt gets exactly one reason of `DeliveryFailureReason` (*Reason vocabulary*): `customer_unreachable`, `customer_postponed`, `customer_refused`, `address_problem`, `cash_problem`, `carrier_side`, `unclassified`, each labelled in the catalog in plain Egyptian Arabic as an agent says it. The classifier (pure, `failure-reason-classifier.ts`) tries, in order: an active rule for the carrier and the exact exception code; then active phrase rules on the normalized reason text (lower case, Arabic letter forms unified, diacritics and tatweel removed, digits and punctuation dropped, spaces collapsed), longest phrase first; else `unclassified`. A `customer_refused` attempt also gets a `RefusedReasonCode` from the same rules, `other` when none says: `wrong_item` (the brand sent the wrong item or size, added 2026-11-13), `damaged` (labelled "damaged or not as shown"), `price` (labelled "price or COD wrong": not what she was told), `size` (does not fit, or she wants another size), `changed_mind` (including "too expensive" when the price was what she was told), `not_ordered`, `other`. The first three are brand side for AC-33. Empty text, text in any language, a code no rule names, or no code at all never throws and never blocks the order; the row records how it was classified (`code_rule`, `phrase_rule`, `person`, `none`). The seeded phrases are replaced, before milestone 2, by the pilot or reference brand's real distinct reason texts (review m1); until then they are marked *Assumed*.
- **AC-3** (people classify and teach): a person with `orders.confirm` sets or corrects an attempt's reason on `POST /api/delivery-attempts/:id/classify` (audited `delivery_attempt.classified`, classified by `person`). A holder of `channels.map` (operations and the manager: mapping outside text to the brand's own codes) lists, adds, and retires phrase rules on `/api/failure-reason-rules` (a phrase of 2 to 60 characters after normalization), sees the unclassified attempts of the last 30 days grouped by normalized text with counts (read through `attemptsOf`, shown only on that screen, never stored), and makes a phrase rule from one group in one action (audited `failure_reason_rule.added`; the grouped screen may slip past the pilot, *Later than the pilot*, while per attempt classify and the seeded real texts stay); exception code rules need `settings.manage` (review m2). A new rule applies to later attempts only; it never rewrites a classified attempt.
- **AC-4** (one call task, before the next run): each new attempt opens or refreshes one task `delivery.failed_attempt` per order (spec 0016, hat support, permission `orders.confirm`, dedupe `delivery_failed_attempt:<orderId>`), due at the earlier of the attempt time plus `delivery_failed_attempt_call_hours` (default 4) working hours, and `delivery_failed_attempt_call_by_hour` (default 10:00) on the next operating day (review M2). It shows the attempt number of `delivery_max_attempts` (default 3), the reason, both of the order's phones (`ship_to_phone`, `ship_to_phone_alt`, read on the order view, never in the task label), feature 18's WhatsApp deep link, and the next step for the delivery's state from spec 0018's *Who can still fix it* table (at Exception, 47: nobody can change COD or address; the phone only through a Bosta support ticket). While `deliveries.carrier_failures` is runnable this task replaces spec 0018's attention tasks for the same attempt (review M1; the list is in Follow-up). No text, task, event, or audit entry says the app told, rescheduled, or updated Bosta.
- **AC-5** (what the customer said, and who told Bosta): each call is one append only `delivery_attempt_contacts` row recorded on `POST /api/delivery-attempts/:id/contacts`: an outcome of `will_receive` (with the expected next try day, default the next carrier delivery day), `rescheduled` (a day at most `delivery_reschedule_max_days`, default 5, ahead), `details_corrected` (a phone or address to pass to Bosta support), `refuses` (with a `RefusedReasonCode`), `size_swap_requested`, `cancel_requested`, `courier_never_came` (she says the courier never called or came, added 2026-11-13 for AC-33), or `no_answer`; audited `delivery_attempt.contact_recorded`. `no_answer` keeps the task open and moves its due time `delivery_no_answer_retry_hours` (default 2) later; the first other outcome closes it; there is no limit on contacts (review M2). The hourly check raises `delivery.next_try_missed` (hat operations) for an order whose expected next try day plus `delivery_next_try_grace_days` (default 1) has passed with no new attempt and no carrier state change since, counting only carrier delivery days (weekdays not in `carrier_non_delivery_weekdays`, default none, review m11), and says whether Bosta was told.

*Every carrier charge on the order*
- **AC-11** (the charges on the order and its campaign): each carrier charge of an order is a `delivery_charges` row with its leg: `forward` for a send that ended delivered or partly delivered; `round_trip` for a delivery that went out and came back as returned to origin on the same tracking number (Bosta charges the parcel once, with a fee only, negative deposit: rationale E5); `return` for a separate return leg with its own charge; `return_pickup` and `exchange_pickup` for a customer return pickup (25) and an exchange (30) delivery of the order (AC-29; an exchange's charge sits on the exchange replacement whose forward delivery it is, AC-34); and `refusal_fee` when Bosta's cash cycle for a refused or returned to origin delivery carries a COD above 0: Bosta's FlexShip amount (at most 150 EGP by Bosta's terms) that the courier collected from the customer on a refused send (rationale E11). It is never recorded as an order collection, so the order stays `nothing_to_collect` (review M9). Sign convention (spec 0020 review M4): every row's `amount` is positive and its leg says which way the money goes, as settlement's lines do: the cost legs (`forward`, `return`, `round_trip`, `return_pickup`, `exchange_pickup`) are what the brand owes Bosta, and `refusal_fee` is money Bosta holds for the brand, so a 150.00 refusal fee is `refusal_fee` 150.00 and agrees with settlement's +150.00 line. An order's net carrier cost is the sum of its current cost legs minus its current `refusal_fee` rows; `DeliveryCharges` answers both and the net. The amount of every other leg is Bosta's own total for the delivery (`bostaFees`, shipping plus opening package plus FlexShip service fee plus VAT) from `CarrierCashCycleRecorded`, with the VAT part beside it. A later cash cycle revision appends a row that supersedes the earlier one; nothing is updated. The row carries the order's store, the order's campaign id (`source_campaign_id`, copied when written), and the deposit day as its operating day. Until Bosta's cash cycle arrives the order page shows "shipping cost pending", never 0. `GET /api/orders/:id/delivery-charges` answers the legs and their total; `DeliveryCharges.byCampaign({ from, to, storeId? })` answers totals by campaign and leg for feature 24. A manual courier's charge is entered by a person (AC-24).

*Partial delivery and the partial cash*
- **AC-12** (the signal, owner decisions Q-21.4 and Q-21.14): when Bosta reports delivered (45) with collected cash at least 0.01 below `cod_expected_amount`, the module first compares the collected cash with Bosta's own COD for the delivery (`cod_amount`, read through `CarrierDeliveries.forOrders`; second pass N1). (a) Collected equal to Bosta's `cod_amount` (within 0.01) and both below `cod_expected_amount`: a brand side difference, not a partial and not a courier shortfall. (b) Collected below Bosta's `cod_amount` on an order of two or more pieces: spec 0018 records the collection and holds the delivered move; `delivery.collection_differs` opens (hat operations, `orders.confirm`), replacing spec 0018's `collected_differs` task (review M1), showing what Bosta collected, Bosta's COD, what the order expects, the pieces, and spec 0018's stale carrier record when it is set. The hold is on the delivered move only (spec 0020 review M9): spec 0018 still calls `recordCollection` with what Bosta collected at once, so cash is `collected_by_carrier` while fulfillment stays `shipped` (requested from spec 0013: `recordCollection` accepts a carrier collection on a `shipped` or `failed_attempt` order), and settlement can match the cash before a person decides; while the move is held no claim can be opened for the order (AC-22, `PARTIAL_UNDECIDED`). (c) Collected below Bosta's `cod_amount` on a one piece order: not a partial; the delivered move applies and the shortfall below Bosta's COD drafts a `short_collection` claim (`CarrierClaims.openShortCollection`, AC-19). (d) Collected more than `cod_due_amount` by 0.01 or more applies the delivered move and opens `delivery.over_collection` (hat operations, `orders.confirm`) with two answers: `refund_customer` (the excess belongs to the customer: `PrepaidShareOwed` with cause `over_collection`, funding `cod_cash`, a refund owed, never a write off) or `belongs_to_brand` (with a reason code, for example a charge agreed at the door), audited (the two answers may slip past the pilot, *Later than the pilot*). (e) Cash collected on an order with nothing due (spec 0020 AC-38): when `CarrierCashCycleRecorded` carries a `cod` above 0 for the forward delivery of an order whose cash is `prepaid`, or `nothing_to_collect` with fulfillment `delivered` (a send, not a refusal, whose cash is AC-11's `refusal_fee`), the whole collected amount is an over collection: `delivery.over_collection` opens with cause `collected_with_nothing_due` and the text "the customer may have paid twice: check her payment and owe the difference back", its resolution is `over_collection_pending` until a person answers `refund_customer` or `belongs_to_brand` as in (d), and the order's cash state is not changed by this module.
- **AC-13** (record the split): a person resolves a held `delivery.collection_differs` on `POST /api/orders/:id/partial-delivery` with the delivered quantity of each line (`dryRun: true` first answers the split rows, the new `cod_due_amount`, and its difference from what Bosta collected), then commits: the module calls `recordPartialDelivery` (spec 0013 AC-16) with the lines, the held delivered time, and source the delivery, actor the person, audited `order.partial_delivery_split` with reason `carrier_report`. The returned rows become awaiting restock and join a `returned_parcels` row of cause `partial_delivery` in state `returning` (AC-8): it becomes `back_at_business` when a later linked return delivery of the order shows 46 or when a piece is received, because Bosta keeps no trail for the returned piece of a partial (rationale E6, review M8). A claim drafted for a partial's missing piece notes that Bosta shows the delivery as delivered, and the runbook says to attach the courier's collection receipt. The other resolution, "every piece was delivered, the cash is short", applies the held delivered move with the person as actor and reason `collection_short_confirmed`, and the shortfall below Bosta's own `cod_amount` drafts a `short_collection` claim (AC-19); a committed partial whose new `cod_due_amount` is still above what Bosta collected drafts one for the rest, again only for the part below Bosta's `cod_amount` (any part between Bosta's COD and the order's is the brand side difference of AC-12 (a)). A shortfall is written off only by deciding a `short_collection` claim or by `brand_side_write_off` (spec 0020 review M7, second pass N1). A prepaid order (nothing for Bosta to collect) cannot show the signal: a person may turn its `delivered` into `partially_delivered` within `partial_delivery_late_days` (default 14) of delivery while no refund or return is recorded on it (requested from spec 0013), with reason `door_refusal_reported_late` (this path may slip past the pilot, *Later than the pilot*; an unexpected parcel, AC-30 (b), still catches the returned piece).

*Money of pieces that never reach the customer (domain review D2)*
- **AC-14** (the prepaid share, owner decision Q-21.3): on `LinesReturnedToOrigin` with `to = 'returned_to_origin'` (a refusal, a return to origin, or the returned rows of a partial), the module computes what the customer prepaid for pieces she never received: the sum of those lines' `prepaid_amount` (split rows included), plus `shipping_prepaid_amount` when no line of the order remains delivered or open, less a shipping deposit the brand keeps (AC-15). When it is above 0 it publishes `PrepaidShareOwed` (order, amount, funding from `prepaid_method`, cause, settlement `refund` or `store_credit`) once per (order, set of lines) and stores it on the outcome row. For lost lines (`to = 'lost'`), on every lost order, COD or prepaid (second pass n2), it does not publish at once: it opens `delivery.lost_resolve` (hat operations, `orders.confirm`) with the answers `send_again` (a `reopened_from` order, spec 0013 AC-23, which carries the prepayment; requested), `refund` (only when something was prepaid; then `PrepaidShareOwed`), or `customer_declined` (the customer wants neither; a reason code), because a brand often resends a lost dress (review M10). Until the credit module's slice 1a refund record exists, `PrepaidShareOwed` opens spec 0016's `refund.unrecorded_payout` task on the order with the amount and method, so slice 1a never loses money owed; afterwards the credit slice opens a refund request by the same method instead, and feature 28 posts it as a `prepayment_release` credit entry (requested), kept out of `returned_credit_amount` like `consumption_release`, so the order still derives `kept` (partial) or `failed_delivery`, whatever the payment method. The credit share keeps spec 0002's `consumption_release`.
- **AC-15** (the shipping deposit, an optional path; owner answer of 2026-11-13 to Q-21.2, Q-21.9, Q-21.10, Q-21.12: usually there is no deposit): this criterion applies only to an order that carries a deposit, which exists only while spec 0017's `confirmation.shipping_deposit_offered` is on; for every other order the failed delivery's money is AC-33's alone. A prepayment with `prepaid_purpose = 'shipping_deposit'` (defined by spec 0017) is allocated to shipping first and only its excess to the lines (spec 0017 and spec 0013), so a partial delivery shows no false shortfall. This module reads spec 0017's core setting `shipping_deposit_on_refusal` (`kept_as_delivery_fee`, `released`, `store_credit`; default `released`) and never defines it; a brand sets `kept_as_delivery_fee` only after its own legal check (Consumer Protection Law 181 of 2018), which is not a pilot step. The deposit is kept as the delivery fee only when all of these hold (review M10): AC-33's fee bearer is `customer`; the order records that the customer was told the deposit term at confirmation (spec 0017's disclosure, requested); no FlexShip refusal fee was collected for the same refusal (AC-11, Q-21.10); and the setting is `kept_as_delivery_fee`. Otherwise the deposit is owed back through AC-14 as a refund or as store credit: a lost or damaged parcel (`released_lost_or_damaged`); a fee bearer of `carrier` (`released_carrier_fault`, or `released_carrier_ignored_request` when AC-33 found a request passed to Bosta), `brand` (`released_brand_fault`), or `undecided` (`released_undecided`, recomputed when a person decides); no disclosure (`released_no_disclosure`); a refusal fee collected (`released_refusal_fee_collected`); or the setting `released` or `store_credit` (`released_by_setting`, `store_credit_by_setting`). The rule that applied is stored on the outcome row (`deposit_rule`). Only the deposit's excess above `shipping_charged_amount` is always owed back; the cancel before pickup case is spec 0017's. Worked case: a 1,000.00 plus 50.00 order of two 500.00 pieces with a disclosed 50.00 InstaPay shipping deposit: keeping one piece, `cod_due_amount` is 500.00 and Bosta's 500.00 collection reconciles with no shortfall; refusing both, nothing is owed back and the 50.00 stays as the delivery fee; Bosta losing the parcel instead, 50.00 is owed back.

- **AC-17** (the outcome projection): the module keeps one `delivery_outcomes` row per order that left the warehouse, and one `delivery_outcome_lines` row per shipped line, built from `OrderStateChanged`, `LinesReturnedToOrigin`, `OrderPlaced` and the replacement link (AC-7), and spec 0018's events, reading order facts through `OrderQueries.deliveryFactsOf` (requested): store, order type, carrier (`bosta` with its connection, or `manual`), governorate and city codes, campaign id, shipment day, `returning_since` (the flip), ending, ending day, refusal reason, `size_swap`, attempts, prepaid owed, `deposit_rule`, and `fee_bearer` (AC-33); per line the variant, style, shipped, returned, and lost quantities. `pnpm --filter @ssdashboard/api deliveries:rebuild-outcomes` rebuilds both tables from orders and connectors reads and must produce the same rows.

*The returned to origin rate*

*Lost and damaged parcel claims (audit item 12)*
- **AC-19** (the claim record): a claim is opened in state `draft` by the system when spec 0018 reports lost (100) or damaged (101) on an order's active forward delivery, when a returned parcel closes short or a piece comes back damaged from a carrier (AC-10; not from a customer return pickup unless marked tampered, AC-29), when a person records `lost` (AC-16), or through `CarrierClaims.openShortCollection` for a shortfall below Bosta's own COD (AC-12, AC-13, AC-22); and by a person with `settlement.work` on `POST /api/carrier-claims`, which also opens the person only kind `not_received` (Bosta says delivered, the customer says she never received it; review m6). A `short_collection` claim is how a courier shortfall is written off: rejecting it with reason `courier_shortfall_written_off` is the write off, audited; the only other write off is a brand side COD difference (`brand_side_write_off`, AC-12 (a)). The claim page's follow up text for `not_received` says what the brand does in slice 1a: a `goodwill_for` gifted resend with gift reason `replacement_goodwill`, or a refund outside the app until feature 34 (second pass n10).
- **AC-20** (the claim's life): `draft` → `submitted` (a person enters Bosta's complaint reference, letters, digits, and `-` up to 40, and the submit time; audited `carrier_claim.submitted`) → `approved`, `partially_approved` (with the approved amount, above 0 and below the claimed amount), or `rejected` (with a reason code), each with Bosta's decision time (audited `carrier_claim.decided`); `withdrawn` from `draft` or `submitted` with a reason (`parcel_found`, `customer_damage`, `opened_in_error`, `other`); a lost parcel that turns up comes back through spec 0011's `found` adjustment, because a lost line is not awaiting. Every change is an append only `carrier_claim_events` row; the claim row's state follows its events. `delivery.claim_follow_up` (hat operations, `settlement.work`) is open while the claim is `draft` (due 2 working days) or `submitted` (due 10 working days, then overdue).
- **AC-21** (no stock edit): a claim never posts or edits stock. A lost parcel's pieces left stock with the order's shipment (spec 0002 orders child, *A lost parcel's stock*), so the loss is already in the ledger; any awaiting restock quantity of a claimed piece is closed through orders with `lost_by_carrier` (or `damaged_not_resold`), which posts nothing. - **AC-22** (ready for spec 0020): the claim exports `CarrierClaims.awaitingCompensation({ connectionId, decidedFrom?, cursor })` (approved and partly approved claims with no compensation recorded: claim id, number, connection, tracking number, approved amount, decided time, complaint reference) and `CarrierClaims.recordCompensation(tx, claimId, { settlementLineId, amount, depositedOn }, actor)`, which spec 0020 calls from its matching unit of work. the caller computes `decidedBefore` as now minus the setting `claim_compensation_overdue_days` (default 30, defined here); this module's daily job does the same and reopens `delivery.claim_follow_up` for each such claim (spec 0020 review M5). `CarrierClaims.openShortCollection(tx, { orderId, deliveryId, amount }, actor)` opens (or returns the open) `short_collection` claim for an order and carrier delivery with `amount` as its claimed amount and price value, no lines, and the delivery's connection and tracking number; - **AC-33** (who bears Bosta's fee on a failed delivery, owner answer to Q-18.3 and Q-21.12): Bosta charges its fee on every parcel, refused or not, so every order that ends `returned_to_origin` (refused or not) gets one fee bearer on its outcome row, decided by the pure `fee-bearer.ts` from the deciding answer of AC-6 (a recorded contact overrides the courier's reason): **`brand`** when the refusal reason is `wrong_item`, `damaged`, or `price` (wrong item or size, damaged or not as shown, price or COD wrong, including a disputed amount at the door), when a `size` refusal led to a size swap replacement (AC-7: the sale is kept), or when the deciding reason is `address_problem` and the order's address or area was edited in confirmation or the courier text says out of zone; **`carrier`** when the deciding reason is `carrier_side` (late, a hub or weather delay), the latest contact is `courier_never_came` (Bosta never reached her), or the latest contact asked for another try and was passed to Bosta (`will_receive`, `rescheduled`, or `details_corrected` with `passedToCarrier` other than `not_possible`) and Bosta returned the parcel anyway (AC-6); **`undecided`** when the deciding reason is `unclassified` with no contact other than `no_answer`, a plain `address_problem`, or a `size` refusal with no replacement and no contact that records the code (the courier's "the size is wrong" cannot tell a wrong size sent from a piece that does not fit; domain review of 2026-11-13, M2); **`customer`** otherwise (`customer_unreachable`, `customer_postponed`, `cash_problem` (no cash), and a refusal for `changed_mind`, `not_ordered`, or `other`, or a `size` refusal whose contact recorded that it does not fit). This table is the only source of fault: AC-15 reads it and keeps no list of its own. The bearer is decided at the ending (46, or `returned_to_origin` by a person) and stored with `fee_bearer_source = 'rule'`; a person with `orders.confirm` may change it on `POST /api/orders/:id/fee-bearer` (`bearer`, a reason code, `expectedUpdatedAt`; audited `delivery_outcome.fee_bearer_set`, source `person`), which is how `undecided` is resolved. Each decision or change publishes `DeliveryFeeBearerDecided` (`orderId`, `customerId?`, `bearer`, `refusedReasonCode?`, `source`, `sizeSwap`) in the same transaction. What follows: **`brand`**: nothing is asked of the customer and the order is left out of her refusal count; the fee stays the brand's cost on the order's charges (AC-11), reported by reason for feature 24 ("refusals the brand caused"). **`carrier`**: a claim of the new kind `failed_delivery_fee` is drafted (AC-19; no piece lines, claimed amount the delivery's current cost legs for that parcel, so it is drafted when both the bearer and the charge are known, on the ending or on the later `DeliveryChargePosted`), followed and matched to Bosta's compensation like any claim; the customer's count is left alone. **`customer`**: the app records the FlexShip `refusal_fee` Bosta collected at the door where there is one (AC-11, Q-21.10) and records the failure against her: spec 0014 counts every customer borne ending in her signal (its suggestion of `refuser` at 2 stays), and sets the `refuser` flag itself, with source `refusal_at_door`, only for a `customer_refused` ending with a customer side code (`changed_mind`, `not_ordered`, `other`, or a `size` recorded as not fitting) that no later contact contradicts, unless she already carries `refuser`, `fake`, or `abusive`; never for an unreachable, postponed, or cash ending on the courier's word alone, never for a size swap, and never on an exchange replacement (AC-34). It never adds a fee to her next order, never requires a payment before a reship, and no screen offers either. A change away from `customer` makes spec 0014 recount and clear a `refusal_at_door` flag that only this order set. **`undecided`**: shown as its own group in the rates (AC-18), on the order page ("decide who bears Bosta's fee"), and as a filter on `/delivery-outcomes` for the operations lead, with no task; while the seeded phrases are still *Assumed*, expect most pilot endings here. The order page shows how the bearer was reached (the reason, its `classified_by`, and the contact), so a brand side count inflated by a loose phrase is visible. A lost or damaged parcel has no fee bearer (its claim is AC-19's `lost` or `damaged`).
- **AC-34** (a Bosta Exchange, the replacement's forward delivery carrying the old piece back; owner decision Q-21.13 of 2026-11-13; feature 34 work pulled into slice 1a): (a) **Create.** On a `delivered` or `partially_delivered` order, a person with `orders.confirm` uses "Exchange with Bosta" on the order page: picks the delivered lines and quantities coming back and, one for one, the variant going out for each (another size or color of the same style first, any variant through the catalog search), and an exchange shipping charge (default 0.00, the amount the brand asks the customer to pay for the exchange delivery). `dryRun: true` answers the allowance, the new total, and the price difference; the commit calls spec 0013's requested `OrderIntake.createExchangeReplacement(tx, { originalOrderId, returning: [{ lineId, quantity }], lines, shippingChargedAmount, shipTo }, actor)`, which in one unit creates the replacement (`type = 'exchange_replacement'`, `original_order_id`, relation `replacement_for`, lines at the variants' catalog prices, except that a new line of the same style as its returning line defaults to the unit price she paid for the old one after discount, so a size swap of a discounted dress never charges the discount back (a person may change either with a reason); the original's store, its numbering for the replacement's number, its campaign source, and its address and phones unless edited) and confirms it (reason `exchange_agreed`, because the agent agreed it with the customer), so it holds stock and appears on the pick list like any confirmed order. Its **exchange allowance** is a prepayment of `prepaid_method = 'exchange_credit'` and `prepaid_purpose = 'exchange_allowance'` equal to what she paid for the returning pieces (their lines' gross after discount, less any refund recorded on them), capped at the replacement's line total; so `cod_expected_amount` is the price difference plus the exchange shipping charge, never below 0. When the allowance is larger than the new lines (a cheaper piece), the excess less the exchange shipping charge is owed back to her (the two are netted, so the courier collects nothing while the brand owes her), published as `PrepaidShareOwed` with cause `exchange_difference` only once the old piece is received (d). A second exchange of a returning quantity still held by a replacement that is not cancelled and not `returned_to_origin` answers 409 `ALREADY_EXCHANGING`; a cancelled or refused replacement frees it, and a retry (she refuses the L and wants the XL) is a new exchange, never a reopen. An original that is not delivered, partly delivered, or `exchanged` (for lines not yet exchanged) answers spec 0013's `RELATED_ORDER_STATE`. The order page then shows what to type into Bosta's dashboard to create the Exchange: the replacement's order number as the business reference, its COD, and the pieces out and back (mirror mode cannot create it). (b) **The new piece out.** Spec 0018 AC-33 links the type 30 delivery to the replacement and moves it like a send: the warehouse packs it from the pick list (`markPacked`), and Bosta's pickup (21) posts its shipment through spec 0013, which takes the new piece out of stock through the ledger at its unit cost (spec 0011); 45 makes it `delivered` and records Bosta's `cod` as its collection. (c) **The old piece back.** At the type 30's 45 the module opens a `returned_parcels` row of cause `exchange_pickup` on the original order (its `carrier_delivery_id` the type 30, state `returning`), expecting the returning lines of the exchange; it becomes `back_at_business` at that delivery's 46. Each piece is received exactly as a return pickup piece (AC-29): spec 0013's `recordCarrierReturn` on the original's line, then `restockAwaiting`, which posts `customer_return_restock` at the line's frozen `unit_cost` to the location it left from. A damaged or missing old piece follows AC-10 (a claim when the courier had it). (d) **Closing it.** When every returning piece is received, the module calls spec 0013's `markExchanged(tx, originalOrderId, { replacementOrderId }, actor)` (requested in a slice 1a form with `exchange_id` null), so the original is `exchanged` and derives `kept`, and publishes any `exchange_difference` owed back. A parcel still expecting pieces after `partial_return_receive_days` opens `delivery.exchange_old_piece_missing` (hat operations, `orders.confirm`) with two answers (domain review M4): `courier_did_not_collect` (Bosta handed the new piece over without taking the old one, or lost it: a `missing_from_return` claim is drafted at the piece's price share, the original's line is recorded returned and closed `lost_by_carrier`, and the original becomes `exchanged`) or `customer_kept` (she kept both: the original stays `delivered`, the allowance becomes money she owes, recorded on the outcome row and passed to support as "customer owes" through `CashDifferences`, and feature 24 counts the replacement's full total as its sale). A replacement packed with no linked type 30 after `exchange_unlinked_hours` (default 24, working hours) shows "no Bosta Exchange created yet" on the pick list and opens `delivery.exchange_unlinked` for that replacement. (e) **Refused at the door.** When the type 30 fails and comes back without a 45, the replacement ends `returned_to_origin` on its own parcel (cause `returned_to_origin`, its new piece restocked as any return), AC-33 decides its fee bearer, the original stays `delivered` with the old piece at the customer's, and nothing is owed back: `PrepaidShareOwed` is never published for `exchange_credit` funding (it is not money she paid twice). (f) **Money and figures.** The price difference is the replacement's own COD, collected by Bosta and settled as that order's forward cash (spec 0020); the exchange's Bosta fee is the `exchange_pickup` leg on the replacement (AC-11); the original keeps its sale, and feature 24 counts the replacement's sales value as its total less the allowance, so one dress is never sold twice in the figures (requested). A type 30 that spec 0018 could not link to a replacement opens `delivery.exchange_unlinked` (hat operations, `orders.confirm`) with "create the exchange order, or link this delivery to it", answered `linked`, `exchange_created`, or `other`. Everything else about exchanges (an exchange id grouping the records, inspection, credit entries, the policy knobs, an exchange without Bosta's type 30) stays feature 34.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option B in [rationale.md](rationale.md): a new core module `deliveries` that owns what happens to an order after it leaves the warehouse and is not simply delivered (failed attempts, their contacts and reasons, returned parcels of every cause and their receipts, delivery charges, claims, manual handovers and run receipts, and the outcome projection), listens to spec 0018's events, and changes orders and stock only through spec 0013's named moves and spec 0011's one named found call.

**Implementation skills**: `nestjs-best-practices` (`kadajett/agent-nestjs-skills`, `.claude/skills/nestjs-best-practices/`) · `drizzle-orm-patterns` (`giuseppe-trisciuoglio/developer-kit`, `.claude/skills/drizzle-orm-patterns/`) · `postgresql-table-design` (`wshobson/agents`, `.claude/skills/postgresql-table-design/`) · `pg-boss-v12` (`sncollective/snc-platform`, `.claude/skills/pg-boss-v12/`) · `nextjs-app-router-patterns` (`wshobson/agents`, `.claude/skills/nextjs-app-router-patterns/`) · `playwright-best-practices` (`currents-dev/playwright-best-practices-skill`, `.claude/skills/playwright-best-practices/`)

**Decided by the architect** (expertise, not owner questions; runner up in brackets):
- The module is `deliveries`, core, because returned parcels, outcomes, claims, and the manual courier path work without Bosta [inside `orders`: it is already the largest module and these are not order rows].
- The reason text stays in connectors and is read in memory [copying it here: two stores of personal text].
- Rates come from this module's own projection [a live query through orders on every request: crosses the module wall and is slow at a year's range].
- Bosta's charge for a returned to origin parcel is one `round_trip` row, because Bosta settles it once on the same tracking number [two estimated legs from a rate card: an invented split].
- A parcel's expected pieces are read live from awaiting quantities [a copy on the parcel: two doors drift, review M6].

| Reason | Means for the team | Typical courier text (seeded phrase rules, **Assumed** until replaced by real distinct texts before milestone 2) |
|---|---|---|

| `cash_problem` | no cash (a disputed amount is a refusal for `price`) | "لا يوجد فلوس", "مفيش فلوس", "no cash" |
| `carrier_side` | the courier did not reach, a hub or a weather delay | "تأخير", "delay" |
| `unclassified` | nothing matched; the text is kept in connectors and shown to a person | |

The bare phrase "غير موجود" is not seeded, because it also appears in "الرقم غير موجود" (the number does not exist), which is unreachable, not postponed (review m1). Exception codes: Bosta's forward codes 1 to 8, 12 to 14, 100, 101 and return codes 20 to 30 have no published meaning (rationale E2), so no code rule is seeded; a manager adds one when the pilot's data shows a code always means one reason. A phrase rule matches when its normalized phrase is contained in the normalized text; the longest matching phrase wins, then the lowest `priority`, then the oldest rule.

### Lifecycles

- **Attempt**: recorded → classified (any time, by a rule or a person) → contacted (one or more contact rows; `no_answer` keeps the task open; the latest contact is the attempt's answer). A later attempt is a new row. The order's own state machine is spec 0013's.
- **Returned parcel**: `returning` → `back_at_business` (46; for a partial, a return pickup, or an exchange's old piece, 46 of a later return delivery, or else the first piece goes straight on; at once for a manual or unexpected one; Bosta's 23 moves nothing) → `received` (nothing left expected) or `closed_short` (a person closes with pieces missing). Pieces may be received in `returning`, which may go straight to `received` or `closed_short`. `received` and `closed_short` are final.
- **Claim**: `draft` → `submitted` → `approved` | `partially_approved` | `rejected`; `draft` or `submitted` → `withdrawn`. Compensation: `not_due` → `awaiting` (on approval) → `received` | `received_differs` (spec 0020).
- **Manual handover**: `out` → `failed_attempt` → `out` (a new try is a `handed_over` event) | `delivered` | `partially_delivered` | `returned` | `lost`; the last four are final. A `customer_pickup` goes `out` to `delivered` in one step.

### The claim record and what spec 0020 matches on

A claim's identity is its `id` (uuid) and its human number `claim_number` (`CL-2026-000123`, unique per tenant). Spec 0020 should match a Bosta "Compensation" wallet line to a claim on these fields, in its own order of preference: `connection_id` (the carrier settlement account's `owner_connection_id`; a claim never matches across accounts), `tracking_number` (when Bosta's line carries one; unverified, rationale E7), `approved_amount` (equal to the line's amount), `decided_at` (the compensation is paid with the next cash out after approval, so the line's deposit day is on or after it), `carrier_complaint_ref` (when the line's text holds it), and `compensation_state = 'awaiting'`. It records the match through `CarrierClaims.recordCompensation`, which stores `compensation_line_id`, `compensation_amount`, and `compensation_deposited_on`; a claim is never matched twice (`compensation_line_id` set once). A Bosta cash cycle COD on a refused or returned to origin delivery matches the delivery's `refusal_fee` charge row, never the order's cash (review M9).

### Key invariants

- The deliveries module is the only writer of its tables; it changes orders only through spec 0013's moves and stock only through `OrderStock.restockAwaiting`, the close, and spec 0011's one named `recordFound` for pre go live pieces, never through the ledger directly (`apps/api/src/inventory/AGENTS.md`).
- A returned piece comes back to stock at most once: one restock per idempotency key, capped by `awaiting_restock_quantity`, whichever door (this module or spec 0013's button) it went through; a close posts nothing; nothing posts for a `held` piece until a person answers.
- A claim never posts stock; its loss is the shipment's movement already in the ledger. No claim is drafted for a quantity no longer awaiting.
- No reason text or personal data is stored in this module, published, logged, or put in a task label.
- The outcome of an order is only `deriveOrderOutcome`; this module stores endings in its projection and never writes `orders.outcome`.
- Every order that was confirmed either ends (a final fulfillment state, or cancelled) or has an open `delivery.outcome_overdue` or `delivery.not_shipped` task after the due days.
- A FlexShip amount collected on a refusal is a `refusal_fee` charge, never order cash; every charge amount is positive and its leg gives its direction.
- A cash shortfall is written off only by deciding a `short_collection` claim (a courier shortfall, below Bosta's own COD) or by `brand_side_write_off` (a difference Bosta was told to collect); this module is their single owner and spec 0020 only reads `CashDifferences.resolutionOf`; an over collection that belongs to the customer is a refund owed, never a write off.
- No text the app shows says the app told, changed, or rescheduled anything at Bosta; it records that a person did.

### Security model

- Hats: support classifies and contacts (`orders.confirm`); the warehouse receives (`stock.receive`) and hands over (`fulfillment.work`); operations decides partial deliveries, lost resolutions, outcomes, and unexpected and wrong pieces (`orders.confirm`, `stock.receive`), teaches phrase rules (`channels.map`), works claims and courier cash (`settlement.work`); the manager also posts `found` and the damaged close (`stock.correct`) and code rules (`settings.manage`); rates need `reports.view`, or `campaigns.view` for the campaign cut.
- Personal data (Law 151): the courier's reason text may name or describe a person; it is read through `attemptsOf` for one attempt on the order page (`orders.view`) and grouped on the rules screen (`channels.map`), never stored here, logged, or exported. A phrase a person saves is limited to 60 characters and the screen warns not to save names or numbers; a phrase with 7 or more digits is refused (422 `PHRASE_HAS_NUMBER`). Phones appear only on the order view behind the task. `courierName` and `carrierTicketRef` are redacted.
- Audit (spec 0010 registry additions, English and Arabic labels, proof tests; record types `delivery_attempt`, `failure_reason_rule`, `returned_parcel`, `carrier_claim`, `manual_handover`, `courier_cash_receipt`): `delivery_attempt.classified`, `delivery_attempt.contact_recorded`, `failure_reason_rule.added`, `failure_reason_rule.retired`, `returned_parcel.piece_recorded`, `returned_parcel.piece_answered`, `returned_parcel.unexpected_recorded`, `returned_parcel.closed`, `order.partial_delivery_split` (existing), `order.outcome_recorded_by_person`, `order.lost_resolved`, `carrier_claim.opened`, `carrier_claim.amount_changed`, `carrier_claim.submitted`, `carrier_claim.decided`, `carrier_claim.withdrawn`, `manual_handover.recorded`, `manual_handover.event_recorded`, `courier_cash_receipt.recorded`, `delivery_charge.recorded`.

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- A refusal or a failed try reaches one person before the courier's next run with a reason in the team's words, and the record says how Bosta was told, even while Bosta's code list stays unknown.
- Every returned piece, from a return to origin, a return pickup, an exchange, a partial, or an unknown bag, has a place, and goes back to stock once through the orders module, whichever button the warehouse uses.
- Every Bosta charge of an order, including the FlexShip refusal fee, is visible per order and campaign, and the prepaid money owed back is never lost before the credit slice exists.
- Spec 0020 gets a claim with a stable identity and a small set of match fields.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- Holding Bosta's delivered move on short cash (Q-21.4) leaves such orders `shipped` until a person decides, and needs a change to spec 0018 AC-16.
- The reason list starts with assumed phrases until real texts replace them before milestone 2.
- Bosta's charge for a returned parcel cannot be split into its two legs, so it is one `round_trip` row; if a recording shows two charges, the leg rule changes.
- The manual courier path depends on people recording each step; nothing outside the app confirms it.
- Fifteen requested changes to spec 0013, one to spec 0011, and eight to spec 0018 must land, some before milestone 1.
- The deposit rule, for a brand that takes deposits, depends on spec 0017's disclosure and on that brand's own legal check.
- The fee bearer rule flags a customer for every refusal that is not the brand's or Bosta's fault; a courier reason classified wrongly flags the wrong customer until a person changes it, so the classifier's real texts (before milestone 2) matter more now.
- A Bosta Exchange adds a sixth milestone and four requests of spec 0013 to slice 1a (AC-34), roughly two builder weeks across orders, the Bosta connector, deliveries, settlement, and the screens; the exchange id, inspection, and credit entries still wait for feature 34.

**Neutral**:
- A new core module, `deliveries`, with sixteen tables, two of them projections that can be rebuilt.
- The pick list is a new warehouse page that feature 33 (pick, pack and ship) later replaces or grows.
