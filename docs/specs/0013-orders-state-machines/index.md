## Summary

An order is where cash on delivery lives or dies, so exactly one place may change it: the new `orders` module in the api. Shopify, the confirmation queue, Bosta, delivery failures, and settlement never touch the order tables; they call a small set of named moves (create, edit, confirm, cancel, pack, carrier fact, channel stock out, channel fulfillment cancelled, channel return, partial delivery, collection, settlement) inside one order transaction, and every move goes through the transition tables that already exist in `packages/contracts`, writes one event row, recomputes the money and the outcome, holds or releases stock, and announces itself to the rest of the app in the same transaction. Staff get an order list and an order page in Arabic and English with the store beside every order number, can create a manual, gifted, reopened, or replacement order, can edit an order until the carrier delivery exists, and can cancel it until the courier picks it up, with every edit in the audit trail. Nothing here redesigns the data model of spec 0002; it adds three columns, seven indexes, and one enum value in milestone 1, and the stock out columns of AC-27 in milestone 5 (amended 2026-11-08), and fixes the rules the domain reviews left to this feature.

## Requirements

**User stories**:

- As a support agent, I want to open any order and see its store, lines, both states, money, and history on one page, so that I can answer a customer on WhatsApp without opening Shopify and Bosta side by side.
- As a support agent, I want to fix the address, change a size, or add a piece before the parcel leaves, so that the courier gets the right parcel and the right amount to collect.
- As a support agent, I want to cancel a fake or unanswered order even when Bosta already has a delivery for it, so that its stock comes back and it leaves the cash expected.
- As a support agent, I want to enter an order a customer placed on WhatsApp or Instagram, or one she reopens after we closed it, so that every order lives in one list and keeps its campaign.
- As a manager, I want to record a gifted order with its reason, so that influencer and giveaway parcels are tracked without ever counting as revenue or as a return.
- As a warehouse person, I want an order whose edit has not reached Shopify, or whose piece is not in stock, to refuse packing, so that we never ship the old size or an incomplete parcel.
- As a manager, I want to see who changed an order and why, so that I can trace a wrong address or a wrong amount.
- As the developer of Shopify, Bosta, delivery failures, or settlement, I want one documented call for each move I need and one way to open the transaction, so that my feature never writes an order table and never breaks the state machines.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract; each is checkable on its own):

_Orders in and out_

- **AC-1** (manual order): a person with `orders.confirm` creates a sale order on `POST /api/orders` into an internal store (owner decision Q-16.1, recommended), on a channel of kind `manual`, `whatsapp`, or `instagram`, with at least one line of a known variant, a ship to name, phone, and address, a shipping charge, and payment `cod` (or fully prepaid by `instapay`, `bank_transfer`, or `wallet`). In one transaction the order gets the number `NumberingService.next(tx, 'SO', placedAt)` (`SO-2026-000123`), `placed_at` = now, lines priced by `LineAmounts.compute` with the `sales_tax_rate` setting and `prices_include_vat`, totals, allocation, `cod_expected_amount`, `cod_due_amount`, the first fulfillment event (`null` to `new`) and the first cash event (`null` to `firstCashState`), outcome `open`, and the customer link from the main phone. A phone that does not parse leaves `customer_id` null, keeps the text in `contact_phone_raw`, and raises the attention `phone_unparseable`. A repeat of the same `clientRef` returns the first order and writes nothing. A store of kind `shopify` answers 422 `STORE_NOT_INTERNAL`; a prepayment other than 0 or the full total answers 422 `PARTIAL_PREPAYMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- **AC-2** (gifted order): only a person with `orders.gift` creates a gifted order on `POST /api/orders/gifted`; it needs a `giftReasonCode` from the list (`influencer`, `giveaway`, `staff`, `replacement_goodwill`, `other`), every line keeps its retail `unitPrice` with an equal discount (gross 0), shipping charged is 0, payment `none`, and it starts `nothing_to_collect`. `replacement_goodwill` also needs the order it makes good (`relatedOrder` with `goodwill_for`, AC-23). Editing the lines or the shipping of a gifted order needs `orders.gift` (403 `GIFT_NOT_ALLOWED` otherwise), and such an edit that gives any line or the shipping a gross above 0 answers 422 `GIFT_MUST_STAY_FREE`; a `ship_to` edit of a gifted order needs only `orders.confirm`, so support fixes an influencer's address (review m1). Every list row, detail page, and API answer carries `type` and `giftReasonCode`, so revenue and return rate readers (feature 24) leave gifted orders out and the accountant's export (feature 45) classes each by its reason (review m2).
- **AC-3** (store beside every number, carried B1): two stores each hold an order `#1001`; a second `#1001` in one store is refused (`OrderNumberTaken`, 409 `ORDER_NUMBER_TAKEN`). Every place this feature shows or returns an order number shows the store code beside it: the list, the detail header, the search result, the event timeline, the toast after an action, and the audit `record_label` (`<store code>/<order number>`, for example `ESS/#1001`).
- **AC-4** (order list): `/orders` (permission `orders.view`) lists orders newest first with keyset pages (50 by default, 100 at most, no skipped or repeated row while new orders arrive), filters for store and channel (their lists come from `GET /api/orders/filter-options`, which also holds Shopify channels), fulfillment state (several), cash state (several), outcome, type, write back hold, short lines, customer (every id of a merged customer, from the customer page), and an operating day range, and one search box that finds a phone (any form `EgyptianPhone` parses, matching the order's main or second ship to phone, or any phone of its customer) or else an order number in every store (`1001` finds `#1001`). Each row shows, in the design system's fixed column order, the number with its store and placed time, the ship to name, the channel, the total and the amount due, the fulfillment chip (with the outcome once it is final), and the cash chip; a hold, a short line, a gift, an exchange replacement, a reopened order, and a phone that needs fixing show as small flags under the number. With the spec 0002 scale load (150,000 orders), each filter alone and the unfiltered first page answer in under 150 ms at p95.
- **AC-5** (order page): `/orders/<id>` (permission `orders.view`) shows the number and store, type and gift reason, channel, source, the related order with its relation, placed time in the install time zone, both state chips and the outcome, the ship to block, the linked customer's name and risk flag with a link to the customer page, the customer note, the lines (SKU code and name, quantity, unit price, discount, gross, line state, prepaid and credit shares, a "short" mark with the missing quantity (`short_quantity`, AC-13), and `unitCost` only for a viewer holding `cost.view`), the money block (total, prepaid with its method, credit applied, COD expected, COD due, collected, remitted), the write back state of each open command, the stock out block (source and time, "Left stock: Shopify fulfillment, 14:05", any reversal, and awaiting restock quantities per line, AC-27), and the event timeline (every `order_events` row with its actor's name, reason, and time). A viewer with `audit.read` also sees the order's audit entries (`GET /api/audit/records/order/:id/entries`, spec 0010). An unknown id answers 404 `ORDER_NOT_FOUND`.

_The two state machines_

- **AC-6** (one way to move): every state change of either machine goes through `nextState` in `packages/contracts/src/orders/order-states.ts` and writes exactly one `order_events` row in the same transaction as the change of `orders.fulfillment_state` or `orders.cash_state`, with the actor, reason code, source, and `occurred_at`. A move a person or a job asks for that the table refuses answers 409 `INVALID_TRANSITION` with both states and writes nothing (a carrier fact the table does not list is AC-15's rule). `orders.outcome` is written only from `deriveOrderOutcome` in the same transaction as any change to its inputs. Replaying an order's events `ORDER BY recorded_at, id` with `replayStates` equals the stored states, including two events written in one transaction.
- **AC-7** (confirm, cancel, pack): a person with `orders.confirm` confirms a `new` order (sets `confirmed_at`, `confirmed_by`), and cancels a `new`, `confirmed`, or `packed` order with a cancel reason code (`customer_cancelled`, `duplicate`, `fake`, `no_answer`, `out_of_stock`, `wrong_details`, `other`); `duplicate` needs the order it duplicates and sets `related_order_id` with `duplicate_of`. A cancel leaves every line and every total as it was, sets `cancelled_at` and `cancel_reason_code`, brings `cod_due_amount` to 0, moves cash `unpaid` to `nothing_to_collect` (actor `system_job`) in the same transaction, releases the order's stock reservations, and publishes `OrderCancelled`. A cancel from `packed` also raises `unpack_cancelled_order` for the warehouse, because the piece sits in a sealed parcel (review m6). An order of a `shopify` store is cancelled in the app only while `ConnectorsService.writeBackAvailable(orderId, 'shopify.order_cancel')` is true; otherwise 409 `CANCEL_IN_SHOPIFY`, and the page says to cancel it in Shopify, from where it syncs back (review M1, the rule Q-16.3 chose for edits). A person with `fulfillment.work` moves `confirmed` to `packed` and back. `packed` is refused with 409 `WRITE_BACK_OPEN` while the connectors service reports an open command on the order (carried B2), whatever `write_back_state` says, and with 409 `LINE_SHORT` while any open line is still short after the top up of AC-13 (owner decision Q-16.5, recommended).
- **AC-8** (two locks, review X1): from the moment `carrier_delivery_created_at` is set, every edit answers 409 `ORDER_LOCKED` and changes nothing, and `cod_expected_amount`, `prepaid_amount`, `credit_applied_amount`, and every allocation share stay as they were (the money freeze). A cancel stays possible until the courier picks the parcel up, that is while fulfillment is `new`, `confirmed`, or `packed`, delivery or not (owner decision Q-16.4, recommended); a person's or the channel's cancel of an order whose carrier delivery exists raises `terminate_carrier_delivery` for operations, because in mirror mode the app cannot terminate the delivery itself. From `shipped` on, a cancel answers 409 `INVALID_TRANSITION`.

_Edits and their audit_

- **AC-9** (edits): a person with `orders.confirm` sends `POST /api/orders/:id/edits` with `expectedUpdatedAt`, an optional reason code (`customer_request`, `address_fix`, `size_change`, `out_of_stock`, `price_fix`, `other`), and one or more operations: change the ship to fields (name, phone, second phone, address, courier note, the carrier area codes), add a line, replace a line (a new variant, quantity, or price), remove a line (with `customer_cancelled`, `out_of_stock`, or `duplicate`), and change the shipping charge. All operations apply in one transaction. A line is never changed in place: a replace cancels the old line with `edit_removal` and adds a new one at the next position. Afterwards `lines_gross_amount`, `total_amount`, `shipping_tax_amount`, the allocation shares, `cod_expected_amount`, and `cod_due_amount` are recomputed with the existing contract functions; an edit in `packed` also moves the order back to `confirmed` (one event). A changed main phone relinks the customer and clears `contact_phone_raw`. Refusals: a stale `expectedUpdatedAt` answers 409 `ORDER_STALE`; an edit that would leave no open line answers 422 `ORDER_NEEDS_A_LINE` (cancel the order instead); an edit that would move `cod_expected_amount` between 0 and above 0 answers 422 `EDIT_CHANGES_PAYMENT`; an edit that would bring `total_amount` below `prepaid_amount + credit_applied_amount` answers 422 `EDIT_OVERPAID` (the refund path is feature 18's; review M5).
- **AC-10** (edits on a Shopify order): an order of a `shopify` store is editable in the app only while `ConnectorsService.writeBackAvailable(orderId, 'shopify.order_edit')` is true (per order and per command kind: review m9 here, review m2 of domain-shopify-connector (kept private)); otherwise every edit answers 409 `EDIT_IN_SHOPIFY` and the order page says to edit that order in Shopify, from where it syncs back (owner decision Q-16.3, recommended). An edit made in the app publishes `OrderEdited` with `origin: 'person'` in its transaction, which feature 13 turns into an outbound command in the same transaction.
- **AC-11** (audit): every edit writes exactly one `order.edited` audit entry in the edit's transaction, with the changed fields in `before` and `after`, the ship to values stored as `"[REDACTED]"` and listed in `personal_fields` (a test with sentinel values proves the address, phone, name, and courier note appear in no column of `audit_entries`), the reason code, and `record_label` `<store code>/<order number>`. A channel edit writes the same code with the `system_job` actor and `after.origin = 'channel'`. Creating a manual, gifted, reopened, or replacement order writes `order.created_manually` (its `after` names the related order and relation); a partial delivery split writes `order.partial_delivery_split`; a cash collection a person enters writes `order.collection_recorded_manually` with a required reason code. A failed audit write rolls the change back.

_The write back hold (carried B2 and n1)_

- **AC-12** (hold): `orders.write_back_state` follows the open outbound commands on the order (`failed` if any failed, else `waiting` if any waits, else `none`), updated in the same transaction as the command change through the `OutboundCommand*` domain events, recomputed from `ConnectorsService.openCommandsFor('order', id, tx)` each time. The list filter "hold" finds every order with `write_back_state <> 'none'`, and the list row and the order page show "Edit not synced, hold" with the command's state and reason.

_Stock_

- **AC-13** (reservations and short lines): creating an order reserves stock at the online location for every line with a variant, adding or replacing a line reserves the new line, removing or replacing a line releases the old line's reservation, and a cancel releases them all, each in the order transaction through spec 0011's `reserveLines` (`shortage: 'reserve_available'`), `releaseLines`, and `releaseSource` (owner decision Q-16.2, recommended: reserve at creation). A shortage never refuses an order or an edit: the line holds what is free, and spec 0011 keeps the rest as `reservations.unfilled_quantity`, which every ledger write that frees stock fills oldest first (review M6; spec 0011 AC-21 owns the restock half). `confirm` and `markPacked` call `reserveLines` again for the order's lines in the move's transaction (it tops `unfilled_quantity` up), and `markPacked` with a line whose answer is still `short` answers 409 `LINE_SHORT` (Q-16.5). `order_lines.short_quantity` is the orders module's copy of the line's `unfilled_quantity`: set from every `reserveLines` answer, lowered by `ReservationFilled`, raised by `ReservationShortened`, and 0 once the line or the order leaves `open`; it drives the list flag and filter and the page mark. When `ReservationShortened` arrives for an order line (a carrier shipment of another order or a damaged or missing piece took a reserved one, spec 0011 AC-22 and AC-23, its Q-11.8), the orders module also raises `reservation_shortened` for support in that transaction. Before a location first goes online, the orders module's check `orders.open_orders_held` (registered with spec 0011's `OnlineStockGate`) passes only when every open order line with a variant holds a reservation there; `POST /api/orders/hold-open-orders` reserves them (AC-26). Once an order's stock out is requested (`stock_out_requested_at` set, AC-27), no path reserves its lines again: `confirm` and `markPacked` skip their top up (and `LINE_SHORT`), `linkChannelLineVariant` sets the variant only and raises `shipped_with_unmapped_lines`, `orders.open_orders_held` skips the order, and `hold-open-orders` reserves nothing for it (amended 2026-11-08, code review M3, domain review S3).
- **AC-24** (carrier facts never wait on stock): the `order_shipment` that follows a carrier `shipped` fact is booked with spec 0011's `LedgerService.postCarrierShipment` (key `order_shipment:<orderId>`), which is never refused for stock or reservations: it posts even when a line was short or another order holds the piece, and publishes `ReservationShortened` or `StockOverdrawn`, which become attentions and tasks. So no carrier fact is ever refused or rolled back because of stock; the posting runs in the shipment job after the move commits (AC-15). One shipment posting per order and stock out generation, whatever reported it (amended 2026-11-08): the carrier `shipped` fact (feature 19), spec 0015's Shopify fulfillment (`recordChannelStockOut`), and later feature 33's pick, pack and ship all request the same job, which builds the posting from the order's lines (never from the source) under the order's current key (`order_shipment:<orderId>` for generation 1, `order_shipment:<orderId>:<n>` from generation 2, AC-27). The job first reads `ledger.postingFor(key)` (spec 0011 AC-29) and stops when a posting exists; it also does nothing when the order is `cancelled`, or its generation is no longer the job's, as it runs. It posts each line's `stock_out_quantity` (set when the stock out was requested: the full open quantity for a carrier fact, `recordChannelStockOut`, or pick, pack and ship, Shopify's fulfilled quantity for "Post what Shopify fulfilled"), never more. `IdempotencyConflict` is then only a real race (another job posted other lines under the key between the read and the post): it posts nothing and raises `shipment_not_posted`. `markPacked` posts no stock. A carrier `shipped` fact on an order with `fulfilled_lines_differ` open is never held back: it requests the full open quantities, the job posts them, and the attention is closed (AC-15). What a cancel, a cancelled fulfillment, or a return does to a posting is AC-27.
- **AC-26** (going online with open orders held): the orders module registers the check `orders.open_orders_held` with spec 0011's `OnlineStockGate` at module start; for a candidate location it returns `passed` when every open line with a variant of an order in `new`, `confirmed`, or `packed` holds an active reservation at that location or at the current online location (holds there stay there after the move, spec 0011 AC-19; amended 2026-11-12), a line of a `packed` order counting as held wherever its reservation is, and `count` the lines that do not. `POST /api/orders/hold-open-orders` (`{ locationId }`, permission `catalog.manage`, the location manager's permission) reserves those lines there with `reserveLines` (`reserve_available`), oldest order first, 200 lines per order transaction, moving a line of a `new` or `confirmed` order held at a location that is not online, and never releasing a hold at the current online location nor a held line of a `packed` order, and answers the lines held and still short; a repeat holds nothing twice. Orders whose stock out is requested (AC-27) are skipped by both (amended 2026-11-08).
- **AC-27** (stock out, cancels, and returns without a carrier, amended 2026-11-08 for spec 0015 AC-28): (a) **Request.** Each source requests the stock out once per generation, recorded on the order (`stock_out_source`, `stock_out_requested_at`) and on each line (`stock_out_quantity`: the line's open quantity, or for "Post what Shopify fulfilled" the quantity Shopify fulfilled; a line whose `stock_out_quantity` is below its quantity is marked not shipped for the rest), with no `order_events` row (no state machine moves) and no audit entry. Every restock and return path caps at what was posted: `awaiting_restock_quantity` never exceeds `stock_out_quantity`. `recordChannelStockOut` requests nothing and raises `fulfilled_lines_differ` (warehouse) when Shopify's fulfilled quantities differ from the order's open lines; the attention's action "Post what Shopify fulfilled" (`stock.correct`, reason code) requests the stock out of exactly the quantities Shopify fulfilled (read with `ConnectorsService.channelFulfilledLines(orderId)`) and releases the reservations of the rest, leaving the frozen money as it is. (b) **Cancel.** Every cancel while no carrier `shipped` fact exists (`cancel`, `cancelFromChannel`, and `recordShipmentFact` `cancelled` with `carrier.terminated_before_pickup`) enqueues the after cancel job in the same transaction, unconditionally, with the order's current generation `n` and the cause; the job decides by the ordered table in *After a cancel* (first match wins). (c) **Fulfillment cancelled.** `recordChannelFulfillmentCancelled` checks under the order lock that no carrier `shipped` fact exists (else `{ applied: false, why: 'after_pickup' }`); it enqueues the fulfillment reversal job with `n` and, when the order has no carrier delivery from feature 19 (no `shipments` row), clears `carrier_delivery_created_at` (audit `order.carrier_delivery_cleared`, actor `system_job`, reason code `channel_fulfillment_cancelled`), so the order is back to "before the first fulfillment" and a size swap arrives as a normal edit. This is an exception to the money freeze, accepted by the owner on 2026-11-08. In the same transaction it moves the order to generation `n + 1`, clears the request and the lines' `stock_out_quantity`, and reserves the open lines again (they read short until the reversal posts and fills waiting demand, spec 0011 AC-21); the job only reverses generation `n`, carried in its data (key `order_shipment_fulfillment_reversal:<orderId>:<n>`, reason code `channel_fulfillment_cancelled`). (d) **Returns.** `recordChannelReturn` puts the returned quantities of an order whose `stock_out_source` is `shopify_fulfillment` on `awaiting_restock_quantity`, whether or not Shopify marked them to restock and whether or not Bosta runs (the attention's source says `shopify_refund_no_restock` when not marked), and raises `returned_pieces_to_receive`; returns of a carrier shipped order stay with features 21, 34, and 35. (e) **Back on the shelf.** Orders owns the restock posting: `OrderStock.restockAwaiting(orderId, lineId, quantity, idempotencyKey, actor)` posts the quantity back with the ledger's `post` (source `returned_to_origin_restock` for a cancelled order, `customer_return_restock` otherwise), at the line's `unit_cost`, to the location the line's movement left from in the latest unreversed `order_shipment` posting (read with `ledger.postingFor`), key `order_restock:<orderId>:<lineId>:<idempotencyKey>`, and lowers `awaiting_restock_quantity` in the posting's `within` with a conditional update (`awaiting_restock_quantity >= quantity`) that throws `NothingAwaiting` (409 `NOTHING_AWAITING`) and rolls the posting back when it matches no row; a replay of the same key answers 200 with the stored result before any awaiting check; the line lock is taken inside the posting, after inventory's locks, and a deadlock with an order write is broken by the ledger's single retry, as for the `ReservationFilled` handler; it fills waiting demand like any inbound posting (spec 0011 AC-21). The "Received on the shelf" action on an awaiting line (`stock.receive`, no scanner needed) calls it, and so will feature 20's scan and feature 21; neither posts on its own. When one variant awaits on several orders, a scan restocks the oldest awaiting line first (`awaiting_restock_since`). (f) **Closing.** Pieces that never come back are closed with a reason code (`lost_by_carrier`, `damaged_not_resold`, `customer_kept`, `other`) by a `stock.correct` holder (`order.awaiting_restock_closed`); a warehouse hat (`stock.receive`) sends the same form as a close request (`order.awaiting_restock_close_requested`, attention `awaiting_restock_close_requested` for the manager), the adjustment request pattern of spec 0011 AC-27. Closing posts no movement, because the pieces already left the ledger. (g) **Seen.** The orders list has an "Awaiting restock" filter, oldest first (searchable by order number and phone like the rest of the list); a daily job raises `awaiting_restock_overdue` once for a line awaiting 10 operating days or more; the order page and list show the stock out source and time, any reversal, and the awaiting restock quantities (domain review N3). Lines of an order cancelled with no carrier delivery come first in the filter, with "Received on the shelf" as one tap.

_The calls other features make_

- **AC-14** (channel intake for feature 13): `OrderIntake.createFromChannel(tx, input, actor)` is idempotent on (`tenant_id`, `store_id`, `channel_order_ref`): a repeat returns `{ orderId, created: false }` and writes nothing; (b) a snapshot line whose `channel_line_id` matches no order line is first matched to an open order line with no `channel_line_id` and the same variant, quantity, and unit price (lowest position first), which takes that id, before anything is cancelled or added; (c) once the carrier delivery exists (a freeze that AC-27 (c) cleared after every Shopify fulfillment was cancelled no longer counts), when the edit would change payment (AC-9), or when it would overpay (`why: 'overpaid'`), it applies nothing, returns `{ applied: false, why }`, and raises `channel_edit_not_applied`; and applies nothing and raises `channel_cancel_after_pickup` while it is `shipped`, `failed_attempt`, `delivered`, `partially_delivered`, or `exchanged`. An import (`actor_type = 'import'`, feature 15, and spec 0015's go live for closed orders) may set the first states as found; an order imported as `shipped` or later takes no reservation and posts no shipment movement, because the opening stock snapshot already excludes those pieces.
- **AC-15** (carrier moves for features 13, 19, and 21): `OrderTransitions.recordCarrierDelivery(tx, orderId, { at, source }, actor)` accepts `source.type` `carrier_delivery` (feature 19) or `shopify_fulfillment` (feature 13, the first Shopify fulfillment, spec 0015 AC-10), sets `carrier_delivery_created_at` once per fulfillment cycle (a repeat changes nothing; after AC-27 (c) cleared it, the next active Shopify fulfillment sets it again from that fulfillment's own time), and raises `delivery_created_before_edit_synced` when a write back is open. `OrderTransitions.recordShipmentFact(tx, orderId, fact, actor)` applies `shipped`, `failed_attempt`, `delivered`, `refused` (with a `RefusedReasonCode`), `returned_to_origin`, `lost`, and `cancelled` (a delivery the carrier reports cancelled or terminated before any pickup, reason `carrier.terminated_before_pickup`, which releases the reservations and moves cash to `nothing_to_collect`, Q-16.4) as the transition table allows for that actor; it sets `carrier_delivery_created_at` on the first fact if it was unset, raises each task the table returns as an attention, moves every `open` line to `delivered`, `returned_to_origin`, or `lost` as the table says, publishes `LinesReturnedToOrigin` for lines that come back or are lost, and moves cash `unpaid` to `nothing_to_collect` when `cod_due_amount` is 0 and nothing was collected. For the `carrier` actor, a pair the table does not list applies nothing, raises `carrier_fact_contradicts_state` with the fact's code as its source, and returns `{ applied: false }`, so feature 19 marks its inbound event processed (review M3); only a malformed fact (a state outside the enum, a refusal reason outside `RefusedReasonCode`) throws. On `shipped` it enqueues the shipment posting job in the same transaction; the job books the `order_shipment` through spec 0011's `LedgerService.postCarrierShipment` and writes each line's `unit_cost` from it (AC-24). On `shipped` it also records the stock out request (`stock_out_source = 'carrier_shipped'`, each line's `stock_out_quantity` its full open quantity) when none is recorded, including while `fulfilled_lines_differ` is open: the courier's pickup is the truth, so the job posts those quantities and orders closes the `fulfilled_lines_differ` attention in the same transaction (decided 2026-11-08); when a request is recorded (a Shopify fulfillment came first) the request is not recorded again, but the job is still enqueued for the order's current stock out generation (owner ruling 2026-11-21): a Shopify fulfillment can record the request, the order can be cancelled before the job ran (the job skips a cancelled order), and a late carrier `shipped` then means the pieces left with the courier unposted. The job reads `ledger.postingFor(key)` first so it never posts twice, and the ledger's idempotency key backs that up under a race. On `cancelled` (`carrier.terminated_before_pickup`) it enqueues the after cancel job (AC-27). A late carrier `shipped` on a cancelled order (the table's `cancelled` to `shipped` row) closes that order's awaiting restock quantities with no movement, because the posting stands and the courier has the pieces. `OrderTransitions.recordChannelStockOut(tx, orderId, { at, source: { type: 'shopify_fulfillment', id }, fulfilledLines: [{ channelLineId, quantity }] }, actor)`, `recordChannelFulfillmentCancelled(tx, orderId, { at, source }, actor)`, and `OrderStock.recordChannelReturn(tx, orderId, { lines, source }, actor)` (added 2026-11-08 for spec 0015 AC-28) move no fulfillment state and follow AC-27; `cancel` and `cancelFromChannel` enqueue the after cancel job whenever no carrier `shipped` fact exists (AC-27).

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 1 in [rationale.md](rationale.md): one `orders` module is the single writer of `orders`, `order_lines`, and `order_events`; other modules move an order only by calling its named methods inside one order transaction opened with `OrderTransactions.run`, and the orders module announces each change through an in transaction `DomainEvents` service it builds here.

Every write path takes the order row lock, checks the move with the existing pure rules, writes the row changes and the event row, recomputes money and outcome, holds or releases stock, writes the audit entry when one is owed, and publishes its domain events, all in that transaction. Runner up: an event sourced order (the order row rebuilt from events), rejected because edits, splits, and money are not state events in the built model and the baseline is already shipped.

The truth of a line's shortfall is spec 0011's `reservations.unfilled_quantity`; `order_lines.short_quantity` is the orders module's projection of it (spec 0002 A6: written in the same transaction as the source, from `reserveLines` answers and the `ReservationFilled` and `ReservationShortened` handlers), so the list can flag and filter short lines without reading inventory's tables. The scale suite decides whether more list indexes are needed (cash state already has the partial `orders_open_cash_idx`); an index is added only for a filter that misses its budget. `orders.updated_at` is the optimistic concurrency token (`expectedUpdatedAt`, microsecond text, the spec 0009 pattern). The `table-ownership.ts` map already lists `orders`, `order_lines`, `order_events`, and `shipments` under `orders`.

### State machines

The transition tables, the first cash state, the line split, the allocation, `codDue`, and the outcome are spec 0002's and are built. This feature applies them and changes these rules (one contracts task, milestone 1):

1. **Cancel until pickup** (review X1, Q-16.4). The `cancelled` row (`new`, `confirmed`, `packed`) no longer refuses on `carrierDeliveryCreated`; the money freeze stays in the order writer, at `carrier_delivery_created_at`. It accepts a person (a cancel reason code), `system_job` (a reason code starting `channel.`, mapped by feature 13 from the channel's own reason), and `carrier` (a reason code starting `carrier.`, `carrier.terminated_before_pickup` for a delivery cancelled or terminated before any pickup). The `cancelled` to `shipped` carrier row stays, with its task. The `packed` to `confirmed` row keeps its refusal once the carrier delivery exists. The exhaustive `nextState` test gains every new pair.
2. **A whole order cancellation** (domain review D3). Lines stay `open`, every total stays, and `codDue` returns 0 when the fulfillment state is `cancelled`, so the guard of `unpaid` to `nothing_to_collect` holds in the cancel's own transaction. Removing one piece still cancels that line (`order_lines.cancel_reason_code`). The cancelled value per agent therefore reads `total_amount` of cancelled orders, and an open line on a cancelled order holds no stock (its reservation is released).
3. **Carrier facts the table does not list** (review M3) are not a rule of `nextState` (it still throws `InvalidTransition`); `recordShipmentFact` catches it for the `carrier` actor only, applies nothing, and raises `carrier_fact_contradicts_state` (AC-15).
4. **A payment method corrected before anything moved** (AC-25 c, the Shopify review's M6). New cash rows for `system_job` with reason `payment_method_rederived` only: `unpaid` to `prepaid`, `prepaid` to `unpaid`, `nothing_to_collect` to `unpaid`, and `unpaid` to `nothing_to_collect` (the last already exists, under its own guard). The service guard: no carrier delivery, nothing collected, and the target is what `firstCashState` gives for the corrected amounts.
5. **A go live import** (AC-25 a). The first fulfillment event of an order may be `null` to `confirmed` with reason `go_live_import` for a `system_job` actor; `replayStates` already accepts any first state, so only the intake checks this. An `import` actor's found states (built in milestone 3) are checked by an allow list: a held order only with a first cash state, a cancelled one only `nothing_to_collect` (what a live cancel writes), collected, remitted, or reconciled cash only once a carrier fact touched the order and with its carrier delivery time, and each cash state against the amounts (`nothing_to_collect` with nothing due, collected or remitted with an amount due, `prepaid` with a prepayment and nothing expected); a found collected, remitted, or reconciled cash records the amount due as collected (and remitted); a found `partially_delivered` is refused, because its line split is not known (see Follow-up); the run id is kept on the first events (`source_type` `import_run`).
6. **A channel edit of a packed order** (AC-14, built in milestone 3). The `packed` to `confirmed` row also accepts `system_job` (reason `channel_edit`), so `applyChannelEdit` sends a packed order back to `confirmed` as a person's edit does; it keeps its refusal once the carrier delivery exists.

The guards `nextState` leaves to the service (its TSDoc lists them) are enforced in `order-writer.ts`: `unpaid` to `nothing_to_collect` only with fulfillment `cancelled`, `returned_to_origin`, `refused`, or `lost`, `cod_due_amount = 0`, and `cod_collected_amount = 0`; `remitted` only with `depositedOn`; `confirmed` to `packed` asks `ConnectorsService.openCommandsFor('order', id, tx)` and passes `writeBackOpen` from that answer, and refuses a line still short after the top up; `partially_delivered` only through `recordPartialDelivery`; `delivered` moves every `open` line.

Automatic follow on moves happen in the same transaction and each writes its own event: an edit in `packed` writes `packed` to `confirmed` (actor the editing person); any fulfillment move that leaves `cod_due_amount = 0` with nothing collected writes cash `unpaid` to `nothing_to_collect` (actor `system_job`, source the triggering event).

### After a cancel (AC-27 (b), amended 2026-11-08)

The after cancel job runs in the order's job group with the generation `n` it was enqueued for and the cause; it reads the order and `ledger.postingFor` of generation `n`'s key. The first row that matches decides. Checked against the fulfillment table in `packages/contracts/src/orders/order-states.ts`: a cancel is possible only from `new`, `confirmed`, or `packed`, and the only carrier fact that applies to a `cancelled` order is `cancelled` to `shipped`.

| # | When | Then |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the order's generation is no longer `n`, or nothing is posted under generation `n`'s key | nothing (the post job, which checks the state and the generation as it runs, posts nothing for a cancelled order either) |
| 2 | generation `n`'s posting is already reversed (`AlreadyReversed`, for example by the fulfillment reversal) | nothing |
| 3 | the order is no longer `cancelled` (a carrier `shipped` fact moved it `cancelled` to `shipped`) | nothing: the posting stands, the courier has the pieces |
| 4 | the cause is the carrier's `cancelled` with `carrier.terminated_before_pickup` | reverse (key `order_shipment_reversal:<orderId>:<n>`, reason code `order_cancelled_before_pickup`), move to generation `n + 1`, clear the request |
| 5 | `stock_out_source` is `shopify_fulfillment` (a fulfillment is still active, since a fully cancelled one was already reversed) or `pick_pack_ship` (feature 33), with or without Bosta, and whether or not Bosta ever had a delivery | put each line's posted `stock_out_quantity` on `awaiting_restock_quantity` and raise `returned_pieces_to_receive`; a later carrier `shipped` (row 3's move) closes those quantities with no movement |
| 6 | `stock_out_source` is `carrier_shipped` | cannot happen (no cancel after `shipped`); nothing, logged as a fault by order id |

Both reversal jobs carry `n` in their data, and their keys differ (`order_shipment_reversal` here, `order_shipment_fulfillment_reversal` for AC-27 (c)), so whichever runs second gets `AlreadyReversed` from the ledger (a group is reversed at most once; the reason code is part of the reversal's request hash, so the keys must differ). The fulfillment reversal job changes nothing on the order (`recordChannelFulfillmentCancelled` already moved it to `n + 1` in its own transaction); the after cancel reversal (row 4) moves the order to `n + 1` only while it is still at `n`.

### Who opens the transaction

Spec 0011 holds stock for an order only inside a stock transaction opened by `LedgerService.transaction(work)`, whose single deadlock retry reruns the whole `work`. **Decided: every order write that reserves or releases runs in `LedgerService.transaction`**, and so does every other orders write, through one orders owned entry point, so a caller never has to know which moves touch stock. So:

- **The orders module owns the entry point.** `OrderTransactions.run(work)` in `apps/api/src/orders/order-transactions.ts` calls `LedgerService.transaction` and hands `work` the branded transaction, typed `OrderTransaction`. Every orders write method (the write paths table below) takes an `OrderTransaction` as its first argument, so a caller cannot reach one with a plain Drizzle transaction; the type checker refuses it. Callers depend on `OrderTransactions.run` and `OrderTransaction` only, never on the inventory module. As built in milestone 4, `run` is `LedgerService.transaction` (milestones 1 to 3 ran it on a plain transaction with the same contract, so switching it changed no caller).
- **Who opens it, per path.** The orders routes (create, edit, confirm, cancel, pack, unpack) open it in the controller, one per request. `hold-open-orders` opens one per batch of 200 lines. The shipment posting job does not use it: it calls spec 0011's `postCarrierShipment`, which runs its own ledger transaction, and writes the unit costs in that transaction's `within` callback. The `ReservationFilled` and `ReservationShortened` handlers run inside spec 0011's own transaction (domain event handlers, spec 0002 A6) and write only `order_lines.short_quantity` through the order writer, one line row, without the order row lock (so a ledger posting that holds pair locks never waits on an order row; the line row lock it may meet is broken by the ledger's deadlock retry). Every other path is opened by the calling feature: feature 13's webhook and sweep job (`createFromChannel`, `applyChannelEdit`, `recordChannelLineIds`, `cancelFromChannel`, `recordCarrierDelivery`), feature 18's queue actions and write back executor (`edit`, `confirm`, `cancel`, `recordChannelLineIds`), feature 19's delivery sync (`recordCarrierDelivery`, `recordShipmentFact`, `recordPartialDelivery`, `recordCollection`), feature 21's screens (`recordShipmentFact` by a person, `recordPartialDelivery`, replacement creation), feature 22's matching (`recordCollection`, `recordSettlement`), and feature 34 (`markExchanged`, and creation of replacements).
- **What a caller's `work` must do.** Write everything of that unit inside `work`, its own rows included (an inbox event's status, a shipment row, a settlement match, a queue row, pg-boss jobs sent with the `db` option on the same transaction), so they commit or roll back with the order. Be rerunnable: read its inputs again inside `work`, keep no state outside it between runs, and call no provider and no other connection inside it (Shopify and Bosta are read before `run` or in a later job). Touch one order per unit on high volume paths (a sync job handles one delivery or one webhook per `run`), so lock waits stay short. Take its own rows first, then call orders, which locks the order row, then the inventory locks (spec 0011's order). Let `LockTimeout` and `InvalidTransition` propagate; a job retries the whole unit, a route answers 503 `BUSY` or 409.
- **What it asks of spec 0011** (not edited here; listed for its owner in Follow-up): the branded transaction must be accepted wherever the api's `Transaction` is (`AuditService.record`, `DomainEvents`, `CustomersService.findOrCreate`, `NumberingService.next`, pg-boss `send` with `db`); spec 0011's sentence "the caller writes only its own rows" must allow a unit of work that writes several modules' rows through their own services; and a `LedgerService.transaction` called inside another must be refused with a named error, never silently nested.

Edit operations (`operations[]`, a discriminated union on `kind`): `ship_to` (any of `name`, `phone`, `phoneAlt`, `address`, `courierNote`, `area` as `{ list, governorateCode, cityCode, zoneCode }` or null), `add_line` (`variantId`, `quantity`, `unitPrice`, `discountAmount`), `replace_line` (`lineId` plus the add fields), `remove_line` (`lineId`, `cancelReasonCode`), `shipping` (`shippingChargedAmount`). Money and quantities use `moneyInput` and `quantityInput` (Arabic digits normalized). The variant picker on the form calls the catalog search route of spec 0012 (`GET /api/skus?q=`); the order page reads SKU code and name through the catalog service, never the catalog tables.

The search `q` travels in the query string of a GET like every list filter. Because a phone typed there would then sit in the access log of the proxy, the web sends a phone search as `POST /api/orders/search` with the same `OrderListQuery` in the body (permission `orders.view`), the pattern spec 0014 AC-3 uses for customer search; `GET /api/orders` refuses a `q` that parses as a phone with 400 `PHONE_IN_URL`.

The list cursor is (`placed_at`, `id`) base64url (`apps/api/src/platform/keyset-cursor.ts`), compared and ordered with `COLLATE "C"` on any text part. Search `q`, tried in this order: text that `EgyptianPhone` parses matches `ship_to_phone = p OR ship_to_phone_alt = p` (two partial indexes) `OR customer_id = ANY(idsOf(CustomersService.findByPhone(p)))` (spec 0014; skipped when it answers null), so an order placed under another number of the same customer is found too; otherwise digits or `#` digits match `order_number IN (q, '#' || q)`; anything else matches the order number exactly. The `customerId` filter is `customer_id = ANY(CustomersService.idsOf(canonicalOf(customerId)))` (spec 0014), served by `orders_customer_idx`. A phone that does not parse is never echoed in an error.

### Key invariants

- Only `writer/order-writer.ts` writes `orders`, `order_lines`, and `order_events` (a unit test greps the module, like the ledger's single writer rule; the module wall already keeps other modules out).
- Every orders write runs inside `OrderTransactions.run`; no orders write method accepts a plain transaction (the type says so). One exception, as built in milestone 2: `OrderWriter.projectWriteBackState` takes the publisher's plain transaction, because it runs in the write back projection's handler inside the connectors' unit of work (an executor opens its own transaction); it locks the order row before it reads the commands and writes only `write_back_state`.
- Every state change has exactly one event row, written in the same statement batch as the column change; `replayStates` over the events equals the row.
- `outcome` equals `deriveOrderOutcome` of the row after every write (`OrderWriter.applyChange` derives and writes it; no caller passes one); `cod_due_amount` equals `codDue` of the row and its lines after every write.
- After `carrier_delivery_created_at` is set, `cod_expected_amount`, `prepaid_amount`, `credit_applied_amount`, and the shares never change (`OrderWriter.applyChange` refuses a change of them, or of the totals they are tied to, with `OrderLocked`), unless `recordChannelFulfillmentCancelled` cleared it because every Shopify fulfillment was cancelled before any carrier delivery existed (AC-27 (c); an exception the owner accepted on 2026-11-08); a channel snapshot never changes `prepaid_amount` or `credit_applied_amount` at any time.
- A line is never updated except its `stock_out_quantity`, `awaiting_restock_quantity`, and `awaiting_restock_since` (AC-27), its `line_state`, `cancel_reason_code`, `channel_line_id` (once, from null), `variant_id` (once, from null, only through `linkChannelLineVariant`), `unit_cost` (once, from the shipment), and the split's quantity and money; a mapped variant, the price, and the discount never change in place.
- No carrier fact is refused or rolled back because of stock.
- No domain event payload and no log line carries a ship to field, the customer note, or a raw phone.
- One order row lock per write path, taken first among the orders module's locks; two orders are locked only in ascending id.

### Security model

- Audience `staff` only; no portal, partner, or hook route here (the portal reads orders later through a service of its own feature).
- Permissions, all existing in spec 0008 (none new is needed): `orders.view` reads (the list, the page, `filter-options`; the create form `options` are `orders.confirm`); `orders.confirm` creates sale, reopened, and replacement orders, edits, confirms, and cancels; `orders.gift` creates gifted orders and is required for line and shipping edits of one; `fulfillment.work` packs and unpacks; `catalog.manage` (manager only, the permission that manages locations) holds open orders at a location before it goes online; `audit.read` sees the edit history panel; `cost.view` sees `unitCost`. Every one of these except `orders.gift` and `catalog.manage` is marked personal (P) in spec 0008, so no external hat can reach an order.
- Law 151: ship to fields, the customer note, and raw phones are personal data. They are already in `PERSONAL_DATA_KEYS`, so the `RedactingLogger` and the audit trail redact them; the orders module logs by order id only, and domain events carry no personal data. `writeBackViewOf` returns ship to fields to the connectors module only, which puts them in the command payload (already personal, spec 0002) and never in a log line.
- Errors at the edge carry a stable code and never echo an address, phone, or note (`InvalidPhone` never carries the input).
- The write paths for other features are in process calls, not routes, so a carrier or a job never gets an http surface here.

## Consequences

**Positive**:

- One file writes orders, so the state machines, the money, and the outcome cannot drift apart, and features 13, 18, 19, 21, 22, and 34 each build against a documented call and one transaction entry point instead of a table.
- Every order change and its consequences (events, attention, outbound command, audit entry, reservation) commit or roll back together, and a deadlock retry reruns them together.
- A fake or unanswered order can always be closed before pickup, so its stock and its expected cash leave the figures the same day (review X1).
- The hold of carried B2 is enforced twice (the packed guard asks the connectors service) and shown where the warehouse looks (carried n1); a short line cannot be packed.
- A carrier fact never waits on stock, so the Bosta sync never stalls on a warehouse mistake.
- A Warehouse only install still has a plain Confirm and Pack, so stock moves on orders without the Operations bundle.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:

- Every caller's unit of work must be rerunnable and must run inside `OrderTransactions.run`; a feature that calls a provider inside it breaks the rule, so features 13, 18, 19, 21, and 22 read providers before `run` or in a later job.
- Every caller shares the order row lock; a burst of carrier facts for one order queues behind an agent's edit. Acceptable at one order per lock and a few events per order per day.
- With the Shopify write back steps off, staff must edit and cancel Shopify orders in Shopify (Q-16.3, M1), which is slower for the confirmation agent until feature 18 turns the steps on.
- Reserving at creation (Q-16.2) holds stock for orders that will be cancelled as fake or unanswered, so available stock reads lower until they are cancelled.
- `short_quantity` is a copy of spec 0011's `unfilled_quantity`; if a handler is ever skipped the copy drifts until the next top up of that line (confirm, pack, or edit rewrites it from the answer).
- A cancel after the delivery exists needs a person in the Bosta dashboard to terminate the delivery; the app only reminds them.
- An edit that changes whether anything is left to collect, or that overpays, is refused; a prepaid order that changes size needs the payment path of feature 18 first.
- `OrderAttentionRaised` opens no task until feature 17 lands; until then the attention is visible only in tests and logs by order id.
- A channel edit while a write back is open waits for feature 13 to read the order again; if feature 13 never does, the channel change is lost until the next sweep.
- Lines on a cancelled order stay `open`, so any reader of line states must also read the order's fulfillment state (the metric dictionary, feature 24).

**Neutral**:

- Three columns, seven indexes (the partial unique key, three list and search indexes, two phone indexes, and the short line index), and one enum value, in two forward migrations; then, in task 15, six stock out columns, one enum, and one partial index (AC-27, amended 2026-11-08); no new table.
- Rule changes in `packages/contracts` (`system_job` and `carrier` cancels until pickup, `codDue` on a cancelled order), with spec 0002 amended beside the lines they change.
- The platform gains `DomainEvents`, which every later projection reuses.

## Questions for the owner

Each is decided with the recommended answer and built that way unless the owner says otherwise.

**Ratified by the owner on 2026-11-08** ("Accept all four", relayed by the lead): the lead asked four questions, the search throttle of spec 0014, the zero net sale line, the reopen rule, and the error codes with the counting rule as one; the four rulings of this spec, each a ruling now:
- (a) A sale line whose net is 0 needs `orders.gift`; without it the create answers 422 `LINE_FULLY_DISCOUNTED` (*API surface*).
- (b) A reopened order must be for the customer of the original (422 `RELATED_ORDER_OTHER_CUSTOMER`), and an original has one reopen, whatever that reopen's outcome (409 `ALREADY_REOPENED`, AC-23; stricter than the one open reopen milestone 1 built, so task 8a tightens it).
- (c) The error codes `CHANNEL_NOT_ALLOWED` and `INVALID_PHONE_ALT` (*API surface*).
- (d) The counting rule of AC-21: a refusal counts by the operating day of its `refused` event, a returned line by the operating day its order was placed, and a gifted order counts in neither.

1. **Q-16.1 Where are WhatsApp, Instagram, and phone orders entered?** _Decided: in the app, into an internal store (`MAIN`), numbered `SO-2026-000123` (recommended)._ Accepted by the owner 2026-11-07; **open check**: the pilot brand has not yet said where it enters these orders today (pilot question 2 below), and the answer may change this. Other options: only in Shopify as draft orders (then this feature's form is for gifts and replacements only), or both. Why: it gives every channel one list and a Warehouse only brand a way in; the cost is that in mirror mode the Bosta delivery for such an order is created by hand in Bosta's dashboard with the order number as its business reference. The domain review keeps the form either way and asks the pilot brand (question 2 below); a shorter prefix is one `document_prefixes` change.
2. **Q-16.2 When does an order hold stock?** _Decided: at creation (recommended)._ Other options: at confirmation; or never before shipping. Why: Shopify already counts the piece as sold at creation, so the app's available stock matches what the brand sees there; fake and unanswered orders release on cancel.
3. **Q-16.3 May staff edit or cancel a Shopify order in the app while the Shopify write back step is off?** _Decided: no, the order page says to do it in Shopify and the change syncs back (recommended)._ Other options: allow it and warn that Shopify and the Bosta app still have the old values. Why: in mirror mode the Bosta app ships what Shopify says, so a change that never reaches Shopify is the 11:40 wrong size parcel of review B2, silently.
4. **Q-16.4 May an order be cancelled after the carrier delivery exists but before pickup?** _Decided: yes; the money stays frozen, the app raises "terminate the delivery in Bosta", and a delivery the carrier reports terminated before pickup moves the order to `cancelled` (recommended)._ Other options: keep the lock and have the agent cancel only in Bosta, with feature 19 mapping "terminated before pickup" to `cancelled`; or keep the lock with no carrier move. Why: the reference brand's own sync shows deliveries created for orders that never ship; with the lock, fake and unanswered orders stay `new`, holding stock and cash expected, for good.
5. **Q-16.5 May an order with a short line be packed?** _Decided: no, 409 `LINE_SHORT` until the line fills or is removed (recommended)._ Other options: allow it with a warning and an attention; or allow it silently. Why: a parcel without a piece goes out with the courier told to collect for it, and the customer refuses at the door.
6. **Q-16.6 How is an order linked to an earlier one created in slice 1a?** _Decided: both create paths accept `relatedOrder` with `reopened_from` or `replacement_for` (and `goodwill_for` for a goodwill gift) (recommended)._ Other options: only `reopened_from` now, with replacements waiting for feature 34 and size refusals counted as failures during the pilot; or no link until feature 34. Why: without it a reopened order loses its campaign and a size refusal counts as a returned to origin failure, the data model review's M4 problem.
